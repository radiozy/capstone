
A Bear Stearns unit has been chosen to underwrite a $500 million initial public offering for a Chinese company.
The Guangzhou Railway Corporation, a state-owned company, today named Bear Stearns Asia Ltd. to be sole lead underwriter for its public offering. 
Guangzhou owns and operates passenger and cargo railways between Hong Kong and cities in southern China.
Owen Williams, senior managing director of Bear Stearns Asia, said he hoped the company would be ready to list its shares in Hong Kong and on the New York Stock Exchange toward the end of this year.
Guangzhou Railway is believed to be the last state-owned company to choose an investment bank for a an international stock offering.
Analysts at rival brokerages in Hong Kong have described Guangzhou Railway as a prize catch because of the potentially large size of a public offer and the company's profitability. 
The company taps southern China's economic boom and earns a significant portion of its revenue in Hong Kong dollars.
