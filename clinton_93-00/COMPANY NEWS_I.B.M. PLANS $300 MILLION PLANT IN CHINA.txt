
I.B.M. said yesterday that it planned to build a $300 million computer chip packaging plant in Shanghai in expectation of heavy demand in China. 
It said the new plant, part of a $5 billion global plan to expand its semiconductor business, would make components on which chips are mounted for use in high-technology devices like digital phones and camcorders. 
The plant is scheduled to begin production in early 2002 with a work force of 1,800. 
I.B.M. closed up $1.06, to $93.75.
