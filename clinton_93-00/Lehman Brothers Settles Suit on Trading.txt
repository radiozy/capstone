
Lehman Brothers Inc. said today that it had settled a lawsuit over trading debts with the China National United Petroleum and Chemicals Company. 
Lehman Brothers said terms of the settlement would not be disclosed.
Lehman sued China National United Petroleum, known as Unipec, in 1994 for $44 million over debts related to foreign exchange trading. 
Unipec countersued, seeking $58 million.
Lehman has had legal disputes with several other Chinese companies.
In April, the company settled with the China National Chemical Import and Export Corporation, or Sinochem. 
Sinochem said it lost $20 million dealing securities through Lehman Brothers. 
Lehman Brothers maintained that it, in fact, was owed money.
