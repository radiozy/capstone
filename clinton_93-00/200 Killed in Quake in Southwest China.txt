
A severe earthquake rocked southwestern China today, killing about 200 people in the remote mountainous region and injuring hundreds, local officials said.
"About 200 people have been killed," a county official in Lijiang, a scenic town in the mountains Yunnan Province, said by telephone.
"We now have several hundred injured," he said. 
Chinese officials here put the magnitude of the tremor at 7.0 on the Richter scale.
Many homes and buildings had collapsed and rescue teams were searching for survivors, the county official said.
The earthquake was centered near Lijiang, about 180 miles northwest of the Yunnan provincial capital of Kunming.
