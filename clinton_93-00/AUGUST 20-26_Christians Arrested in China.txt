
Last Tuesday in Los Angeles, a leader of China's government-approved churches said the country had entered a "golden age" of religion. 
On Wednesday in central China, the limits of that freedom were clear as some 130 evangelical Protestants, including three visiting Taiwanese-American missionaries, were arrested at a revival. 
The worshipers were from one of several large, fervent Christian groups that refuse to cooperate with official bodies and face police harassment and the jailing of their leaders. 
By week's end, the Americans had been released but the fate of the detained Chinese was unknown.   Erik Eckholm
