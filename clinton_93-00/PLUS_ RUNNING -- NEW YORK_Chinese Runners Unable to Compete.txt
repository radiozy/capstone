
The closing of the United States Embassy in Beijing since May 8 will force five Chinese runners to miss three races in the United States because they cannot get visas from the embassy, Anne Roberts, the elite athlete coordinator for next Saturday's New York 10k Mini-Marathon, said yesterday.
Officials for the May 23 Rock 'n' Roll Marathon in San Diego and the May 31 Bolder Boulder 10K race at Boulder, Colo., said Chinese runners expected for their events would not be able to compete either.
The New York and Boulder races will each lose the same three runners and the San Diego race will lose two others.
Missing from New York and Boulder will be Zhao Shengzhi, Wang Yanrong and Lu Jing. 
All three have run the 10K (6.2 miles) under 33 minutes, led by Wang's 32 minutes 31 seconds.
This would have been their first time in New York.
