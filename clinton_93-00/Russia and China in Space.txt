
Russia is ready to help China build a national space station, Deputy Prime Minister Ilya Klebanov told the Itar-Tass news agency today before returning from a trip to Beijing.
"They have their own ideas, but asked us to help them with our expertise," he said.
Mr. Klebanov, whose three-day visit to China ended today, held meetings with President Jiang Zemin of China. 
He said that the Chinese leader supports Russia's actions in Chechnya, where soldiers are battling a separatist movement. 
In return, Mr. Klebanov said, Russia supports China's stance on Taiwan, Chinese television reported.
