
TCBY Enterprises Inc. said yesterday that an investment group based in Hong Kong had signed a franchise agreement to open 23 frozen yogurt stores in China. 
The group, Top Green International, expects to open the first Chinese TCBY in Shanghai next fall. 
TCBY said its Americana Foods subsidiary would work with Top Green and a Chinese dairy company to upgrade its factory equipment for frozen yogurt production. 
TCBY, which is based in Little Rock, Ark., currently sells frozen yogurt in 21 foreign countries.
