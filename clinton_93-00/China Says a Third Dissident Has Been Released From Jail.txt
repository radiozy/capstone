
China has released a third dissident, Wu Xuecan, a former editor of the People's Daily jailed for taking part in student protests in 1989.
A Justice Ministry spokeswoman said today that Mr. Wu was released on Thursday after completing a reduced sentence. 
She gave no more details.
This week China also released its most famous political prisoner, Wei Jingsheng, who had been in prison since 1979, and Zhai Weimin, a student leader in the 1989 protests.
