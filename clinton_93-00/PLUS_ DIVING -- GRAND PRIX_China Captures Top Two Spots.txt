
Chinese divers finished first and second in the women's one-meter springboard on the opening day of a FINA diving grand prix in Beijing. 
Zhu Zhijia finished with 269.30 points, and Yan Jie had 263.70. 
Chantelle Michell of Australia, third in the women's three-meter springboard at the world championships in Perth, Australia, in January, was third with 259.85.
