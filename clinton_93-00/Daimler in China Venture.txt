
Daimler-Benz A.G. said today that it had started a joint venture with a Chinese partner to manufacture buses. 
The new company, to be based in Guangzhou province, will be called Yaxing-Benz and will be the largest Asian bus manufacturer. 
Daimler-Benz and Guangzhou Motor Coach Manufacturer each hold 50 percent in the joint venture. 
Yaxing-Benz will build 8,500 buses a year.
