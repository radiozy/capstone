
The Boeing Company said yesterday that it had received a $600 million order from China Yunnan Airlines for three 767-300 wide-body jetliners and options on three more. 
The order is the first for Boeing in more than a year from a Government-controlled airline in China, a Boeing spokesman said. 
Boeing, based in Seattle, was among the American companies that lobbied for President Clinton to renew trade ties with China last May. 
Boeing has said it expects China to be the fastest-growing market in the world for airline travel.
