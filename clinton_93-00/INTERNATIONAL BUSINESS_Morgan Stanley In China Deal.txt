
The China Three Gorges Project Development Corporation, the company in charge of the world's biggest dam project, has hired Morgan Stanley Dean Witter & Company to draft preliminary documents for a sale of shares on the New York and Hong Kong stock exchanges, The Financial Times reported today.
The newspaper cited Wang Jiazhu, the deputy general manager of the project company, as the source of its information. 
Morgan Stanley officials did not immediately return telephone calls.
The transaction, which would seek to raise about 20 percent of the 40 billion yuan, or $4.8 billion, that China is seeking from foreigners, could be China's biggest ever share sale. 
The initial public offering is awaiting approval from China's state council, the newspaper said.
The Three Gorges Dam project is one of the biggest public works projects and is expected to cost more than $30 billion when completed in 2009.
