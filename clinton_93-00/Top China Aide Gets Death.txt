
China has sentenced a former top parliamentary official to death for taking almost $5 million in bribes, the official New China News Agency said today.
Cheng Kejie, former vice chairman of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress -- China's Parliament -- would be the most senior Chinese official to be executed for corruption.
He was dismissed from his post in April. 
The government said he took the bribes while he led the government of the southwestern region of Guangxi, a hotbed of crime.
