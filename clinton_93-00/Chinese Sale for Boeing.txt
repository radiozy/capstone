
China Southern Airlines plans to buy 21 Boeing passenger planes in the next two years, the Xinhua news agency reported today. 
The news agency said this year's purchases would include two 767-300's, four 757's and five 737's. 
Ten aircraft will be bought in 1994. 
China Southern, which is owned by the Government, has also announced plans to buy six Boeing 777's when the new model is ready in 1995. 
The airline, based in Guangzhou, already has 46 Boeing aircraft and 97 other planes of other makes. 
It flies 150 domestic and international routes.
