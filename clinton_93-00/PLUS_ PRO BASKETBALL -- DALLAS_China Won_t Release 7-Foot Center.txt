
China will not release the 7-foot center Wang Zhizhi of the Bayi Rockets to play for the Dallas Mavericks, an official of the Chinese army team was quoted yesterday as saying in Beijing.
"It's a nice thing that Wang was selected by Dallas at the N.B.A. draft on June 30," the state-run Xinhua News Agency quoted Qian Limin, Bayi's manager, as saying. 
"But we can't let him go because it's not the right time either for our team or for Wang himself."
