
Zhang Fang's voice trembled today after she saw photos of the wreckage of the Chinese Embassy in Yugoslavia.
"The Chinese people should never forget," she said angrily.
The photos chronicled NATO's May 7 attack on Belgrade, which destroyed the embassy. 
The exhibition opened on Friday at the National Art Museum, adding to an anti-NATO, anti-American propaganda campaign in China's state-controlled news media suggesting that the attack was part of an American plan to destabilize China.
Images at the exhibition portray the Chinese and Yugoslav people as common victims of NATO aggression. 
There are photos of the bloodied bodies of the three Chinese people killed in the bombing.
Ms. Zhang and others said the photos brought home the war's horrors.
