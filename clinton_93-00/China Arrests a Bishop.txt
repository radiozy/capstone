
A bishop in the underground Catholic Church in China has been arrested, a United States-based advocacy group said in a statement received here today.
Bishop Zhang Weizhu of Xianxian, in Hebei Province near Beijing, was detained on May 31 while traveling to his village, the Cardinal Kung Foundation said.
Official comment was not immediately available because government offices were closed for the weekend. 
The underground church remains loyal to the Vatican and rejects the state-approved Catholic church set up by the Government in the 1950's.
