
Apple growers have asked the Government to impose tariffs of up to 91 percent on imports of apple juice concentrate from China. 
The petition by five juice concentrate makers contends that China has flooded the American market with concentrates priced either below the production cost or at Chinese market prices, a practice known as dumping. 
The petition was filed Monday with the Commerce Department and the International Trade Commission. 
It said imports of Chinese concentrate had jumped 1,200 percent since 1995, while prices had dropped by 50 percent.
