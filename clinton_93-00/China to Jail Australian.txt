
Chinese court officials said today that James Peng, the Australian businessman jailed for embezzlement while planning to sue his former Chinese partners, must serve 16 years in prison before he can be deported.
"Normally, Peng has to serve the principal penalty, which is 16 years' imprisonment, before implementation of the expulsion," an official of the Shenzhen People's Intermediate Court said.
"It is possible that he gets parole or serves a reduced term because of satisfactory performance, sickness or other factors," the court spokesman added.
Mr. Peng, the former chairman of the Shenzhen Yuan Ye Company, a trading and property company, was accused of transferring into his personal account $1.4 million borrowed to finance company investments in Australia.
