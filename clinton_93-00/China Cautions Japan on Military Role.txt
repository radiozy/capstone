
China demanded today that strengthened military ties between the United States and Japan not be used to interfere in any conflict between Beijing and rival Taiwan.
China will be closely watching Japan's statements and actions to evaluate whether it is keeping promises not to become a military power, Foreign Ministry spokesman Zhu Bangzao said at a news conference.
Guidelines adopted by Japan's Parliament on Monday bolster the United States-Japanese military alliance by committing Japanese forces to provide greater assistance to American troops in Asia. 
The guidelines also give the United States greater access to airports and seaports in Japan.
Public comments by Japanese officials and American commitments to Taiwan have made China suspicious that the alliance might come to the island's defense in any conflict.
China and Taiwan split amid civil war in 1949, and Beijing still regards Taiwan as a rebel province to be reclaimed by force if necessary.
