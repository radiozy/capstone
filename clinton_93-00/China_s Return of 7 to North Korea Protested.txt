
United Nations officials have accused China of violating international refugee laws by deporting a family of seven North Koreans, who could face severe reprisals in their own country.
"We are greatly concerned by the Chinese decision to deport people whom the U.N. High Commissioner for Refugees has recognized as refugees under the 1951 Refugee Convention," said the United Nations refugee office, which is based in Geneva.
The office said it had sent a protest letter to the Beijing government on Wednesday. 
A copy was faxed to The Associated Press.
South Korean officials say the North Korean family, the youngest of whom is 13, entered China in November to seek food. 
They were later taken into custody by Russian border guards while crossing from China into Russia. 
Russia sent them back to China on Dec. 
30.
Rebuffing South Korean requests, Beijing refused to regard them as refugees and repatriated them to North Korea this week.
