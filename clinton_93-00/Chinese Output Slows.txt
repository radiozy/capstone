
China's estimated industrial output slowed in April, an official newspaper said today. 
April's industrial output was an estimated $39.3 billion (341.3 billion yuan), 17 percent higher than in the corresponding month last year, but lower than the 18.6 percent growth rate in the first quarter, The China Business Times said. 
Quoting a report from the State Information Center, the daily predicted that industrial output would total $40.4 billion (350.6 billion yuan) in May, a 16 percent increase over May 1993.
