
China is expected to have attracted a record $40 billion in foreign investment this year, the Xinhua news agency said today.
This would represent a 16 percent rise over 1995, but the number of projects approved was expected to be 20 percent fewer, Xinhua said, quoting official sources.
The Government has approved 281,300 overseas-financed projects since introducing economic reforms in 1979, it said, adding that about $172 billion had actually been invested in the country.
The Government approved the establishment of 22,000 overseas-financed businesses in the first 11 months of 1996, with pledged investment of $66.5 billion, Xinhua said. 
Of the figure, $36.9 billion has been actually poured into the country, the agency said.
