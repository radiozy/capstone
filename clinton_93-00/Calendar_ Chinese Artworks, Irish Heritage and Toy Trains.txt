
Chinese Arts
Metropolitan Museum of Art, Fifth Avenue at 82d Street; (212) 879-5500.
An exhibition of paintings, jade objects, ceramic works, lacquerware and decorative items from the National Palace Museum in Taipei, Taiwan, starts Tuesday and continues through May 19. 
The 450 works in the show represent historical collections of Chinese rulers, especially those of the Qing Dynasty, and date from the third millennium through the 1700's. 
Open Tuesday through Sunday, 9:30 A.M. to 5:15 P.M.; Friday and Saturday until 8:45 P.M. Suggested admission is $7; students and those 65 and older, $3.50.
 
Irish Immigrant Experiences
Museum of the City of New York, Fifth Avenue at 103d Street; (212) 534-1672, extension 206.
"Gaelic Gotham: A History of the Irish in New York," an exhibition of more than 400 prints, documents, clothing, household items and photographs, opened yesterday and runs through Oct. 
27. The exhibition examines the cultural, social, political and religious experiences of Irish immigrants from 1682 to the present. 
Open Wednesday through Saturday, 10 A.M. to 5 P.M.; Sunday, 1 to 5 P.M. Suggested contribution is $5; children, students and those 62 and older, $3; families, $8.
 
Railroads, Small Scale
Hudson River Museum of Westchester, 511 Warburton Avenue, near Phillipse Road, Yonkers; (914) 963-4550.
An exhibition of model trains running in miniature landscapes and towns starts tomorrow and continues through Sept. 
1. Two related shows are open at the museum through Sept. 
1: a display featuring artifacts, photographs and paintings about the history of the railroad in Westchester County, and an exhibition of advertising posters highlighting the golden age of rail travel in the 1920's and 30's. 
Open Wednesday through Saturday, 10 A.M. to 5 P.M.;Friday to 9 P.M.; Sunday, noon to 5 P.M. Admission is $3; those 12 and younger or 62 and older, $1.50.
