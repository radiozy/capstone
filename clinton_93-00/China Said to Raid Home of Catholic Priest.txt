
The police have ransacked the home of a priest of China's underground Roman Catholic Church in Shanghai, seizing religious articles, cash and electronic equipment, an American-based group said today.
The raid occurred on Saturday night and lasted into the early hours of Easter at the home of the Rev. Zen Caijun, the group, the Cardinal Kung Foundation, said.
Newt Gingrich, the Speaker of the House, visited Shanghai just after the raid reportedly took place. 
He arrived on Sunday and attended an Easter service at the Shanghai Community Church, part of China's officially sanctioned nondenominational Christian church.
In a similar raid on March 4, the police took Bibles, rosaries and other religious articles and $2,400 in cash from the leader of Shanghai's underground Catholic church, Bishop Joseph Fan Zhongliang, the foundation said. 
Bishop Fan, 79, spent 20 years in prison for refusing to join the official church.
After seizing power in 1949, the Communists made Christians join Government-sponsored churches, but many refused and continue to worship illegally underground.
