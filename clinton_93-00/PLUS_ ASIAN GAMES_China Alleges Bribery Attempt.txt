
A bribery scandal rocked the Asian Games yesterday, with China claiming that an agent had approached a table tennis player offering money to throw a final.
The Olympic Council of Asia will warn all 41 nations about the danger of repeat approaches at a meeting today after Chinese Olympic officials said it was not an isolated incident.
An agent who spoke Chinese approached a Chinese table tennis player, trying to buy a match with a certain amount of U.S. dollars, said Wei Jizhong, chairman of the Olympic Council of Asia sports committee.
China won six of the seven table tennis titles at the Games. 
It lost the men's title when the Olympic champion Liu Guoliang was upset by South Korea's Kim Tae Soo.
