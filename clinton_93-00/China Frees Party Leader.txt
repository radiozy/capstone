
An advocate of democracy, detained with eight others for forming a political party, has been freed and is urging the release of his colleagues, a human rights group said today.
The police escorted the advocate, Cheng Fan, from the eastern city of Hangzhou to his home in Wenzhou, 150 miles south, and released him on Monday, the Information Center of Human Rights and Democratic Movement, in Hong Kong, said.
Four of the nine organizers of the group, the China Democracy Party, have been held since Friday.
