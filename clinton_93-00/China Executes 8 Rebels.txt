
China executed eight Muslim separatists in the restive northwestern Muslim region of Xinjiang today for carrying out bombings, murders and robberies that left 18 people dead, the New China News Agency said.
Authorities have intensified a crackdown on separatists and on underground religious activity in the region, which borders Afghanistan, Pakistan and three other nations.
The executions brought to 12 the number of Muslims put to death since April for unrest.
The eight were executed in the regional capital of Urumqi after the Xinjiang Higher People's Court upheld death sentences issued by a lower court. 
They were found guilty of planting homemade bombs on public buses that blew up within minutes of each other in Urumqi on Feb. 
25, killing 9 people and injuring 58.
