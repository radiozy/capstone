
Eleven Chinese competitors from the Asian Games in Hiroshima, Japan, in October were stripped of their medals yesterday after testing positive for drugs, the Olympic Council of Asia said.
A council statement said urine samples of the 11 competitors all showed traces of Dehydrotestosterone, or DHT, a drug banned by the International Olympic Committee.
Two of those named, Lu Bin of the women's team and Xiong Guoming of the men's team, each won four gold medals in swimming at the games.
