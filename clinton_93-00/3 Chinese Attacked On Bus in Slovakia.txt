
A top diplomat at the Chinese Embassy in Slovakia and two other Chinese were attacked by a group of youths, the embassy said today.
A group of eight young men assaulted the Chinese on a trolley-bus late Saturday, the daily newspaper Sme reported. 
The youths got off at the next stop and ran away.
The police said no suspects had been identified. 
Witnesses said the attackers were skinheads -- youths known for violence and racism.
One of those attacked was the First Secretary, Sun Shizhong. 
He suffered a concussion, a broken rib and small cuts, and remained hospitalized today.
"We were shaken and horrified that a Chinese diplomat was attacked and his life was in danger," the embassy said in a statement.
The diplomat's Chinese companions, who were not identified, were taken to the hospital but were released, the embassy said.
A senior Foreign Ministry official met with the Chinese Ambassador, Agence France-Presse reported, to "energetically condemn" the attack and said he would take measures to insure the safety of diplomats.
