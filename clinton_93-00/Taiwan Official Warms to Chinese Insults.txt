
Joking about Chinese state media reports describing her as the "scum" of the country, Taiwan's next vice president said today that she was flattered that China felt it had to go out of its way to insult her.
Annette Lu, an outspoken feminist, recently became the target of China's state-run press, which accused her of trying to whip up anti-China sentiment in Taiwan and called her insane and evil.
The People's Daily, the ruling Communist Party's newspaper, said Saturday that Lu "has become the scum of the Chinese nation."
Speaking at a youth seminar today, Ms. Lu said: "I'm not angry. 
Such a big country has to use all its resources to insult Annette Lu. 
Isn't that an honor?"
Ms. Lu urged President Clinton to help Taiwan's president-elect, Chen Shui-bian, set up talks with President Jiang Zemin of China.
