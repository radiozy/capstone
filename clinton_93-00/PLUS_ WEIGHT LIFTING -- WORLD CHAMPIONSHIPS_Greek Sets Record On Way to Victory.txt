
Leonidas Sabanis of Greece, the European champion and the Olympic silver medalist, lifted 704 pounds yesterday to win the 62-kilogram class in the world championships in Lahti, Finland.
Sabanis lifted a world-record 324 pounds in the snatch on the way to a 6-pound victory margin over Bulgaria's Nikolai Peshalov, the Olympic bronze medalist in 1996. 
Bulgaria's Sevdalin Mintchev was third.
Taipei's top women lifters won two gold medals from Chinese competitors under the body-weight rule. 
Kuo Ping-Chun of Taipei and China's Song Zhihuan both lifted 456 pounds in the 58-kilogram class, but Kuo won the gold because she weighed about a pound less than her Chinese opponent.
In the 63-kilogram class, Chen Jui-Lien of Taipei, also a pound less than China's Shi Lihua, won gold when both lifted 495 pounds.
