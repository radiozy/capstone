
Goldman, Sachs & Company opened its first China office today in Beijing. 
In 1993, the American investment bank was the co-lead manager in 11 bond issues for Chinese companies in Europe, the United States and Asia that raised $2 billion, the official New China News Agency reported. 
Goldman, Sachs joins 62 other foreign investment banks with offices in Beijing; more than 100 foreign banks have offices or branches in all of China, the news agency said. 
Goldman said it also planned to open an office in Shanghai.
