
The Chinese Government will order 21 more Boeing jets worth approximately $800 million, Boeing said today.
The order by the Civil Aviation Administration of China was expected, and Boeing released the details today in its company newspaper.
Boeing said China was ordering 20 of its 737-300 jetliners and one 757-200.
Boeing has delivered 125 jets to China; with today's announcement it has unfilled orders for 69 more. 
Another 21 Boeing planes are to go to China from various leasing companies.
Robert Dryden, executive vice president of the Boeing Commercial Airplane Group, was quoted in the newspaper as saying the company would deliver 47 planes, or 14 percent of its new planes this year, to China.
China has 35 airlines, of which 18 are expected to be jet operators by the end of the year. 
Among the new Boeing operators will be China Xinhua Airlines, Zhongyuan Airlines and Xinjiang Airlines, the Boeing newspaper said.
Boeing considers China to be among its largest potential markets. 
Chinese officials have estimated the country will need 800 more airplanes in 15 years.
