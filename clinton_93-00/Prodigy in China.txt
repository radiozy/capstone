
Prodigy Inc. has become the first Government-approved foreign Internet provider in China, which closely controls access to the global computer network. 
The Prodigy service, to be introduced in Shanghai, stems from a venture with the state-run China North Industries Company. 
Prodigy, which is based in Medford and owns an 80 percent stake in the venture, plans to invest $50 million in it and expand to nine other Chinese cities.
