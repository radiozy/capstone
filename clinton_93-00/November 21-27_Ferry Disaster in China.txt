
The ferry boat, with more than 300 passengers and crew on board, set out from China's northeastern port of Yantai for the journey north to Dalian. 
In a blinding snowstorm with high winds and waves, the ferry somehow caught fire and the extreme conditions prevented rescue ships from approaching.
When the burning vessel began to sink, lifeboats were swamped and most of the passengers who donned life jackets and jumped into the icy waters perished.
Only 22 people were confirmed as surviving one of China's worst ship disasters in decades, and the government immediately opened an inquiry into the cause.   ERIK ECKHOLM
