
Caterpillar Inc. announced a joint venture today with the Xuzhou Construction Machinery Group of China to build hydraulic excavators for the Chinese market. 
Caterpillar is the majority partner in the enterprise, called Caterpillar Xuzhou Ltd. 
Both partners will contribute capital, but other terms of the agreement were not disclosed. 
The company will produce four models of excavators from Caterpillar's 300 series.
