
The insurer American International Group said today that it would consider an investment in China Airlines, Taiwan's largest carrier.
Maurice R. Greenberg, A.I.G.'s chairman, will meet on Saturday in Taipei with bankers who want the company to join a bid for a 36 percent stake in the airline to be sold by the Taiwan Government, said Stephen Tsuei, executive director of the AIG Investment Corporation in Hong Kong.
The bid would be led by the China Development Bank, the investment arm of Taiwan's ruling Nationalist Party.
China Airline's controlling shareholder, the Government-owned China Aviation Development Foundation, plans to sell the shares "around February," said a foundation official, who insisted he not be named. 
He declined to comment on a possible investment by A.I.G.
China Airlines holds landing rights for 27 major destinations, including Japan, Hong Kong, New York, Los Angeles and San Francisco.
