
The Compaq Computer Corporation plans to open new offices in five Chinese cities to handle increasing sales, the overseas edition of People's Daily reported today. 
Compaq opened its first Chinese office last year in Beijing. 
New offices will open later this year in Shanghai, Shenzhen, Shenyang, Chengdu and Xi'an, the report said. 
Compaq's sales in China have been increasing 30 percent a year for the last few years and are expected to top $1 billion in 1995.
