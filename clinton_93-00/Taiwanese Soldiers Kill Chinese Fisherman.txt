
Taiwanese soldiers killed a Chinese fisherman while firing warning shots at his fishing boat off the military island fortress of Quemoy, an official news agency reported today.
A sampan with four people aboard had used explosives in catching fish and had ignored warnings to leave the area, the Central News Agency said.
The 32-year-old fisherman was shot accidentally when the boat was 200 yards north of Quemoy, six miles from China, the news agency said. 
The three other fishermen on the boat were unharmed.
China has long accused Taiwan of firing at Chinese fishing boats, which use the rich fishing ground off the Taiwanese offshore islands.
Taiwan has said it has to keep a close watch on the fleet of Chinese fishing junks because they could be used for smuggling illegal immigrants or for military purposes.
Last month, shells fired during an artillery drill on Quemoy landed on a village in China, wounding four people. 
The Taiwanese Government apologized for that accident.
