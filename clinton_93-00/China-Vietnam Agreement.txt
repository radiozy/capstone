
The presidents of China and Vietnam signed agreements here today settling a longstanding border dispute in the Tonkin Gulf, the state news media reported.
State television showed President Jiang Zemin of China greeting his Vietnamese counterpart, Tran Duc Luong, before the men signed the two-part settlement at the start of an official visit by Mr. Luong.
The Tonkin Gulf agreements included a demarcation of the territorial waters and the exclusive economic zones of the two sides, and an agreement on fishing, the official New China News Agency said.
They were signed after years of negotiations over the body of water, known in China as Beibu Bay, and an agreement last year on the two countries' land border.
Beijing and Hanoi normalized diplomatic relations in 1991.
