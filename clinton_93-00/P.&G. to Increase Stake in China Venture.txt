
The Procter & Gamble Company is planning to increase its stake in a Chinese joint venture with Hutchison Whampoa Ltd. to 80 percent from 69 percent for $650 million.
The agreement gives Procter & Gamble's management control of the venture, which makes and sells Tide detergent and other P.& G. products, and permits the company to increase its stake to 100 percent by 2017 for an undisclosed price.
While the weakening of currencies in Southeast Asia has hurt the earnings of American companies that do business there, the strong dollar gave P.& G. added buying power in its transaction with Hutchison, the company said.
P.&G.'s sales in Asia total about $3.5 billion a year, though only 3 percent of that comes from Southeast Asian nations where currencies have been sliding for months. 
It sells about $1 billion in goods annually in China.
Under the agreement, P.& G. will pay Hutchison $650 million between now and early 1998. 
Hutchison will realize a gain of $186 million this year and $434 million during 1998.
The venture -- called P.& G.-Hutchison -- said it had the biggest share of China's market in skin- and hair-care products, soap detergents, dental care and paper products.
