
Millennium Partners has taken control of the building on West 66th Street formerly occupied by the Chinese Mission to the United Nations, which has moved to First Avenue between 34th and 35th Streets in a swap arranged by Millennium.
The arrangement, in negotiations for over four years, permits Millennium to expand the adjacent Phillips Club, its extended-stay hotel, by incorporating the old mission building into the real estate development company's Lincoln Square complex. 
The remodeled building will add 100 units and 25,000 square feet of retail space between Broadway and Amsterdam Avenue, more than doubling the number of rooms.
The new Chinese mission on First Avenue, developed by Millennium, is in two buildings containing 125,000 square feet of headquarters space and 88,000 square feet of residential and recreational space.
"This landmark deal was an education and an experience of a lifetime," Christopher M. Jeffries, a founder of Millennium, said of the negotiations, which were conducted in New York, Washington and Beijing. 
According to Millennium, it was the first real estate transaction in the United States between a private American company and the government of China. 
The existing Phillips Club, which Mr. Jeffries said has an occupancy rate of well over 90 percent, has 96 rooms in the Grand Millennium building at Broadway and 66th Street. 
The expansion is to be completed in a year.
