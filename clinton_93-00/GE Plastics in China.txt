
GE Plastics is building a $50 million plant in China to sell its goods to that growing market. 
The Pittsfield-based division of the General Electric Company makes high-performance thermoplastics for cars, computers, telephones and other goods. 
The plant in the southern Chinese city of Nansha will be able to produce 20,000 tons of thermoplastics a year. 
It will be GE Plastics' first plant in China.
