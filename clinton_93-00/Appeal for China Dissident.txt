
The family of Chen Ziming, the accused leader of the Tiananmen Square uprising, appealed today to Chinese security officials to "provide a humanitarian environment" for his "medical treatment and convalescence."
Mr. Chen had surgery this month for testicular cancer after he was released on medical parole last May at the request of President Clinton and 54 United States senators. 
He was serving a 13-year sentence.
In a letter to the Ministry of Public Security, his wife, Wang Zhihong, said Mr. Chen had no means to pay his medical bills, that he is being held virtual prisoner in his apartment in Beijing and that she had lost her computer services business due to the sealing off of their home.
