
The Wm. 
Wrigley Jr. 
Company in Chicago said yesterday that it had named offices of the Leo Burnett Company and Starcom Worldwide, both part of the B Com3 Group, to handle the creative and media duties for Wrigley brands in China. 
Billings were estimated at $18 million to $20 million.
The decision came after a monthlong review that had been narrowed to Burnet, Starcom and offices of three other agencies: DDB Worldwide, part of the Omnicom Group; D'Arcy Masius Benton & Bowles, another unit of B Com3 ; and Grey Advertising. 
DDB and Grey were the incumbents.
