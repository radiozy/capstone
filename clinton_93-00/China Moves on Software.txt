
The Chinese Government said last week that it had begun a drive to purge state agencies of illegally copied software. 
The move comes more than a month after China and the United States signed an agreement to halt such pirating of American software. 
The Government has begun allocating more money for software, while the State Copyright Administration has vowed to prosecute anyone who makes illegal software copies. 
The copyright agency issued an edict last week urging all computer users to buy their software rather than copy it, the state-run press agency said.
