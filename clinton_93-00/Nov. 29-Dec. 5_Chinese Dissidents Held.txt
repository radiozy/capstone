
Three prominent dissidents who had been trying to set up China's first opposition political party were arrested and charged with endangering state security. 
For the last six months, Xu Wenli, Qin Yongmin and Wang Youcai have led a small network of activists across China trying to win recognition for the China Democratic Party. 
Although the White House and human rights groups decried the arrests, senior Chinese officials defended them, reminding the world that China is not a multi-party political system, and angrily telling America to mind its own business.   ELISABETH ROSENTHAL
