
The Procter & Gamble Company said today that it had begun sales of Crest toothpaste and toothbrushes in 500 cities throughout China. 
The move to national distribution follows months of preliminary sales in about a dozen cities in three Chinese provinces, a P.& G. spokesman, Don Tassone, said. 
The Crest items are the first health care products to be introduced in China by P.&G.
