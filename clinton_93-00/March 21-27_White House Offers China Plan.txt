
The White House thought it had a plan to improve relations with China: Get Beijing into the World Trade Organization, something the Chinese have sought for more than a decade. 
But the Chinese never wanted to pay the price -- lower tariffs on imports, an end to huge trade barriers and protection for state-owned industries.
Now, the Chinese seem willing to play, and negotiations are becoming furious in the run-up to Prime Minister Zhu Rongji's visit to Washington on April 8. 
But in the White House, some are getting cold feet. 
Amid charges of spying and human rights violations, some wonder whether Congress will torpedo the whole deal.   DAVID E. SANGER
