
Chinese officials reinforced their recent efforts to silence political opposition, sentencing two New-York-based dissidents who had sneaked back into China recently to work for democratic change to three years in a labor camp.
The two men, Zhang Lin and Wei Quanbao, were charged with hiring prostitutes and evading the border police. 
But the Chinese Foreign Ministry noted that the two were members of a group of dissidents which it identified as a "hostile foreign organization."
A friend said the men were hoping to work with the fledgling China Democratic Party, which organizers tried to register as a legal party this year. 
Earlier this month, three of the party's leaders received heavy prison sentences.
