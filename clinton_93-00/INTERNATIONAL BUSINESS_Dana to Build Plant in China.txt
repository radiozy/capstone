
The Dana Corporation, a maker of auto parts, said yesterday that it would build an axle factory in China's Fujian province, part of the company's efforts to offer customers in the Asia-Pacific region better support.
Dana, which is based in Toledo, Ohio, already operates three manufacturing plants in China and 13 in the Asia-Pacific region.
Dana's Fujian Spicer Drivetrain Systems unit will build the 40,000-square-foot plant in Fuzhou. 
The plant, scheduled for completion next summer, will eventually employ about 150 workers. 
The company did not disclose the amount of its investment.
Initially, the plant is to make axle parts for full-size and small vans and sell them to a Fujian-based vehicle assembler. 
Later, it will make front and rear axles for light-duty vehicles.
