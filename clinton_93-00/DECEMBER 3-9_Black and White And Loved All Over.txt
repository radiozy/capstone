
Of all the attempts of the Chinese Communist government to win over Washington, none has attracted so much advance good will as the delivery of two sad-eyed giant pandas named Mei Xiang and Tian Tian. 
China will receive $1 million a year from the National Zoo, where officials expect the pair will become an instant star attraction and hope they will relax enough to produce the viable offspring that eluded their predecessors, the late Ling-Ling and Hsing-Hsing.   Francis X. Clines
