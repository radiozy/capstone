
The American Telephone and Telegraph Company has formed a joint venture with three Chinese partners to manufacture optic fiber for the nation's telecommunications industry, the official New China News Agency reported. 
A.T.& T. and its partners -- the Shanghai Optical Fiber Telecommunications Engineering Corporation, the Shanghai municipal posts and telecommunications administration, and the Post and Telecommunications Industrial Corporation -- signed the contract on Wednesday. 
Plans call for building a factory in Shanghai that will produce 654 million feet of optic fiber a year.
