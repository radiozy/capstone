
The Yongquan temple has become the first Buddhist monastery in China to buy television advertising time -- to promote tourism to the mountain retreat, the New China News Agency said today.
The monastery beat out other contenders with a bid of $24,000 to win time on the 8 A.M. "Good Morning, Fujian" program on Fujian provincial television, the news agency said.
Yongquan temple will use the advertising spot to promote Mount Gushan, a leading tourist spot in Fujian and the site of the monastery, the temple abbot, Master Pufa, told the news agency.
He said the monastery wanted to extend the monks' greetings to Fujian and its people.
After this first venture into the media, the Yongquan temple will buy additional television time to improve its communications with the outside world, Master Pufa said.
