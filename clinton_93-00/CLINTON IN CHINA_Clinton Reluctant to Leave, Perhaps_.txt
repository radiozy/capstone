
Just because you're President of the United States doesn't mean you can't get stuck in an elevator. 
It happened to Bill Clinton today -- for 10 long minutes.
Finally the doors were pried open and Mr. Clinton was freed, along with his wife, Hillary Rodham Clinton; his chief of staff, Erskine Bowles, and Secret Service agents.
Apparently a lightning bolt during a thunderstorm triggered a power surge that knocked out a computer at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Center, which stopped Mr. Clinton's elevator as he was leaving after speaking to business leaders.
A reserve power supply allowed the elevator to move to a floor but the doors would not open, a White House spokesman, Barry Toiv, said. 
The agents with Mr. Clinton radioed for help; it was clear to agents waiting for the President that something was amiss, Mr. Toiv said.
After 10 minutes, they pried open the doors, he said, adding: "Nobody was overly concerned. 
They were cracking jokes about it, the President and everybody."
