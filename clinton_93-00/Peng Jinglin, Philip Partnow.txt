
Peng Jinglin, a daughter of Wang Yuwen and Peng Tengyun of Beijing, is to be married this afternoon to Philip Partnow, a son of Marcy R. Partnow and Dr. Michael J. Partnow of Moorestown, N.J. 
Rabbi Richard F. Steinbrink is to officiate at the Rittenhouse Hotel in Philadelphia.
The bride, 30, is a consultant in the international sales department of Shu Uemura, a cosmetics maker in Tokyo. 
She graduated from Kanagawa University in Japan. 
Her father, who is retired, was a senior engineer and vice president at Army Factory 21, an aircraft manufacturing plant in Hebei, China, and a member of the sixth Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, an advisory body of distinguished non-Communist Party members in Beijing. 
The bride's mother, also retired, was a Russian-Chinese translator for the Chinese Air Force in Beijing.
The bridegroom, 31, is a senior vice president and the counsel in the transaction structuring group at Nikko Salomon Smith Barney, an investment bank in Tokyo. 
Until March, he was an associate in the Tokyo office of the law firm of Sullivan & Cromwell. 
He graduated magna cum laude from Harvard University and received a law degree from Columbia University.
His father is a neurologist at the Neurological Center, a group practice in Willingboro, N.J. 
The bridegroom's mother teaches French at Westfield Friends School in Cinnaminson, N.J.
The couple met in 1997 at a cocktail party given by a mutual friend in Hong Kong, where the bride was visiting on business and where the bridegroom was working.
