
To the Editor:
Does Clyde Haberman ("We Become Just What We Feared," NYC column, Jan. 
25) believe that if a government allows a few free (nongovernment) enterprises that the government is no longer Communist?
He writes: "This is the specter of Communism that seems to be haunting us today. 
Even though the Reds have been shown the door in all but a few outposts like Cuba and North Korea."
Approximately one-fourth of the world is still under Communist rule. 
China's embrace of a few limited free-market policies does not mean that the "Reds have been shown the door."
Communists are still in power. 
They still trample civil rights and, indeed, human rights.
 
GEORGE YOUNG
Bronxville, N.Y., Jan. 
26, 2000
