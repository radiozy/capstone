
The ITT Corporation said yesterday that it had agreed to form a joint venture in China to manufacture sewage pumps and mining pumps. 
ITT will have a 65 percent stake in the venture with the Jinbei Automobile Company of Shenyang, China. 
The new company will employ about 100 people and make about 15,000 pumps a year, according to Leif E. Carlsson, the president of ITT Flygt A.B., a Swedish unit of ITT. 
Jinbei, which employs 40,000 people, is the 35th-largest industrial enterprise in China.
