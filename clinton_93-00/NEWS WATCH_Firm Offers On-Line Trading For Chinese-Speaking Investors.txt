
Chinese speakers who may have felt left out of the on-line stock market can now buy and sell shares through a Web site run by Charles Schwab (www.schwab.com/chinese). 
The site, which was announced in an advertising campaign that began yesterday, focuses on Chinese-speaking investors in the United States, China, Hong Kong, Singapore and Taiwan.
People using Microsoft Internet Explorer 4.0 can download Chinese fonts that allow them to communicate with the brokerage in traditional or simplified Chinese.   TINA KELLEY
