
The Japanese auto maker Toyota plans to help China's fledgling auto industry by sharing managerial expertise and technology, the company's president, Tatsuro Toyoda, said yesterday in Beijing. 
After meeting with the Chinese Prime Minister, Li Peng, Mr. Toyoda also said his company hoped to begin making cars in China in 1996. 
Mr. Toyoda did not provide details of a planned assembly plant, saying the project would be decided by the Chinese Government and Chinese partners. 
But Japanese news media reported that Toyota planned to begin a large-scale manufacturing project with Tianjin Automotive Industrial Group. 
Tianjin Automotive currently assembles cars under an agreement with the Daihatsu Motor Corporation, a small-car maker affiliated with Toyota.
