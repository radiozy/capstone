
The Western Company of North America said Friday that it would sell a drilling rig to the China National Machinery Import and Export Corporation for $37.5 million. 
Western expects to close the sale in January. 
The semisubmersible rig is completing a contract in the North Sea this month. 
Western also said it had completed the sale of another rig, now working offshore in Brazil, to Queiroz Galvao Purfuracoes S.A. of Brazil, a drilling contractor, for $11.7 million.
