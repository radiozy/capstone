
In the Upper Register
MONDAY -- The National Asian American Theater Company will benefit from a concert performance of the musical "Falsettoland."
The cast will include Kevin Gray, Jason Ma and Ann Harada. 
The evening will begin with cocktails at 6:30, followed by the performance at 8, all at St. Peter's Church, 619 Lexington Avenue (54th Street). 
Tickets, $50, from (212) 505-3003.
 
Chinese Art From Five Millennia
THURSDAY -- Hillary Rodham Clinton is expected to attend a gala event that will celebrate the opening of "China: 5,000 Years," an exhibition at the Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum. 
The evening will begin at 6 with drinks and a preview of the show, which includes nearly 500 works from China. 
Proceeds will support the museum's programs. 
A performance of Chinese music, introduced by the actress Joan Chen and directed by Tan Dun, will follow at 7:30. 
Dinner will be served at 8. 
Black tie. 
Tickets, $1,000, from (212) 423-3581.
The Stars Come Out
FEB. 9 -- Robert Duvall, Francis Ford Coppola, Helena Bonham Carter, Greg Kinnear, Anne Heche, Bai Ling, Richard Gere and Jack Nicholson will be among those honored by the National Board of Review of Motion Pictures at a dinner at Tavern on the Green. 
The event will raise money for the board's educational programs and its Freedom of Expression Fund. 
Cocktails at 6. 
Tickets, $275, from (212) 534-0825.
 
Friends to Wild Animals
FEB. 9 -- The Tanzania Wildlife Fund will hold a party at Jezebel, a restaurant at 630 Ninth Avenue (45th Street), to raise money for the organization's efforts to preserve rain forests in Africa. 
Denzel Washington and George Plimpton will be among the evening's hosts. 
Drinks at 7 will be followed by a buffet and an auction of photographs of Africa. 
Tickets, $150, from (212) 252-2328.
 
For Healthy Hearts
FEB. 10 -- Barbara Walters will be the master of ceremonies for a dinner-dance at the Waldorf-Astoria benefiting the American Heart Association. 
The evening will begin with drinks at 7, followed by dinner and the presentation of the group's annual Heart of New York award to Brian D. Perkins, the president of the McNeil Consumer Products Company. 
Black tie. 
Tickets, $500, from (212) 878-5928.
 
Urban Justice
FEB. 10 -- The Urban Justice Center will hold a cocktail party and scotch tasting from 6:30 to 10 P.M. at the SoHo Grand Hotel, 310 West Broadway (Canal Street). 
Proceeds will go to the group, which provides free legal services. 
Tickets, $50, from (212) 533-0540, ext. 
338, or $60 at the door.
 
Honoring a Financier
FEB. 10 -- Henry R. Kravis, a founding partner in Kohlberg, Kravis, Roberts & Company, the investment and buyout firm, will receive the Fiorello H. La Guardia Award at a dinner at the Plaza. 
The black-tie evening will raise money for scholarships to the Milano Graduate School of Management and Urban Policy at the New School for Social Research. 
Cocktails at 6:30. 
Black tie. 
Tickets, $500, from (212) 229-5957.
