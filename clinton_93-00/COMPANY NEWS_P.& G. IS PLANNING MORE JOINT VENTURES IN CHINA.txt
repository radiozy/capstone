
The Procter & Gamble Company said yesterday that it had agreed to establish three joint ventures in China with Hutchison Whampoa Ltd. of Hong Kong and three Government-owned factories. 
Two of the projects involve laundry and cleaning products. 
The third is in the personal-cleansing products business. 
Hutchison Whampoa is already a partner in P.& G.'s other businesses in China. 
P.& G. makes and markets several shampoo brands and personal care products there.
