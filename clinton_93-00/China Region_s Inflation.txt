
Inflation in Shenzhen, China's booming special economic zone neighboring Hong Kong, reached 16.6 percent in the first four months of 1993, its highest level in four years, the daily newspaper Wen Wei Po reported on Friday. 
The figure, based on an index of residents' typical living costs, includes a huge rise, of 35.8 percent, in retail prices, the Beijing-controlled newspaper said. 
Inflation in building materials prices is running at 39.9 percent, and for various fuels, at 24 percent, it said. 
Prices of services have risen 20.3 percent.
