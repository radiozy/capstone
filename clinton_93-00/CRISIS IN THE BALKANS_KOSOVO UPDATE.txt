
COMBAT
* NATO said its aircraft bombed the Chinese Embassy in Belgrade on Friday night because the military command mistakenly believed that it was a headquarters for a Yugoslav arms agency. 
The accident killed at least three people.
* The embassy bombing will not slow the air campaign, NATO and Pentagon officials said.
 
DIPLOMACY
 
* China called an emergency meeting of the United Nations Security Council and denounced both NATO and the United States for the bombing. 
Other members expressed "shock and concern" but the Council did not issue a statement of condemnation.
* The White House said the American Ambassador in Beijing, James Sasser, had apologized to the Chinese Government for the bombing.
* Russia condemned the embassy bombing. 
Its Balkan envoy, Viktor S. Chernomyrdin, flew to Bonn to meet with German leaders and was expected to go to Belgrade on Sunday.
 
PROTESTS
 
* Large, angry protests erupted around American Government offices in several Chinese cities as people learned of the embassy bombing.
