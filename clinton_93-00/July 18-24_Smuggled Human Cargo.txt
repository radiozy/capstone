
"The Golden Mountain" is what many poor Chinese call North America, and the interception of a boatload of more than 100 illegal immigrants in a ship off British Columbia shows once again what people will endure to reach these shores. 
Some paid more than $35,000; all endured filthy conditions for more than a month. 
Now Canadian immigration judges must decide if they will stay, or be deported to China.   ANTHONY DePALMA
