
A Chinese Boeing 737 passenger plane flying from Guangzhou to Guilin in south China crashed today, the official Xinhua news agency reported.
Xinhua said the plane belonged to the Civil Aviation Administration of China.
The news agency said casualties and the cause of the accident were being investigated, and gave no further details.
The crash was the fifth air disaster in China in four months.
On Nov. 
8, a helicopter distributing leaflets crashed into a building and exploded as it plunged into a crowd, killing 33 people.
A Soviet-built charter plane crashed Oct. 
9 in a remote village in northwest China, killing nine French tourists and five crew members.
A helicopter carrying Japanese tourists crashed Aug. 
11 near the Great Wall outside of Beijing, killing 15 people.
A passenger plane crashed July 31 while trying to take off from the Nanjing airport in eastern China, killing 107 people.
