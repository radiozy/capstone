
The McDonald's Corporation, the American fast-food chain, opened its first restaurant in China.
China is the 53d country in which McDonald's has opened an outlet.
Unlike Kentucky Fried Chicken and Pizza Hut, which opened their first Chinese restaurants in Beijing, the capital, McDonald's advanced from the south, opening in Shenzhen, just across the border from Hong Kong. 
At 8.90 Hong Kong dollars (about $1.14), a Big Mac costs about four times more than an ordinary Chinese-made hamburger. 
A Shenzhen factory worker takes home the equivalent of about 500 to 800 Hong Kong dollars ($65 to $100) a month.
