
Two Chinese dissidents who disappeared last month before a planned appeal to free political prisoners said today that they were held by the police to prevent unrest on the anniversary of last year's army crackdown.
A third dissident who organized the appeal and was also detained, Hou Dejian, a Taiwanese pop singer, fled the mainland on Monday and arrived in Taiwan today in a fishing boat. 
Mr. Hou defected to China in 1983.
The two other dissidents, Zhou Duo and Gao Xin, who vanished shortly before their scheduled news conference, said they were taken into custody by security officials and held until after the anniversary of the crackdown, on June 4.
The army crushed the democracy movement in Beijing on June 3-4 last year, killing hundreds of people.
