
China has bought an additional 299,740 metric tons of United States wheat under a price subsidy program, the Agriculture Department said today. 
F. Paul Dickerson, general sales manager for the department's Foreign Agricultural Service, said three private exporting companies had made the deals under the department's Export Enhancement Program. 
Delivery of the wheat is scheduled for the first three months of 1991, he said. 
It was the first wheat sale to China under the program since Aug. 
1.
