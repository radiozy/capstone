
China's experiment with creating stock markets will remain limited, and avoiding "chaos" will be a priority, a Chinese official told a news conference here today.
Hong Hu, vice minister of the State Commission for Restructuring the Economy, said the experiment with stock markets had reached only a small scale and was being constantly evaluated. 
China's two stock exchanges are in the cities of Shanghai and Shenzhen.
"We don't seek to have this experiment in many places," Mr. Hong said of China's policy toward stock exchanges. 
"We do not demand perfection, but we also want to avoid chaos."
