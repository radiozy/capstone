
Vietnam reported progress today in relations with Beijing and said the Vietnamese leader, Nguyen Van Linh, would go to India next week in the search for peace in Cambodia.
The Hanoi radio said Chinese and Vietnamese negotiators had reached agreements during four days of discussions in Beijing ending on Thursday, the first talks at a senior level between the two countries in eight years.
''The two parties discussed normalization of relations and matters of mutual interest, which included Cambodia,'' the radio said. 
''They reached a number of agreements and remaining problems will be discussed later.''
