
The British publisher Robert Maxwell yesterday suspended the European publication of China Daily, the official English-language newspaper, because of the Chinese Government's crackdown in Beijing.
In a statement, Mr. Maxwell accused the Chinese Government of ''bloody murder'' and said publication would not resume until there was ''a drastic reversal of the course upon which the Chinese leadership has embarked.''
Under a deal made with the
Chinese Government in 1986, the Maxwell Communication Corporation printed and distributed 6,000 copies of China Daily in Europe. 
The eight-page paper was sent from Beijing to London via satellite.
 
In yesterday's issue of China
Daily - the last Mr. Maxwell will publish - the paper endorsed the Government's efforts in quelling a ''counterrevolutionary riot.''
China Daily sells most of its 150,000 copies a day to English-speaking executives doing business in China.
Karen Tse, business manager of the China Daily Distribution Corporation in New York, said the company distributes about 5,000 copies of the paper in North America. 
She said it had no plans to stop publication.
