
Many Chinese businesses are paying managers too much, an influential Beijing newspaper said on Friday.
The Economic Daily said that too many managers earned more than the state allowed and that controls should be tightened to prevent dissatisfaction among workers.
According to rules issued by the State Council, or Cabinet, managers can earn as much as three times as much as workers.
The Central Committee of China's ruling Communist Party last week called for closer links with the people. 
Western diplomats said the party was taking up issues it saw as popular with workers to solidify its grip on power. 
One such issue is disparity in pay.
The official news organizations have also described workers as the backbone of the economy and praised companies that promoted officials from the ranks.
