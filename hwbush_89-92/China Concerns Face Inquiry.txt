
The Chinese authorities have recommended fining five state-owned companies, including one founded by the eldest son of the leader Deng Xiaoping, for profiteering, the Economic Reporter magazine said.
The companies' illegal operations were said to include speculative trading in foreign exchange, rare raw materials and luxury consumer products.
Chinese investigators recommended the highest fine of 17 million yuan, or $4.6 million, against the China International Trust and Investment Corporation, a state enterprise, the Hong Kong-based magazine said. 
A fine of seven million yuan, or $1.9 million, was imposed on the Hua Development Corporation, founded by the son of the leader.
