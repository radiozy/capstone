
China's foreign debt surged 27 percent last year, to $52.55 billion, the Government said today. 
"The debt rate and the debt-service ration were within the limits that are internationally held reasonable," the state-run Xinhua News Agency quoted an unidentified official of the State Administration of Exchange Control as saying. 
China's exports during the first eight months of the year soared to a record $35.54 billion, up 17 percent from the same period of 1990, a separate Xinhua report said. 
Imports lagged far behind, at $19.06 billion, up 9.2 percent from the period in 1990.
