
About 200,000 young people attended a daylong open-air pop concert in Hong Kong today to raise money for the pro-democratic demonstrations in China.
The crowd fell silent as a relayed newscast from Beijing reported the apparent triumph of hard-line leaders.
''We are very disappointed,'' said a student, putting her head in her hands.
But organizers and performers vowed the struggle would go on.
''We are just awakening,'' said John Shum, a film producer.
''This is not the end, it is just the start of the movement of the whole Chinese community,'' said Yuen How Siu, a social worker.
When asked why they had come to the concert, two students looked slightly bewildered by the question. 
''Can't you see?'' said one. 
''We are Chinese.''
Halfway through the concert more than $500,000 had been raised at the event and through pledges from viewers watching on television.
