
Cell Technology Inc., a Boulder, Colo., biotechnology company, reached an agreement in principle to form a joint venture with the Chinese Medical Academy of Science. 
The company said it was one of the first joint ventures between the Chinese Government and the biotechnology industry.
In connection with the Chinese Cancer Institute, Cell Technology will initially begin clinical trials in China involving Imuvert, the company's prinicipal anti-cancer agent for brain cancer, which is based on biological response modifier technology. 
The company said it was also exploring possible economic collaboration that might lead to the establishment of a joint manufacturing center in China. 
Cell Technology is also contemplating the possibility of licensing several of the biological response modifier products developed by the Chinese Cancer Institute.
