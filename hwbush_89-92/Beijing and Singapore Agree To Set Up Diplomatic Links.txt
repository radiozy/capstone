
China announced tonight that it had established diplomatic relations with Singapore.
The announcement followed the restoration of relations with Indonesia.
Singapore, whose population is mostly of Chinese ancestry, has a trade office in Beijing and has maintained good ties with the leaders of China and Taiwan. 
But even though relations warmed in recent years, Singapore said it would wait for Indonesia.
The establishment of relations took effect immediately after the signing of a joint communique at the United Nations in New York.
