
Hong Kong residents are moving more of their wealth out of the British colony, bankers said this week.
This year alone, Chinese customers are likely to transfer about $770 million to Australia and four times that much to Canada, estimated Will Buttrose, a consultant to the private banking operation in Australia of National Westminster Bank P.L.C.
''The amounts are going to escalate in the next two years,'' he said, adding that ''people are very concerned about 1997,'' when Hong Kong is to come under Chinese control.
He said wealthy individuals in Hong Kong and Taiwan were transferring about $100 million a month to Canada, which many Hong Kong immigrants now call home.
''The bulk of the money is going to North America,'' he said. 
''Switzerland is a player, but more is going to North America.''
Participants at a conference on private banking in Geneva on Tuesday said Hong Kong investors are eager to place their money in overseas tax havens and trust funds before China takes control.
