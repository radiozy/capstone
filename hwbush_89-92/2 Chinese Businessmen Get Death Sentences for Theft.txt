
Two businessmen were sentenced to death today for theft and embezzlement, as part of the Government's crackdown on economic crimes.
Tonight's television news programs showed the sentencing before a large public meeting held in a gymnasium. 
Both the publicity and the stiffness of the penalties seemed intended to discourage such corruption.
The ways business and Communist Party officials have illegally used their jobs to amass wealth were a major concern of the democracy movement this spring.
