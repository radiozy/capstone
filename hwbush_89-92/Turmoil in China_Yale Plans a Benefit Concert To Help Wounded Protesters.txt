
More than 40 musicians, including Yale music students, faculty and alumni, will put on a nine-hour concert on Friday to raise money for medical supplies and other aid for those wounded in the demonstrations in Beijing.
Chinese students and scholars will speak at the concert, which will feature chamber and jazz ensembles, soloists, Chinese and American choirs, and singers performing selections from ''Turandot,'' a Puccini opera set in Beijing.
The proceeds from the concert, to be held from noon to 9 P.M. at the Center Church on the Green, will be turned over to the China Democracy Fund, which was organized by Chinese students at Yale.
