
A Chinese television factory has signed an agreement with a South Korean company, the first time the Seoul Government has approved a direct investment by a company from a Communist nation, the Finance Ministry said Tuesday. 
The Hanzhou TV Factory will invest $2.8 million in a joint venture with the Orion Electric Company of South Korea and the Zhejiang Fuchen Company of Hong Kong. 
A ministry spokesman said the venture was expected to produce 580,000 tubes for color TV sets in 1989, 1.1 million of the 20-inch tubes in 1990 and 1.2 million in 1992.
