
L. M. Ericsson, the Swedish telecommunications company, and Pacific Link Communication Ltd., a Hong Kong-based company in which Millicom International Inc. has a 28 percent stake, are giving a mobile phone network to the city of Beijing.
The donation is intended to open the door to orders for networks in other Chinese cities and for extension of the Beijing network, which will be able to serve 2,000 subscribers in the center of the Chinese capital and portions of its suburbs.
Pacific Link also expects the gift to help it compete for customers for its new Hong Kong $25 million network by making it possible for them to use their phones in Beijing and, eventually, throughout China. 
The donated system is worth roughly $2.5 million, a Millicom executive said.
