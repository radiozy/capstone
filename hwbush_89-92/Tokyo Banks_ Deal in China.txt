
Four Japanese banks have agreed to make a loan to a Chinese state company, the first since a military crackdown last June, but they denied that the move involved any change of policy or return to business as usual.
 
Sumitomo Bank, Sanwa
Bank, Dai-Ichi Kangyo Bank and Industrial Bank of Japan are lending $20 million to the China International Iron and Steel Investment Corporation, bankers said.
The Chinese company will use the money to import second-hand equipment from the Nippon Steel Corporation for use at the Meishan Steel Works near Nanjing.
