
A court notice posted today says that China will try two more Tiananmen democracy advocates on charges of "organizing and leading a counterrevolutionary clique."
If convicted, the two dissidents, Chen Yanlin and Zhang Yafei, would face a minimum prison sentence of five years, but not the death penalty, unless there are other charges that have not been announced. 
The notice did not further identify Mr. Chen or Mr. Zhang, and their names are not known to human rights organizations or diplomats.
