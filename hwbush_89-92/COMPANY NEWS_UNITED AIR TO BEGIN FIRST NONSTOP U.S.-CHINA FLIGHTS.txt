
United Airlines Inc. opens the first nonstop flights from the United States to China today with three-day-a-week direct service between San Francisco and Shanghai, continuing to Beijing. 
United has served the two cities through Tokyo since 1986. 
The new service aboard Boeing 747's will be the first direct link to China's capital and to Shanghai, the world's sixth-largest city. 
The direct flights will continue through September, when service will resume through Tokyo.
