
The escalating turmoil in China has prompted Kentucky Fried Chicken, one of the nation's largest advertisers, to drop a television advertisement filmed partly in Beijing.
 
And the American Express
Company said it would reconsider whether to use an ad featuring images of that country.
A spokesman for Kentucky
Fried Chicken said yesterday that the company would cancel an ad using pictures of a Kentucky Fried Chicken restaurant in Beijing. 
''We have no plans to run that spot in the future,'' the spokesman, Richard M. Detwiler, said. 
''We just don't think it would be appropriate given what's been going on in Beijing.''
American Express said it would evaluate events before deciding whether to run an ad depicting an American couple at the Great Wall of China.
Nancy Muller, a spokeswoman for American Express, said the ad ''would be completely inappropriate at a time like this''
