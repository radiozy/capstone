
The United States will withhold its annual contribution to the United Nations Population Fund because the fund subsidizes abortions in China, a State Department official said today.
The official, Nyle C. Brady, the United States representative to the Governing Council of the Population Fund, said the United States would withhold its $19 million annual contribution to disassociate itself from family-planning programs in China.
The United States decreased its contribution to the agency in 1985 and has withheld its entire contribution since 1986 because it asserts that China's policy of one child per couple relies on coercive abortions and involuntary sterilization. 
Officials said the population agency does not endorse abortion as a family-planning method and is not involved in the Chinese program.
