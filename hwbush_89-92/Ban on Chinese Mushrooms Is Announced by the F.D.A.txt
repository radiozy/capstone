
The Food and Drug Administration on Monday ordered all Chinese mushrooms detained at United States ports because of a bacteria contamination that can cause severe food poisoning.
An F.D.A. spokesman said it was unlikely that contaminated mushrooms were on grocery shelves or in American homes because most of the known contamination involved 68-ounce, institutional-size cans that have been banned since May.
''Very little got into the marketplace,'' said the spokesman. 
Emil Corwin. 
The F.D.A. ordered the 68-ounce cans detained at ports after food poisoning outbreaks in New York, Mississippi and Pennsylvania last February and April caused 100 illnesses and sent at least 16 people to hospitals.
The agency said Monday that it had also now identified bacteria contamination in 5.5-ounce and 16-ounce cans of mushrooms produced by 10 plants in China. 
All those products have been recalled or are being recalled, the F.D.A. said.
