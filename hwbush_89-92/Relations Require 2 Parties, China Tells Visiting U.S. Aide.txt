
Foreign Minister Qian Qichen told the highest-ranking American official to visit China in more than a year today that improving relations could not be done by one side alone, the official New China News Agency said.
Robert M. Kimmitt, Under Secretary of State for Political Affairs, held talks with Mr. Qian and other Chinese officials in an effort to repair relations hurt by human rights and trade disputes. 
Ties began to break down in June 1989, when the Chinese authorities crushed demonstrations by democracy advocates near Tienanmen Square in Beijing, killing hundreds.
