
Prime Minister Li Peng said today that Hu Yaobang, the former Communist Party leader, had been hospitalized for an undisclosed ailment.
The South China Morning Post, an English-language Hong Kong newspaper, reported today that Mr. Hu suffered a heart attack on Saturday.
Mr. Hu, 73 years old, was forced to resign as party leader in January 1987 after student demonstrations that called for more democracy in China. 
Mr. Hu was said to have been accused of being too lenient toward liberals. who had challenged party ideology.
