
The American Telephone and Telegraph Company is selling $5 million worth of telephone equipment to the Chinese Army, a company spokesman said. 
The contract, for digital telephone exchanges, was signed last month and is awaiting United States Government approval, an A.T.&T. spokesman, Tom Harris, said in a telephone interview from the headquarters of the A.T.&T. 
Network Systems Company in Morristown, N.J.
Mr. Harris also said A.T.&T. had been granted a license by the Chinese Government about two months ago to start a joint-venture company to manufacture telecommunications transmission equipment. 
A.T.&T. Network Systems International, based in the Netherlands, owns 50 percent of the joint venture. 
The Chinese partners are the Shanghai Optical Fiber Communications Engineering Corporation and the Shanghai Telecommunications Equipment Factory.
