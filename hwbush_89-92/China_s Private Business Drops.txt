
The number of private businesses in China has fallen for the first time since 1981.
 
The Farmers Daily reported
Friday that at the end of June the country had 12.34 million private businesses employing 19.43 million people, a drop of 2.19 million and 3.57 million respectively from the end of last year.
Output value of private business in industry, construction and transportation - three of the biggest sectors apart from restaurants and retail shops -fell 29 percent in the first six months of the year from the corresponding period last year, it said.
Private business has come under strong attack in the official press in the last two months for what has been described as widespread tax evasion, exploitation of cheap labor and excess income.
