
Officials tonight suspended the search for 10 men still missing after an oil exploration barge sank in the South China Sea with nearly 200 aboard.
Four of those missing were trapped in a decompression chamber when the barge capsized in a typhoon on Thursday about 65 miles east of Hong Kong. 
Officials said their air supply would run out on Friday morning.
The search ended after rescue workers battled high waves and low visibility in the search for the missing.
At least 174 people, including 11 Americans, were pulled from the sea after the barge sank just below the surface on Thursday. 
The American skipper, Billy Young, and 12 others were confirmed dead.
Trevor C. Berry, spokesman for the Hong Kong Maritime Rescue Center, said the chances of finding any of the missing alive were slim, although rescuers were still hoping for a miracle.
