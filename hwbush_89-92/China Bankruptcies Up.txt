
Sixty-six companies went bankrupt in China in the first six months of this year, the New China News Agency said today. 
The figure is far higher than previously reported and was seen as a warning to money-losing state factories that efficiency is now prized over ideology in the drive to reform the economy. 
The Government, fearing urban unrest, has made it clear that it will not abandon workers before a social security net is in place. 
In the meantime, it is stressing less painful methods of reform, like encouraging the worst-performing factories to merge with successful ones or seek foreign buyers.
