
IRAN BARKLEY will defend his International Boxing Federation supermiddleweight title in Beijing in in October.
Barkley (29-7) will face DOUG DeWITT of Yonkers (33-7-5) in a 12-round bout at 17,000-seat Capitol Gymnasium on Oct. 
16.
The card, which is scheduled to be shown on pay-per-view in the United States, will also feature LEEONZER BARBER (15-1), the World Boxing Organization light heavyweight champion, against MIKE SEDILLO (23-9-1). 
Two other W.B.O. champions, middleweight GERALD McCLELLAND and junior welterweight JESSE BENAVIDES, will also be on the card.
