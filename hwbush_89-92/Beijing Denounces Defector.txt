
The Government has denounced as a traitor and expelled from Parliament a senior offical who has sought refuge in the United States. 
The man, Xu Jiatun, who served from 1983 until early 1990 as the top Chinese official in Hong Kong, fled China in April and is now living is seclusion in California. 
A member of the Communist party's Central Advisory Commsiion and friend of Deng Xiao-ping, China's senior leader, Mr. Xu was the highest official to flee China after the military crackdown in 1989.
