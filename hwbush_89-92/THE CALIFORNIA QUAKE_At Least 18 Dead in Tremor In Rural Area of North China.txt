
A series of earthquakes rattled northern China late Wednesday and early today, killing at least 18 people and flattening about 8,000 houses in a largely rural area, the authorities said.
Officials of the State Seismological Bureau said the quakes were not related to the tremor in Northern California on Tuesday.
The bureau said that five tremors, registering up to 6.0 on the Richter scale, shook an area along the Shanxi-Hebei provincial border and that at least 28 people had been injured.
The first quake, measuring 5.7, was felt in Beijing, about 135 miles to the east, but there were no reports of injuries or damage in the capital.
