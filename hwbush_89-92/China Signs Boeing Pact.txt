
China today signed a contract to buy 13 jetliners from the Boeing Company for $800 million and it plans to send a trade delegation to the United States to try to ease conflicts between the nations.
The deal for the 757's was signed late today by Jim Chorlton, a Boeing vice president, and Liu Yuanfan, president of the China Aviation Supplies Corporation.
In June, Prime Minister Li Peng of China had used China's deals with Boeing as an example of how United States businesses would suffer if the United States revoked China's most-favored-nation trade status.
Last month, American lawmakers voted to place stringent conditions on renewal of the trading status, which grants Chinese exports to the United States the lowest possible tariffs. 
Critics of China wanted to use the issue to resolve trade disputes and put pressure on Beijing to address concerns over its human rights policies and arms sales abroad.
