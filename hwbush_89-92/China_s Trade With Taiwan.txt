
Taiwan's trade with China through Hong Kong is expected to slump dramatically after hitting new highs earlier this year, officials said today.
The Board of Foreign Trade reported two-way trade between Taiwan and China rose 61 percent, to $1.45 billion, in the first five months of 1989 from $900 million a year earlier.
Taiwan's exports to China, mainly synthetic fibers, textiles and electronics, totaled $1.2 billion between January and May 1989, up nearly 70 percent from $708 million a year ago. 
Its imports, mainly herbal medicines, and agricultural and industrial materials, amounted to $245 million, up about 28 percent from $192 million.
