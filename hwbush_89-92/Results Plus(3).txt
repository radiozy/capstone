
TENNIS
 
McEnroe Reaches Quarterfinals
John McEnroe moved to the quarterfinals of the Swiss Indoors tournament in Basel yesterday with a 6-1, 6-2 victory over Niclas Kulti. 
In beating the 19-year-old Swede, the No. 1 junior player in the world last year, McEnroe had eight aces in the match, which lasted 1 hour 18 minutes. 
McEnroe will next take on unseeded Andrei Cherkasov, who beat the defending champion, Jim Courier of the United States, 2-6, 6-2, 6-3. 
(AP)
Navratilova Advances: Top-seeded Martina Navratilova easily advanced to the quarterfinals, and Monica Seles and Jennifer Capriati lost their quarterfinal matches yesterday in the Nichirei International championships in Tokyo. 
Navratilova beat Stephanie Rehe of Highland, Calif., 6-1, 6-3. 
Eighth-seeded Amy Frazier beat the second-seeded Seles, 5-7, 7-5, 6-2. 
Third-seeded Katerina Maleeva of Bulgaria defeated Capriati, 7-6, 3-6, 7-6. 
(AP)
 
Graf Sweeps Into Semifinals: Steffi Graf, the No. 1 seeded player, swept past Manon Bollegraf of the Netherlands, 6-1, 6-2, yeserday to advance to the semifinals of the Volvo tournament in Leipzig, East Germany.
 
GOLF
 
Twitty Leads by 2 on 62
Howard Twitty had six birdies on the back nine on yesterday to complete an eight-under-par 62 and take the first-round lead of the Southern Open in Columbus, Ga. 
Frank Conner and Larry Mize were two strokes back in the $600,000 tournament. 
(AP)
 
ASIAN GAMES
 
Chinese Sets Cycling Record
Zhou Lingmei of China won the women's one-kilometer time trial at the Asian Games in Beijing in 1 minute 13.899 seconds, averaging 30.45 miles and hour and breaking the world record for the event. 
The Chinese swept all four gold and silver medals in the first day of track and field action. 
They also picked up five in swimming, five in shooting and one each in tennis, table tennis, cycling, weightlifting and women's foil team fencing. 
(AP)
