
A northeast China province regarded as a pioneer of market-based economic reforms is moving to cut the ''unfair'' high incomes of entrepreneurs, China's official newspaper said Friday.
The government of Liaoning
Province will place a ceiling on private incomes and review agreements under which entrepreneurs have leased companies from the state, The China Daily said.
In recent years, the region has encouraged individuals to manage money-losing state enterprises. 
A certain percentage of profits is handed over to the state.
Profit quotas will now be adjusted to curb managers' incomes, and a ceiling will be set for the highest earners, the newspaper said.
