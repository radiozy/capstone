
Four members of a human rights delegation who came from the United States to investigate conditions of political prisoners in China were detained overnight before apparently being expelled from the country today.
The American and Canadian Embassies said they were told late this afternoon that the four had left the country. 
The four, all ethnic Chinese, are Yang Chen Borchert, a 53-year-old American who was leading the group; Liu Qiyang, a 53-year-old American scholar; Ning Chyn, a 34-year-old Canadian national living in the United States, and Hsu Ru-hsueh, a 46-year-old Taiwanese who had worked for China Spring, a magazine that China considers counterrevolutionary.
