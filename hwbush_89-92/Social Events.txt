
Human Rights Anniversary
Jan. 15 -- The International League for Human Rights will observe its 50th anniversary with a reception, 6 to 9 P.M., at the Delegates Dining Room at the United Nations for Prof. Fang Li Zhi, a Chinese astrophysicist who will be presented with the league's 1991 Human Rights Award. 
Mr. Fang, who had been a leader of the movement for democracy t in China, and his wife, Li Shuxian, were recently allowed to emigrate. 
Proceeds from the event will support the league's human rights activities on China. 
Tickets $150, $250, $500 and $1,000; (212) 684-1221.
 
Party in Gatsby's Garden
Jan. 16 -- "An Evening in the Great Gatsby's Garden," a dinner and dance at the New York Marriott Marquis, which hopes to evoke F. Scott Fitzgerald's Long Island of the 1920's, is to benefit the National Center for Learning Disabilities. 
The organization helps disabled people to become literate and self-sufficient. 
The evening will start with cocktails at 6:30 P.M., dinner at 7:30 and an auction of luxury items and a raffle. 
Tickets $375. 
(212) 687-7211.

Celebrating Anna Sokolow
Jan. 17 -- Anna Sokolow, a modern-dance choreographer who recently turned 80, will have three of her dances performed at a benefit for the Dance Center of the 92d Street Y at 1395 Lexington Avenue, where she has been performing since 1936. 
Anna Sokolow's Players' Project will perform "Opus 65," "Steps of Silence" and "Kurt Weill" in the Kaufmann Concert Hall of the Y, starting at 8 P.M. Tickets $250, $75 and $20. 
The $250 ticket includes a pre-performance dinner at 6 P.M. at the nearby home of Diane and Blaine Fogg and a champagne reception after the performance at the Y. Mrs. Fogg is the chairwoman of the Y's Dance Center Committee. 
The $75 ticket includes the champagne reception. 
(212) 414-5488. 
The $20 tickets are available at the Y's box office or (212) 996-1100.

For the Catholic University

On Dr. King's Birthday
Jan. 21 -- The Congress of Racial Equality will mark the birthday of the Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. with its seventh annual Martin Luther King Ambassadorial Reception and Awards dinner at the Sheraton Centre, with Charlton Heston as the honorary chairman and Alan King as the toastmaster. 
Ambassadors of 86 countries have been invited and many celebrities in sports, the theater, movies and television are expected to attend.

Songs for Dr. King
The Boys Choir of Harlem will observe Dr. King's birthday with a concert of classical and pop music at the Equitable Center, 787 Seventh Avenue (between 51st and 52d Streets). 
Members of the the choir, established 23 years ago, receive an academic education as well as musical instruction at the Boys Choir of Harlem Academy. 
Of its members, 98 percent have gone on to college since it was founded. 
A cocktail reception at 6 P.M. will precede the concert at 7, followed by dinner at 8:30 in the Equitable Dining Room. 
Tickets $500 and $1,000; (212) 685-1095.
 
Scandinavian Skating Party
Jan. 21 -- Scandinavia will take over the Wollman Rink in Central Park with a skating party from 6 to 9 P.M. to promote the American-Scandinavian Foundation and the American-Scandinavian Society of New York. 
Scandinavian food and drink will be served and there will be gift bags for children. 
Tickets, which include the rental of skates and a locker, $25 for adults and $10 for guests under 18; (212) 879-9779.
