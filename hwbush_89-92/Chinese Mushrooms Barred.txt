
The Food and Drug Administration said today that it was blocking indefinitely all shipments of canned and pickled mushrooms from China after a number of food poisonings were associated with earlier shipments.
China supplies most of the canned mushrooms used by schools, hospital dining rooms and pizza restaurants, agency officials said. 
They said it could not immediately be determined whether any contaminated mushrooms were on retail grocery shelves.
''The agency has concluded that contamination may be widespread throughout the mushroom processing industry in China,'' the F.D.A. said.
Nearly 60 percent of all mushrooms imported into the United States come from China, said Rex Dull of the Agriculture Department.
