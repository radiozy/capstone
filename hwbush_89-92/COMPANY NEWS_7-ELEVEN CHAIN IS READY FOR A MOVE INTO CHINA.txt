
The first 7-Eleven convenience store is scheduled to open in China later this summer in the southern city of Shenzhen. 
News reports said yesterday that Dairy Farm International Holdings, which operates 280 7-Eleven stores in Hong Kong, had formed a joint venture with two other companies to open 15 outlets in the coming year, with the first scheduled for the summer. 
The company formed by the venture, Shenzhen Convenience Stores Ltd., will receive an initial infusion of $3.87 million from Dairy Farm, the Shenzhen Luohu Economic Development Corporation and Max Bail Investment of Hong Kong.
