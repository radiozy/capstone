
China says it plans to free a jailed student leader and to drop charges against a labor militant, reports a businessman who has successfully lobbied for releases.
The businessman, John Kamm, a former vice president of Occidental Chemical Far East in Hong Kong, said officials also indicated to him that the Government planned to release other dissidents before the end of the year. 
He was not told specifically how many.
One name that was brought up in Mr. Kamm's discussions was that of Wang Dan, a 22-year-old Beijing University student leader who was No. 1 on the wanted list of 21 students. 
Mr. Wang is serving a four-year sentence. 
Mr. Kamm said the authorities indicated that Wang Youcai, a 25-year-old student leader in the university's physics department, would soon be freed.
Mr. Kamm was also told that charges would be dropped against the labor campaigner, Han Dongfang, who had been charged with counter-revolutionary activity and was released in April on a medical leave. 
He was told that Mr. Han would be allowed to apply for a passport.
