
South Korea's Hyundai conglomerate plans to invest in vehicle manufacturing in China, a company spokesman said today.
The spokesman said Chung Ju Yung, Hyundai's founder and honorary chairman, was expected to visit Beijing in the next few months.
"During his visit, he would discuss with Chinese officials ways to invest in the Chinese auto industry," the spokesman said. 
"We will probably start with investment in the production of small trucks in China."
Mr. Chung said after visiting Beijing last year that a senior official had asked him to invest in the Chinese auto industry.
Annual sales of Hyundai, which operates more than 40 companies including the nation's largest auto maker, represent more than 10 percent of South Korea's gross national product.
Seoul has no diplomatic relations with China, but South Korea established a trade office in Beijing in January.
