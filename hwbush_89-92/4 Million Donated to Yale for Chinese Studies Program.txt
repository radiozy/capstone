
Two leading Hong Kong businessmen have donated $2 million to Yale University and another $2 million to a Yale-administered fund that fosters exchanges between the Ivy League school and the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Yale has announced.
The donation by Cheng Yu-tung and Lee Shau-kee will be used to strengthen Yale's East Asian studies program and to forge stronger bonds between Yale and the university in Hong Kong, Yale officials said Friday.
Chinese University also received a separate $2 million donation from the two businessmen.
