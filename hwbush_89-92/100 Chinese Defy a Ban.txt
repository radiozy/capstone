
About 100 Chinese today defied a Government ban on ''English Corner,'' a glade in a Beijing park where people have gathered for years to practice English-language skills with foreigners. 
Cordons sealed off the glade, in the Purple Bamboo Park, which has served as a Sunday venue, and official notices nearby proclaimed that ''English Corner has been withdrawn.''
But defiant students and workers said they would continue to hold the conversation sessions until they are forcibly driven away.
