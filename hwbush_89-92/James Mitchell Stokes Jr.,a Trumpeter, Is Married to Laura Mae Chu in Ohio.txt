
Laura Mae Chu and James Mitchell Stokes Jr. were married yesterday at the Ohio State University Faculty Club in Columbus by the Rev. Victor Makari, a Presbyterian minister. 
The bride is a daughter of Prof. and Mrs. Samuel C. Chu of Columbus. 
Mr. Stokes's parents live in Hendersonville, N.C.
Mrs. Stokes, a purchasing assistant at CBS Records in New York, graduated from Indiana University.
Her father is a professor of history at Ohio State. 
Her mother, Lucy Chu, is the founding director of the Columbus Yamaha Music School.
The bride is a granddaughter of Grace Zia Chu of Columbus, the Chinese cooking authority and author, and the late Lieut. 
Gen. Shih-ming Chu of Tokyo, a retired Nationalist Chinese officer who served as a military attache of the Chinese Embassy in Washington, as China's military attache in Moscow and as China's representative on the Allied Control Commission in Tokyo during the first years of the Allied occupation.
Mr. Stokes, who plays the trumpet professionally, is a graduate of the Indiana University School of Music. 
His parents own Stokes Realty in Hendersonville.
