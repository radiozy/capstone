
China already has McDonald's and Kentucky Fried Chicken. 
Now it is getting its first 7-Eleven stores. 
Officials said that five of the 24-hour convenience stores would open in the Shenzhen economic zone bordering Hong Kong on Oct. 
16 and there are plans to add between 10 and 15 more outlets in the city within the year.
Thomas Cheung, chief executive of Hong Kong Convenience Stores Ltd., which controls 7-Eleven operations in Hong Kong, said the Chinese venture would involve two other partners, Maxiball Investments Ltd. of Hong Kong and the Shenzhen Luohu Economic Development Corporation. 
He said the initial investment for the 15 stores would be about $3.8 million. 
Company officials said 7-Eleven might expand in China, depending on the success of the Shenzhen venture.
