
China's industrial production jumped 19 percent in the first eight months of 1992, compared with the corresponding period last year, the state-run New China News Agency reported today.
The press agency quoted the State Statistical Bureau as saying output from heavy industry climbed 21.3 percent while light industry rose 16.3 percent to push total industrial production to $331.9 billion from January through August.
Officials attributed the jump to faster economic development in southern China's market-driven coastal cities and an increase of 40 to 50 percent in joint ventures, the agency reported.
In specific sectors, coal production rose 10.8 percent, electricity 10 percent, nonferrous metals 18.3 percent, ethylene 18.9 and plastic 17.3 percent.
