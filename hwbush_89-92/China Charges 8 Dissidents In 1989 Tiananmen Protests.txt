
China started court proceedings today against eight dissidents, including a prominent historian, for their roles in the Tiananmen Square demonstrations in 1989.
Bao Zunxin, a scholar at the History Institute of the Chinese Academy of Social Sciences, was charged with "counterrevolutionary propaganda and incitement."
He was arrested shortly after the crackdown on June 4, 1989.
Wang Haidong was charged with the same crime, and six other people were accused of arson, looting, larceny and blocking traffic: Li Shuntang, Liu Xiaojing, Yang Junzhong, Zhou Wanshui, Lu Xiaochun, Yu Yongjie.
