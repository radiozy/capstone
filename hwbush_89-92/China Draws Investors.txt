
China said today that it had been successful in attracting foreign investment since 1989, the year investors fled after army tanks and troops crushed pro-democracy demonstrations.
"The number of foreign-funded enterprises China approved in the two and a half years since 1989 exceeded the total of the previous 10 years," the the New China News Agency said.
China has worked hard to repair the diplomatic and economic damage done when the army killed hundreds of people in the streets around Tiananmen Square in Beijing on June 4, 1989.
Between 1979 and the end of last year, Beijing approved 41,274 foreign-financed enterprises with a total contracted volume of $47.9 billion, about $20 billion of which has been put into operation, the agency said.
