
China announced plans today to fight the growing "brain drain" of scholars and scientists by giving more money to the country's top researchers and allowing them more freedom to choose their jobs, an official newspaper reported.
The China Daily quoted Personnel Minister Zhao Dongwan as saying that the Government would set up a special fund to pay $18 monthly bonuses to 9,000 selected scholars and scientists. 
Such bonuses would amount to 30 percent to 50 percent wage increases for most intellectuals, who rarely earn more than $54 a month. 
Some primary school teachers earn as little as $18.
