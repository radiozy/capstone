
Motorola Inc. will build a $120 million factory in northern China to manufacture semiconductors, cellular phones and other products, company officials and news reports said today.
Pancy Wu, a Motorola spokeswoman in Hong Kong, said the new subsidiary, Motorola (China) Electronics Ltd., would operate in Tianjin, a large industrial city less than 100 miles from Beijing.
China has been attracting increasing amounts of investment money, with foreign investment jumping 76.9 percent in the first 10 months of last year, to $4.96 billion. 
A further rise is expected this year.
The official China Daily reported today that China's State Council, or Cabinet, approved the Motorola factory earlier this week. 
The newspaper said the 70-year contract involved a total investment of $120 million.
Ms. Wu said the factory would manufacture electronics components and equipment, including semiconductors, radio paging systems, cellular phones and computer software.
