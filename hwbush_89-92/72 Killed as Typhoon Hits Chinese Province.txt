
A storm has killed 72 people and left hundreds missing in Zhejiang province, Chinese national televison reported today.
The official New China News Agency said the typhoon, which struck on Saturday, was the most severe storm in the coastal province in 27 years.
The broadcast today did not give a figure for the number injured, but the press agency over the weekend put the total at 190.
The broadcast showed the aftermath of the typhoon - toppled houses and workers rebuilding the shoreline with stones and sandbags.
Rain flooded the port of Shujiang and inundated 163,000 acres of rice and 32,000 acres of orange orchards in 37 counties.
In July, torrential rains killed 122 people and caused economic losses of almost $300 million. 
Last year, 400 people in Zhejiang were killed by typhoons, heavy rain and flooding.
