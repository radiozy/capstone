
The American International Group announced yesterday that it had been granted a license to open both life and property casualty insurance companies in China, the first foreign insurer to do so in more than four decades.
The new operation for A.I.G., a New York-based company that is the largest writer of commercial insurance in the United States and a major insurer abroad, will be based in Shanghai and will sell life insurance to Chinese citizens and foreign nationals, the company said. 
A.I.G. will also provide property and casualty products to joint ventures and other foreign business in Shanghai.
"In recent years, Shanghai has seen phenomenal growth in its industrial and commercial base," M. R. Greenberg, A.I.G.'s chairman, said. 
"Our roots are in Asia, and we have great confidence in the future of China and its role in Asia and the world."
Unlike most American insurers, A.I.G. has specialized in overseas markets since its founding in Shanghai in 1919 by an American entrepreneur. 
Like other foreign insurance companies, A.I.G. left China in 1950, the year after the Communist victory in the civil war.
The company's affiliate, the American International Assurance Company, has experienced strong growth through the marketing of insurance products in Southeast Asia in recent years.
