
Acustar Inc., the wholly owned auto parts subsidiary of the Chrysler Corporation, said it had agreed to export up to 80,000 axles a year to the Beijing Jeep Corporation of China. 
Acustar said in a statement that it would begin exporting the axles for rear-wheel-drive applications with a 1,000-axle shipment scheduled for March.
It said it was also developing front-drive axle assemblies for eventual use in Beijing's Cherokee models.
The contract also specifies that technology for the manufacture of axle assemblies be transferred to Beijing Jeep, a joint venture between the Chinese Government and the Chrysler Corporation, by the end of the companies' 10-year contract. 
Chrysler acquired the Jeep line of sport-utility vehicles in its purchase of the American Motors Corporation in the fall of 1987.
