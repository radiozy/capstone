
Ye Xuanping, the popular Governor of Guangdong province in southern China, is stepping down after six years of overseeing the economic transformation of what is now the most prosperous region in this country, the official People's Daily said today.
National leaders have been trying for a couple of years to lure Mr. Ye, a 67-year-old Cantonese who strongly favors economic change, out of Guangdong. 
In March he was named vice chairman of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, a largely ceremonial and advisory post.
