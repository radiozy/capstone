
CBS News reported last night that two of its employees in Beijing, including its correspondent, Richard Roth, were seized by Chinese soldiers yesterday and were being held at an unknown location last night.
Teri Everett, a spokeswoman for the network, said Mr. Roth was on the air by telephone at the time, describing the scene in Tiananmen Square.
CBS, like other broadcasters, had to rely on telephone reports since Chinese authorities cut off video broadcasts from Beijing last week.
She said Mr. Roth, 40 years old, was taken into custody along with a cameraman, Derek Williams, 42, a New Zealander.
Two other crew members, a sound man and a translator, faded into the crowd and avoided arrest, Ms. Everett said. 
There was a report that Mr. Roth might have been beaten by the soldiers, but she said it was not confirmed.
As of 11:30 last night, intense efforts to find the crew were still under way, another CBS News spokesman said.
