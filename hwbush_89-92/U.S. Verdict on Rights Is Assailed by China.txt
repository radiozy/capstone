
Accusing the United States of interfering in its affairs, China today attacked a State Department report that described the Beijing Government as a repressive Government that routinely violates human rights.
In a statement read on an evening television news program, the Chinese Foreign Ministry said the report "distorts and attacks China's domestic situation in the name of human rights."
It called the document "an improper comment" that constituted "interference in China's internal affairs.
The State Department's annual report on human rights, issued on Friday, covered 160 countries but focused on China. 
It said the Beijing Government fell far short of internationally accepted standards on human rights and listed many examples of reported abuses, including torture of detainees and repression of worship outside officially sanctioned religious groups.
The report was issued while Prime Minister Li Peng was in New York for a summit meeting of the United Nations Security Council. 
It was Mr. Li's first visit to the United States since the 1989 crackdown against the democracy movement.
Mr. Li also met with President Bush on Friday, their first encounter since the events of 1989.
