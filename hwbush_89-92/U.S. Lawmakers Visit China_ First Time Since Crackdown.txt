
The first group of lawmakers from the United States to visit since the June 1989 crackdown on pro-democracy demonstrators were greeted today by Prime Minister Li Peng, who urged them to help improve relations with the United States.
The United States Embassy refused to provide details about the activities of the delegation, led by Representative Michael G. Oxley, Republican of Ohio. 
But the visit was the first item on the state-run evening television news, underlining its importance to China.
China has recently won international goodwill by joining in efforts aimed at forcing Iraq to withdraw from Kuwait.
