
The Hewlett-Packard Company entered a joint development agreement to build computer work stations in China.
The joint venture will assemble and market Hewlett-Packard's newest line of work stations, the HP Apollo 9000 Series 400, for the Chinese domestic market.
Hewlett-Packard said it would jointly create and invest in the Huapu Information Technology Company Ltd., which will manufacture the work stations and develop software. 
Financial terms of the agreement were not disclosed. 
The joint venture will employ 70 people, of whom about 40 will work in software development.
