
A Government official convicted of taking bribes was one of four people put to death recently, official reports said today.
In the central Chinese city of Wuhan, the head of the Overseas Chinese Office, Zhang Guorong, was executed for taking $48,263, including $100 in American currency, in bribes, the official China News Service reported. 
It did not say what Mr. Zhang did in return for the money.
The Peasant Daily reported that three more people were executed after being found guilty of stealing and destroying electrical equipment in towns in Hebei province.
