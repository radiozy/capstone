
China has agreed to resume participation in the Fulbright program, but on a smaller scale than in recent years, the United States Embassy said today.
Chinese education officials have agreed to send 16 scholars and students to the United States in the next academic year to do research, and to accept 16 United States professors who will teach in China, said Sheridan W. Bell, a spokesman for the embassy.
He said the embassy was awaiting a formal letter from the Chinese Government to conclude the agreement.
China took part in the exchange program for a decade, with 24 scholars traveling in each direction in recent years. 
But in August China canceled the 1989-90 academic year exchanges in anger over American criticism of Beijing's military crackdown.
