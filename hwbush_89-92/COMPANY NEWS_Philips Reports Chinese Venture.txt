
Philips Electronics N.V. announced the opening of a new plant in China and the acquisition of a 51 percent stake in a state-owned television factory there. 
Philips, Europe's largest consumer electronics company, said in a statement that it wanted to "encourage the development of high-tech products in China" through the transfer of Western expertise and technology.
The factory at Suzhou, west of Shanghai, will produce 14- , 21- and 25-inch TV sets under the name the Philips Consumer Electronics and Manufacturing Company, the statement said.
The plant, in which Philips owns a 51 percent stake, is a joint venture with the Shanghai Number 7 Radio Factory. 
It will employ 500 people.
