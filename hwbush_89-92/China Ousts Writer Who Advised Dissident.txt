
A day after arresting a pro-democracy leader who had returned from exile in the United States, China today expelled an American writer who was his adviser.
The Foreign Ministry issued a two-sentence statement saying Ross G. Terrill, a China expert based in Boston, had been asked to leave immediately because of "actions which are incompatible with his status as a tourist."
Mr. Terrill was an adviser to Shen Tong, a leader of the 1989 pro-democracy protests who was arrested Tuesday as he was preparing to meet with foreign reporters.
Mr. Shen, a 24-year-old graduate student at Boston University, returned about a month ago from the United States. 
He was arrested hours before he was to announce the establishment of a Beijing branch of the Democracy for China Fund, a United States-based group he heads.
Mr. Shen's mother, Li Yixian, said she had no news of him today. 
Two associates of Mr. Shen, Qi Dafeng and Qian Liyun, were arrested with him. 
The authorities have not commented on their whereabouts.
Mr. Terrill, a member of the democracy fund's advisory board, was detained in his hotel room Tuesday after meeting with reporters but was released after the American Embassy intervened.
