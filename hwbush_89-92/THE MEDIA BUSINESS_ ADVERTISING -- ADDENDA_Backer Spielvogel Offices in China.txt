
Backer Spielvogel Bates Worldwide will open three offices in China, in Guangzhou, Shanghai and Beijing, as part of plans announced yesterday to expand its presence in the Asia and Pacific region.
The new offices, which will supplement Backer's existing China service out of Hong Kong, will be named BSB/Saatchi, following the style of names for Backer's recently opened offices in Eastern Europe. 
Services will be made available, as in Eastern Europe, to clients of both Backer and its sibling, Saatchi & Saatchi Worldwide.
