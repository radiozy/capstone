
Two former regional officials were convicted and sentenced to life in prison for accepting bribes and selling Government-owned cigarettes on the black market, a report said today.
Yang Jie, former director and Communist Party secretary of the Tobacco Monopoly Bureau, was convicted of graft and bribery involving more than $17,000, the official New China News Agency said.
Wang Yanju, a market section chief with the bureau, was convicted of graft and bribery involving more than $23,800, the report said. 
The bureau is in the Ningxia Hui autonomous region, in northern China.
The life sentences were imposed on Friday by the regional Supreme People's Court, the press agency said.
