
More than 300 protesters chanted, screamed and cried outside the Chinese Consulate yesterday demanding that the Chinese Government stop its attacks on students in Beijing.
With signs such as ''People's Republic - What a Joke'' and ''Violent Government Must Die,'' the angry protesters grieved for the demonstrators killed by Chinese troops in Beijing.
''It's so horrible,'' said Lea Liang before addressing the crowd outside the consulate at 12th Avenue and 43d Street. 
''I can't believe they used the army - the People's Army - on the people. 
This hurts very much.''
A shouting match erupted at one point among the demonstrators when a young man displayed a Chinese flag on which he had sprayed a Nazi swastika.
''That is what our government has become!'' he screamed.
But Paul Cheng wanted the flag to come down, saying,''the flag is the people's flag, too.''
