
China, which has said it will bar holders of foreign passports from top government jobs in Hong Kong after Beijing resumes sovereignty in 1997, will extend the ban to more posts, a Chinese official said today.
Xiao Waiyan, a Chinese member of the committee charged with drafting the British colony's future constitution, told reporters the list had been extended to cover the director of immigration and the future customs and excise chief.
On Wednesday it was announced that top jobs, including those of chief executive and the leading judges, would be restricted to Chinese citizens who prove they do not have the right to live outside Hong Kong.
