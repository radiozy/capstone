
Easing restrictions on economic ties with China, Taiwan will let its banks deal directly with their Chinese counterparts to ease trade, financial officials said.
The Bureau of Monetary Affairs said it would soon propose a set of rules governing financial contacts.
Under the proposal, the Government will allow the overseas branches or subsidiaries of local banks to deal directly with their Chinese counterparts, Susan Chang, the bureau's deputy director, said.
The Finance Minister, Wang Chien-shien, has called the proposal "a breakthrough" and said it would set an example for financial relations between Taiwan and mainland China.
He said the ministry would agree on details with the central bank before submitting the proposal for Cabinet approval.
