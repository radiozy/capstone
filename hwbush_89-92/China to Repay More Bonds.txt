
China's repayment of loans from its own citizens will reach a peak next year, a Chinese Foreign Ministry official said recently.
The New China News Agency today quoted Zhang Jialun, a deputy director of the Finance Ministry, as saying the state's domestic debt had reached at least 80 billion yuan ($21.6 billion), of which 30 billion must be paid back in 1990.
The 30 billion yuan will be used to repay bonds issued by the Government to its citizens to finance construction projects and the budget deficit.
China issued bonds in the 1950's, stopped for nearly 20 years and then resumed to speed up modernization.
