
PPG Industries has made progress in negotiations for a joint venture with a Chinese company and a Government agency, an executive of the Pittsburgh glass company said.
But the executive added that a recent Japanese news report that PPG had reached an agreement to start the joint venture was ''extremely premature.''
A United States Embassy official in Beijing said PPG had been selected over two other foreign companies in late April to become the sole candidate for a venture to make glass sheets. 
The official said he believed that the venture would be capitalized at more than $30 million.
