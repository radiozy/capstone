
Wang Weixiong, the heavyweight amateur champion of China, and four other amateur titleholders from China arrived in New York on Saturday to begin 15 months of training at Gleason's Gym in Brooklyn in preparation for the 1990 Asian Games and the 1992 Olympics. 
One of the five athletes, Bai Shijie, with 10 years of amateur boxing experience, will act as their coach.
The boxers were met at Kennedy Airport by Lina Hsu, whose company, Le and Le Associates, is sponsoring the athletes, and by Bruce Silverglade and Ira Becker, the co-owners of Gleason's Gym, who will provide training facilities, boxing equipment and a trainer.
Despite 24 hours of travel, an exuberant Wang eagerly experimented with his first words of English. 
''O.K., O.K., O.K.,'' he said excitedly after a midnight tour of Gleason's. 
Wang sported a tattoo of a woman on his chest and an eagle on his left arm. 
''We're very excited,'' he said through an interpreter. 
''The skyline is very beautiful. 
But I'm very interested in boxing only.''
The five will begin training today at Gleason's, and Silverglade plans to take them to the Felt Forum on Thursday evening, when Michael Dokes, the former World Boxing Association heavyweight champion, meets Arturo Diaz.
