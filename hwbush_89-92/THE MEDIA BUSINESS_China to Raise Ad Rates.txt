
China's state-run media plan to raise advertising rates sharply next year, since companies appear ready to pay whatever it takes to promote their products, the Economic Daily has reported. 
China's largest television network, CCTV, will raise its rates to $4,419 for 30 seconds during prime viewing hours, from $1,767. 
The newspaper said advertising spending would increase as Chinese companies, many of them state-owned, took advantage of greater autonomy offered by economic reforms.
