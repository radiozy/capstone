
The world trade body GATT decided today to resume consideration of China's request to join it.
A working group, scheduled to look into the request in July, was canceled after the June protests at Tiananmen Square.
The organization issued a statement then that in light of the situation in China, progress could not be expected on the issue.
Today, however, a group of leading members of the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade decided to end the refusal. 
A GATT spokesman, David Woods, said the organization's member countries wanted ''a clarification of recent developments of economic and trade policies in China.''
He added that the China working group's next meeting would be held Dec. 
12 to 14.
