
The Sara Lee Corporation said it was negotiating its first joint venture in China, a majority interest in a Chinese manufacturer of shampoo and other bath products. 
The deal was mentioned briefly during the company's annual meeting, and little additional information was provided.
John H. Bryan, Sara Lee's chairman and chief executive, said at a news conference after the meeting that since the venture was still being negotiated, he did not want to reveal the name of the Chinese company or the amount of money involved. 
A Sara Lee spokesman, E. Ronald Culp, said the Chinese company had its manufacturing facilities in the Fujian region but he declined to supply other information. 
A move into China would be in keeping with Sara Lee's recent history of expanding internationally in nonfood areas.
