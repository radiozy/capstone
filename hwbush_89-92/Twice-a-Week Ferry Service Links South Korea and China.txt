
South Korea began regular ferry service with China today for the first time since the Korean peninsula was divided in 1945.
A 4,300-ton ferry carrying 130 people left the western port city of Inchon for Weihai on China's Shandong peninsula, the South Korean press agency Yonhap reported.
The boat will leave Inchon twice a week, Yonhap said. 
It will fly a Panamanian flag, because the two countries have no diplomatic relations.
South Korea has strengthened economic and nonpolitical ties with China, a close ally of Communist North Korea.
