
Here is a sampling of events scheduled this weekend in the metropolitan New York area in celebration of the Chinese New Year, the Year of the Goat, which starts today:
 
TODAY
 
"FOLK TRADITION NEW YORK, NEW YORK," an exhibition of photographs and items relating to Chinese folk art, at the Asian American Arts Center, 26 Bowery. 
Exhibition hours, 11 A.M. to 6 P.M. Monday to Friday. 
Admission, $8 ($4 for the elderly). 
Information: (212) 233-2154.
 
TOMORROW
 
"OPEN HOUSE," a daylong celebration with food, crafts demonstrations, lectures and video presentations, at the China Institute in America, 125 East 65th Street, 10 A.M. to 5 P.M. Admission, $10 ($3 for those under 12). 
Information: (212) 744-8181.
 
SUNDAY
 
MUSIC, DANCE AND GAMES, to celebrate the New Year, at the Children's Museum of Manhattan, 212 West 83d Street, 10:30 A.M. to 2:30 P.M. Free with museum admission, $4. 
Information: (212) 721-1223.
 
FESTIVAL, with performances and crafts demonstrations, at the Asian American Arts Center, 26 Bowery, 4 to 6 P.M. Admission, $8 ($4 for the elderly). 
Information: (212) 233-2154.
