
A venture including ARCO and the Chinese Government agreed today to sell natural gas from the South China Sea to a company that would use it to generate electricity in Hong Kong.
The $1.2 billion project, to be China's largest offshore energy development, was announced in Beijing at a ceremony attended by the Chinese Prime Minister, Li Peng.
The natural gas is from the Yacheng 13-1 gas field south of Hainan Island. 
The field, discovered by ARCO in 1983, has an estimated three trillion cubic feet of recoverable natural gas, the oil company said.
Production is expected to begin in 1996. 
About 280 million cubic feet a day will be delivered to Hong Kong through a 475-mile underwater pipeline and 50 million cubic feet a day to Hainan Island through a 60-mile underwater pipeline.
The China National Offshore Oil Corporation owns 51 percent of the project, ARCO 34.3 percent and a unit of the Kuwait Foreign Petroleum Exploration Company 14.7 percent.
The electricity-generating company is owned by the China Light and Power Company and Exxon Energy Ltd.
