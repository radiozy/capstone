
Would-be visitors to an exhibition of avant-garde art yesterday in Beijing being confronted by a locked gate. 
Police closed the exhibition after artists defied a ban on performance art and a sculptor shot her work with a BB gun. 
She was later captured. 
It was not clear if the show would be reopened.
At one point, two carloads of riot policemen drove into the crowd, but left immediately when their cars were surrounded by curious onlookers.
