
A Chinese army officer and his wife defected to South Korea today across the border dividing the two Koreas, the Defense Ministry said.
A ministry spokesman said the officer, Maj. Zuo Xiukai, one of a detachment of Chinese troops monitoring the 1953 Korean War armistice, and his wife crossed the demarcation line from North Korea at Panmunjom.
Wilt Baker, a public affairs officer at the United Nations Command, confirmed that the defection took place.
Major Zuo, 41 years old, is the first Chinese officer reported to have defected to South Korea through Panmunjom since the Korean War.
