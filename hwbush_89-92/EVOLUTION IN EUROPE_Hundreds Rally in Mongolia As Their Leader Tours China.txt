
About 1,000 Mongolians demonstrated today in support of pro-democracy protesters staging a sit-in in the capital, and hunger strikers in other parts of the country fasted through an eighth day.
In China, the Mongolian President, Punsalmaagiyn Ochirbat, continued his visit and headed to Inner Mongolia, a region where three million ethnic Mongolians live under Chinese rule.
As Mr. Ochirbat met Chinese leaders, Ulan Bator's official news agencies described the situation at home as ''chaos'' caused by illegal demonstrations, strikes and hunger strikes against the Communist authorities.
