
Three of China's most outspoken dissidents were arrested on Thursday morning, shortly before they were to hold a news conference to call on the Government to release political prisoners, a Chinese with access to information in the Public Security Bureau said.
The fate of the three men has been a mystery over the last few days.
Two dozen foreign reporters who gathered for the news conference on Thursday discovered that the dissidents had vanished overnight. 
One of them, Hou Dejian, a popular singer, sent a message saying he had some personal business and was canceling the news conference, but nothing has been heard from him or the others since.
All three were arrested early Thursday and remain in custody, the Chinese contact said. 
He said he did not know if they would be charged or if they would be released after the June 4 anniversary of the killings of hundreds of protesters last year. 
The Government has been concerned that the anniversary might prompt unrest, but there have been no signs that this will happen.
Mr. Hou and his two friends - Zhou Duo, a former computer company executive, and Gao Xin, a former editor of a university newspaper - played peripheral roles in the democracy movement last year.
