
Prime Minister Li Peng today indicated a willingness to give foreign companies a role in developing China's most promising oilfield, which has been closed to foreign involvement. 
The official New China News Agency said Mr. Li made the statement at a meeting with the president of the Mitsubishi Corporation of Japan, Shinroku Morohashi. 
Mr. Morohashi mentioned the Tarim Basin in northwestern China, saying his company "is ready to participate" in oil development there. 
Testing has indicated that the basin is rich in oil and natural gas deposits.
