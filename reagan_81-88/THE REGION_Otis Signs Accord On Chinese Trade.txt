
HARTFORD (UPI) - The Otis Elevator Company, the world's largest manufacturer of elevators and escalators, announced yesterday that it had signed a business agreement with the largest elevator company in China.
Otis Elevator Company signs business agreement with largest elevator company in China
The announcement by Otis, a subsidiary of the United Technologies Corporation, said the memorandum of understanding was reached with the Tianjin Elevator Company of Tianjin, China.
The Tianjin company has selected Otis as its representative outside China and has been named to represent Otis in China. 
''Otis and Tianjin will vigorously pursue elevator and escalator sales opportunities in the People's Republic of China and other nations,'' said Francois Jaulin, the president of Otis.
