TOKYO, Sept. 
9 (AP) - A two-month dispute with China and South Korea over Japan's new history textbooks ''has tentatively been settled,'' Foreign Minister Yoshio Sakurauchi told reporters today.
The Deputy Foreign Minister of China, Wu Xueqian, told Ambassador Yasue Katori that the Chinese Government ''understood and appreciated'' Mr. Katori's explanations of Japan's stance on the textbook dispute.
''We will judge whether the Japanese side conscienciously corrects the mistakes in the textbooks by its concrete actions and their effects,'' Mr. Wu was quoted as saying by the official New China News Agency. 
''We reserve our right to comment on this matter.''
