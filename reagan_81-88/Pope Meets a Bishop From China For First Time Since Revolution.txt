
Pope John Paul II met with a bishop from mainland China today for the first time since the Communist Government in Peking forced its Roman Catholics to break relations with the Vatican in 1949, a Vatican spokesman said.
Pope meets a bishop from China for first time since revolution
The bishop was Msgr. Dominic Tang, apostolic administrator of Canton, who spent 22 years in a Chinese prison after being accused of anti-patriotic activities. 
No details of his private conversation with the Pope were made public.
During the Pope's visit to the Philippines last February, he broadcast a fervent greeting to the Chinese Catholics and told them the church ''wants to be, in China as in any other country, the herald of the kingdom of God.''
