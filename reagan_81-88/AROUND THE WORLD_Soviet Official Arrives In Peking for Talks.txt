PEKING, Monday, Oct. 
4 (UPI) -Deputy Foreign Minister Leonid F. Ilyichev of the Soviet Union arrived in Peking today for talks on the prickly relations between the two nations, the first high-level official visit to China by a Moscow envoy in three years.
No other details were released. 
Diplomatic sources said the issues to be discussed include territorial claims made by Peking along the Chinese-Soviet border, the Soviet military occupation of Afghanistan and Moscow's support for the Vietnamese occupation of Cambodia.
The visit comes as Peking tries to distance itself from the appearance of a strategic alliance with the United States and follow a more independent posture.
Diplomatic analysts believe Peking's welcome for Mr. Ilyichev was another effort to reduce tensions along its border so it can concentrate on building its national economy.
