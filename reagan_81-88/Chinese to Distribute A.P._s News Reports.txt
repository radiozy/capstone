
The Associated Press and the New China News Agency agreed today on the first teleprinter distribution of any foreign news agency in China in 34 years.
The A.P. and the official Chinese press agency agreed on distribution of The A.P.'s world service news report to private foreign subscribers through the Chinese agency. 
Chinese law prohibits a foreign news agency from distributing its service directly in China.
