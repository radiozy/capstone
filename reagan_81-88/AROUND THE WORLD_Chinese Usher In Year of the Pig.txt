PEKING, Feb. 
13 (AP) - China welcomed the Year of the Pig today with firecrackers, mass weddings, calls for frugality and reminders not to forget family planning.
Peking television showed crowds in Fujian Province launching two orange rubber rafts toward Taiwan, 100 miles east. 
They stocked the boats with mao-tai liquor, food and fruits and topped them with banners reading ''Wishing You Happy New Year'' and ''Reunification Is Everyone's Sacred Duty.''
In Tian An Men Square in Peking, thousands of lights and red lanterns decorated major buildings. 
Crowds of merrymakers milled in the square.
The holiday is the traditional time for weddings, and China has therefore stepped up its family-planning campaign to remind newlyweds they are permitted to have only one child.
