
The Exxon Corporation said that a subsidiary had abandoned the first exploratory oil well it had drilled in the South China Sea as part of a joint venture with units of the Royal Dutch/Shell Group and the China National Offshore Oil Corporation. 
Exxon said the well, about 165 miles southeast of Zhanjiang in the western part of the Pearl River Mouth Basin, was about 12,690 feet deep.

While a small volume of oil was discovered, Exxon said, it was unlikely that the well could be developed economically. 
While Exxon said it would return to the area for exploration, it said it was moving a drilling rig to a second well, about 215 miles southeast of Guangzhou in the eastern part of the Pearl River Mouth Basin.
