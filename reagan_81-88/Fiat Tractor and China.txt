
Fiat Trattori, the tractor manufacturing unit of Italy's largest private industrial group, Fiat S.p.A., said it had signed a letter of intent to renovate two tractor plants in China. 
Fiat Trattori said Friday that it would renovate plants at Shanghai and at Luoyang under the agreement with China's Ministry of Machine Manufacture. 
The plants will produce Fiat tractors under license, it said.
