
Signs of a boom in Hong Kong's property market set off a rush for shares Friday, pushing the stock market index here to a four-year high.
Brokers said sharp gains by property stocks were a sign of confidence in the territory's future.
Stock market index in Hong Kong reaches four-year high (S)The market index rose 34.59 points to close at 1,560.
Confidence had sagged in the two years that Britain and China were negotiating an accord, signed in December, under which the territory would be returned to China in 12 years. 
''Everything was doom and gloom,'' one Chinese broker said. 
''Now investors think this place is good for another 12 years - if not another 62 years.''
China has promised that Hong Kong can keep its capitalist system for 50 years after 1997.
