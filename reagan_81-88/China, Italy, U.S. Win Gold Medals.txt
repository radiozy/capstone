
Pat Spurgin of the United States shattered the American record and came close to a world mark today as she won the gold medal in the women's air rifle competition.
Li Yuwei won China's second shooting gold, taking the men's running game target.

Luciano Giovanetti won Italy's first gold medal, taking the Olympic men's trap shoot title in a three-man shootout. 
Peru's Francisco Boza won the silver, and Don Carlisle of the United States took the bronze in trap shooting.
''I performed well,'' said Miss Spurgin, who lives in Billings, Mont. 
''I just wanted to come in and shoot well and have a little fun. 
I guess I did both.''
Miss Spurgin, 18 years old, scored a 393 out of 400. 
Italy's Edith Gufler won the silver, and the bronze went to Wu Xiaoxuan of China.
The American record in the event had been 391, while the world record is 395. 
Miss Gufler scored 391 and Miss Wu 389.
The silver in the running game target went to Helmut Bellingrodt of Colombia and the bronze to China's Huang Shiping.
Bellingrodt scored 584, and Huang was tied with West German Uwe Schroder at 581. 
The Chinese shooter won the bronze on the basis of more perfect rounds.
