PEKING, July 15 (Reuters) -Progress has been made in ChineseAmerican talks on nuclear energy, and the two sides have agreed to hold more talks soon, the New China News Agency said today.
Diplomatic sources said the talks had focused on Peking's reluctance to allow United States officials to inspect its atomic installations. 
That reluctance resulted in a ban on United States nuclear exports to China.
The Chinese agency gave no details of the talks, which ended in Washington on Thursday.
