
China entered the plastic money age today when two leading bankers became the first Chinese citizens to own credit cards. 
The chairman of the Bank of China, Bu Ming, and its president, Wang Deyan, were presented with two Mastercards bearing a picture of the Great Wall.

They will able to use the cards at 300 establishments in more than 70 Chinese cities. 
The cards, to be billed in Hong Kong dollars, can also be used worldwide, according to Lam Siubiu, assistant general manager of the Nanyang Credit Card Company. 
Mr. Lam said the bank planned to issue the cards to Chinese companies whose employees go abroad on business.
''This is just a first step,'' he added. 
''It is hard to predict the size of the future market.''
