
Official Says Chinese Will Join Shuttle Crew
WASHINGTON, April 10 (UPI) - A Chinese astronaut is expected to fly on a United States space shuttle mission and conduct joint experiments as early as 1990, a senior Administration official said today.
The official, who asked not to be identified, said the mission would probably be announced on President Reagan's visit to China April 26 to May 2.
He said the Chinese astronaut could fly aboard a shuttle as early as four years from now.
A diplomatic source in Peking said the astronaut would help conduct joint experiments as part of a technological agreement being negotiated between the United States and China.
A report in the April 9 issue of the magazine Aviation Week and Space Technology said Chinese officials had discussed the project with officials from the White House and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration.
