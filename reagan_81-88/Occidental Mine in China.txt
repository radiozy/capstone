
The chairman of the Occidental Petroleum Corporation, Dr. Armand Hammer, will preside Monday at dedication ceremonies at the beginning of development of an open-cut coal mine in central China designed to produce 15.33 million tons of raw coal annually.
On Saturday, Occidental signed a $650 million contract with China for the mine's development.
Dr Armand Hammer, chairman of Occidental Petroleum Corp, will preside at July 1 dedication ceremonies for Pingshuo open-cut coal mine to be developed in Shanxi province, China, joint venture of Occidental, Bank of China Trust and Consultancy Co and China National Coal Development Corp (S)
The Pingshuo mine project is a joint venture owned 25 percent by Occidental; 25 percent by the Bank of China Trust and Consultancy Company, a wholly owned Bank of China subsidiary, and 50 percent by the China National Coal Development Corporation.
The mine in Shanxi province is expected to ship its first coal in September 1987. 
Production will be two-thirds for export.
The $650 million figure does not include infrastructure being built at the site by the Chinese Government, including a town to accommodate 17,000 workers, roads and railway lines. 
Occidental will provide $175 million, increasing to about $200 million with interest, Dr. Hammer said.
