
An overloaded bus traveling without windshield wipers in the rain toppled into a ditch and burst into flames, killing 43 people and injuring 40, an official report said today. 
The People's Daily newspaper said the survivors managed to climb out the rear window, while the rest of the 83 people on board burned to death. 
The bus crashed Wednesday in a rural area of Shaanxi Province in northern China, the paper said.
