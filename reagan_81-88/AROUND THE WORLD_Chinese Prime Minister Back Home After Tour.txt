
Prime Minister Zhao Ziyang arrived back in China today from a 17-day tour of the United States and Canada.
China's state-run television interrupted its regular programs to broadcast live coverage of Mr. Zhao's arrival in Shanghai from Vancouver, Canada.
Mr. Zhao, 64 years old, was the first Chinese prime minister to visit the United States. 
He caught a cold at the end of his trip but looked fit when he stepped off the plane.
He spent Jan. 
7 through Jan. 
16 in the United States, stopping in Hawaii, Williamsburg, Va., Washington, San Francisco and New York City. 
He visited Canada from Jan. 
17-23.
