PEKING, June 20 (AP) - China announced today that Ling Yun, a Deputy Minister of Public Security since 1964, would head a new Ministry of State Security.The Congress, which has little real power, also approved two new Deputy Prime Ministers, a list of 45 Cabinet ministers and completed selection of the new Central Military Commission headed by Deng Xiaoping. 
All of the choices were approved in advance by the Communist Party.
In an interview with the official New China News Agency after his formal selection today, Mr. Ling said spying had increased in China since the Government began inviting more foreigners into the country four years ago as part of its plan to modernize the economy with outside help.
