MELBOURNE, Australia, Tuesday, March 15 (Reuters) - Prime Minister Zhao Ziyang of China was quoted today as having said that he was not satisfied with relations with the United States and had no immediate plans to accept President Reagan's invitation to visit Washington.
Mr. Zhao, whose visit to Australia next month was confirmed today, gave his views in an interview in Peking with an Australian publisher.
In the interview, published in Melbourne Age, Mr. Zhao said: ''You will be well aware that we are not satisfied with the present state of Chinese-United States relations. 
The major obstacle is the Taiwan issue.''
China has called on the United States to end all military aid to Taiwan.
