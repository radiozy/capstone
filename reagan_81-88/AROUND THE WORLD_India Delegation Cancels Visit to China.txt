
A delegation from India's Parliament has canceled a visit to China after a new dispute over disputed territory on the Indian-Chinese border.
Delegation from India's Parliament cancels visit to China after new dispute over disputed territory on Indian-Chinese border
The delegation was to have attended a United Nations-sponsored conference on population and development for Asian members of parliaments.
The Chinese authorities refused to issue a visa for a delegate from the region of Arunachal Pradesh in northeastern India, an area that China claims. 
The Chinese were quoted as saying that granting a visa to T.L. 
Rajkumar would have amounted to recognizing India's sovereignty in the area.
