
''Dragons in the New World: Native Americans and the China Trade,'' an exhibition designed to celebrate one aspect of trade between China and the United States, is on view through Sept. 
30 at the Museum of the American Indian, Broadway and 155th Street. 
Hours, Tuesday through Saturday, are 10 A.M. to 5 P.M. Admission is $2; $1 for students and the elderly. 
Information: 283- 2420.
