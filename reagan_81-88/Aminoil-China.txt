
Aminoil USA Inc. of Houston, a subsidiary of R.J. 
Reynolds Inc., announced its signing of an agreement to buy refined gasoline from the China. 
Under the agreement, Aminoil will import the Chinese gasoline into its Vancouver, Wash., facilities, beginning with a shipment of about 180,000 barrels scheduled early in February. 
An Aminoil spokesman said the company has options on further shipments, but noted that they would depend on market and demand conditions.
