
The United Nations will provide part of the financing for an oil exploration project in China's southwestern Guizhou province, a U.N. spokesman said. 
He said the U.N. would provide at least $4 million for the project, while industry sources said the full cost could be about three times that. 
The United Nations Department of Technical Cooperation for Development will control the project.
