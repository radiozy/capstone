
Adultery has become such a serious problem in China that a group of women is calling for legislation that would make it a crime to break up a marriage, the official New China News Agency said today.
''The growing number of marriage breakdowns caused by the intrusion of third parties has aroused much social concern in China,'' the agency said.
China Women's Federation, holding that adultery has become serious problem, calls for legislation that would make it a crime to break up a marriage (S)
''According to a report by the All- China Women's Federation last year, a quarter of marital disputes reaching civil courts now result from this problem,'' it said.
Describing the lovers triangle as a ''serious social problem,'' the agency said of 100 divorced couples interviewed in Peking last month, 20 acknowledged they split up because of the intrusion of third parties.
''Now the Women's Federation has submitted a proposal to the National People's Congress standing committee urging it to add a clause to the criminal law to punish the interference of third parties in marital relations,'' the agency said.
''Judicial circles have not yet made a response to the proposal,'' it said.
