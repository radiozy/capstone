
The United Technologies Corporation will supply China with 37 industrial gas turbine engines adapted from the workhorse JT8D commercial jet engine in a 10-year deal worth $150 million, the company said.
The company's Pratt & Whitney Group, maker of the JT8D, will work with Turbo Power and Marine Systems Inc. to develop and produce the 30,000-horsepower FT8 gas turbines. 
The power plants will be used for 25 megawatts of electrical generation, mechanical drive for the petroleum and natural gas industries, and marine applications. 
The FT8 runs at nearly 40 percent efficience - more fuel-efficient than current industrial power plants, Frank Bruno, general manager of Turbo Power, said in a statement.
