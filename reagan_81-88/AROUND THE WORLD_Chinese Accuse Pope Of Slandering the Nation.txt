More than 200 Chinese Catholics met in Shanghai and denounced Pope John Paul II, accusing him of ''vicious slanders'' against China, the official New China News Agency reported today.
The protest took place Monday, a day after the Pope celebrated a special two-hour Mass for unity with Chinese Catholics. 
Last week, he called on Catholic churches around the world to pray for ''persecuted Chinese Catholics.''
Chinese church officials condemned that statement.
The Chinese Catholic Church, officially known as the Patriotic Catholic Association, broke with the Vatican after the Communists came to power in 1949. 
It does not acknowledge the authority of the Pope.
