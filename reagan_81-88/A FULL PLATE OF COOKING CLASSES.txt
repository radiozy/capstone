
AS inevitable as the last day of summer is the first day of the fall's cooking school classes. 
In summer, even the best of cooks take a holiday. 
By now they are ready and eager to face a new season of culinary adventures.
In the past few seasons we have noted certain culinary trends - an interest in food processors and a curiosity about the regional aspects of the Chinese kitchen, for example. 
But this autumn's offerings reveal no discernible new passion. 
Instead, one might call them consolidation classes, designed to help cooks deepen their basic skills, broaden their gustatory repertory and solidify their knowledge.
The following is a sampling of cooking schools throughout the state, listed alphabetically, first by town, then by class within a town.
