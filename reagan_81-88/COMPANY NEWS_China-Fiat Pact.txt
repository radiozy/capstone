
The Iveco Company of the Italian Fiat Group and China's Nanking Automobile Plant have signed a contract to produce 60,000 light trucks in China using technology from Iveco, the official New China News Agency said.
The engines and all the other parts of the trucks will be manufactured in China, the agency said. 
The Italian Government has provided $7 million as a donation to the joint project, $110 million in soft-loan terms and $100 million in export credits, the agency said. 
The trucks may be marketed both domestically and abroad, according to the agency. 
Their carrying capacities will range from one to three tons.
