
Armand Hammer, chairman of the Occidental Petroleum Corporation, conferred with Chinese officials today on cooperating in exploiting a Chinese coal mine, the official New China News Agency reported.
The news agency said that Occidental and the China Coal Development Corporation will sign a feasibility study agreement in a few days. 
Mr. Hammer conferred with the Chinese company's chairman on exploiting an open cut coal mine in Shanghi Province, one of the world's biggest, which has an annual output of 15 million tons, the agency added.
