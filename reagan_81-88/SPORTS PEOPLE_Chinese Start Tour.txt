
The reorganized Chinese national soccer team, with Nian Weisi as its new coach, left for Papua, New Guinea, and its first international competition since the team was disbanded last May 19, following the worst soccer riot in China since Communist rule began in 1949. 
The riot began after a 2-1 loss to Hong Kong that eliminated China from World Cup play. 
One Hong Kong player was cut, cars of foreign spectators were damaged and several policemen were injured during the glass-smashing melee after the game.
