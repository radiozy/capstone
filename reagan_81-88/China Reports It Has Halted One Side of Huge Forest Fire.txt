
Firefighters have halted the eastward spread of a huge forest fire that has burned in northeastern China for 10 days and has killed 160 people, the official New Chine News Agency reported today.
As thousands more people were mobilized to fight the flames, the agency said firefighters were also beginning to succeed in battling the fire on its western front.
According to the Beijing-based Weather Forecasting Center, the fire has engulfed at least 2,350 square miles in the Da Hinggan mountains area in Heilongjiang Province, near the border with the Soviet Union.
