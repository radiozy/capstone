
China has agreed to compensate Japan's Mitsubishi industrial group for scrapping a $369 million contract to build a steel rolling mill at Baoshan, near Shanghai, Mitsubishi said today.
China will compensate Japan's Mitsubishi industrial group for scrapping $369 million contract to build steel rolling mill at Baoshan
Mitsubishi would not specify the amount of compensation but trade sources said it would be about $39 million, around half of what the company had sought. 
The compensation was agreed to after seven months of talks between Mitsubishi and Chinese officials.
China initially proposed canceling the order as part of its policy of curtailing foreign industrial contracts while it adjusted its economy. 
It later sought a five-year suspension but Mitsubishi insisted on the cancellation.
Several other claims, from West German and Japanese companies, remain outstanding.
