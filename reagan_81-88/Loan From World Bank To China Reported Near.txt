
The World Bank has completed negotiations with China on a $200 million loan application to improve science and engineering teaching at universities, bank sources say.
Loan from World Bank to China reported near
If approved by the executive board, which is expected to consider the loan next month, it would be the first extended to China since it replaced Taiwan in the bank's ''China seat'' a year ago.
China's entry into the borrowing field poses a potential demand, according to bank experts, of loan applications of up to $2 billion a year.
The present loan is supposed to provide 26 universities with funds to build and equip facilities, improve teaching methods and develop international relations in science and engineering.
