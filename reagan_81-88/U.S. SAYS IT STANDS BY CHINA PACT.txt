WASHINGTON, Feb. 
26 (AP) - The State Department today denied charges from Peking that recent remarks by President Reagan totally disregarded the main principles of the Chinese-American accord on the Taiwan arms issue.
The department's statement was issued in response to the official New China News Agency, which said Friday that Mr. Reagan used an interview with Human Events, a conservative weekly magazine, to play down United States commitments on the arms issue in what the agency called a ''serious retrogression'' in the United States position.
In the interview, Mr. Reagan said the United States ''did not give an inch'' in signing a joint communique with China last Aug. 
17, in which Washington promised ''to reduce gradually its sale of arms to Taiwan leading over a period of time to a final resolution.''
Mr. Reagan said those commitments ''are tied to progress'' in peacefully resolving the dispute over Taiwan.
The State Department said Mr. Reagan had consistently stated that the United States would abide fully with the provisions of the three United States-Chinese communiques issued to date.
