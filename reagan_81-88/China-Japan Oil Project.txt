Chinese-Japanese joint venture is close to starting commercial development of offshore oilfield; joint venture, known as Japan-China Oil Development Corp, is operating in Bohai Gulf, just off northeast China; China holds 51% interest in corporation, and 48 Japanese companies have balance (S)
''Oil flows of commercial value have been reported from eight wells in five oil-bearing formations,'' the agency said. 
''The Chinese and Japanese have agreed that development should begin as soon as possible.''
The joint venture, known as the Japan-China Oil Development Corporation, is operating in the Bohai Gulf, just off northeast China.
China holds a 51 percent interest in the corporation, and 48 Japanese companies have the balance.
