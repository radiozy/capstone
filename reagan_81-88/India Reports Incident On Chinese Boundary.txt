
Chinese troops crossed into Kashmir in May but withdrew within a few hours, the Indian Government said today.
Indian Government reports Chinese troops crossed into Kashmir, in May, but withdrew within a few hours
A spokesman, J. N. Dixit, said the problem ''was sorted out after our people pointed out'' to the Chinese that they had crossed the border. 
Recent published reports said the Chinese had refused to leave and had hoisted their flag. 
The report was denied by China. 
Mr. Dixit declined to elaborate on his brief statement.
An Indian delegation is to visit China before the end of the year for talks on the disputed territory. 
India is demanding the return of 14,500 square miles of territory that it says China seized during their 1962 border war.
