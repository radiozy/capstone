
Soviet press agencies say President Reagan's forthcoming trip to China is intended to achieve military objectives and to promote his image in an election year.
''Reagan intends to use the visit to China to enhance the militarist position of the United States,'' said the Government press agency Tass on Friday, adding that he wanted ''to try to draw China into the sphere of United States interests.''
The Novosti press agency, a feature syndicate, said ''Reagan intends to use his trip to China as a means of repairing the public image of his foreign policy'' in an election year.
