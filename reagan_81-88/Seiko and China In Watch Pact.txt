
The Seiko Group, Japan's largest watchmaker, announced it had reached agreement with China to establish a joint venture company in Hong Kong to assemble watches for worldwide export.
Japanese watchmaker, Seiko Group, reaches agreement with China to establish joint venture company in Hong Kong to assemble watches for worldwide export
A spokesman for K. Hattori & Company, Seiko's marketing arm, said the agreement was reached recently with the China National Light Industry Products Export and Import Corporation. 
Negotiations for the establishment of the joint venture started in the spring of 1981 at China's request, the spokesman said.
The spokesman said the new company, which will employ about 80 workers initially, will begin in April by making movements and cases for about a million timepieces a year.
