
The Occidental Petroleum Company said that it expected to announce contracts over the weekend with China's National Offshore Oil Corporation for oil exporation in two large offshore tracts in the South China Sea.
Occidental, which has been negotiating the contracts as lead partner for a consortium of oil companies, declined to say when it expects to begin drilling operations on the tracts, which cover 1,600 square miles off the China coast.

Several other major international oil companies, including the Atlantic Richfield Company and British Petroleum Ltd., have signed contracts to explore for oil in the South China Sea since the Chinese government began offering oil concessions in the area last year.
