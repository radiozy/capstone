
Ye Jianying Born May 14, 1897, son of wealthy merchant in Guangdong Province . . . Joined Communists in 1927 . . . From 1928 to 1931, studied military science in Moscow . . . Helped plan Long March with Mao Zedong . . . Chief of staff of Eighth Route Army fighting the Japanese . . . In World war II, sent by Mao to Chongqing as liaison officer with United States forces . . . Deputy chief of staff of Communist forces during civil war . . . Briefly served as first Communist Mayor of Peking in 1949 . . . Later Guangdong Province governor . . . Promoted to marshal in 1955 . . . Appointed to Politburo in 1966 on eve of Cultural Revolution . . . Held on throughout that period of turmoil, allied to Zhou Enlai . . . Elected deputy chairman of party in 1973 . . . In 1976, played key role in arrest of Gang of Four . . . Subsequently threw his support to Deng Xiaoping . . . His son, Ye Jianmin, recently appointed governor of Guangdong Province.
 
Deng Yingchao
Born 1904 in Henan Province, daughter of impoverished landlord . . . Her widowed mother worked as a teacher and governess to support the family . . . At school in Tianjin, joined the Awakening Society, a Communist student group founded by Zhou Enlai . . . Went with Zhou to France for study . . . They were married on return to China in 1924 . . . Attended Soviet party congress in 1928 . . . Became highest-ranking woman Communist in 1934 in early party stronghold in Jiangxi Province . . . Ill with tuberculosis, had to be carried on stretcher during Long March of 1934-35 . . . Was helped by Edgar Snow to escape from Japanese-occupied Peking in 1937 . . . Represented Communists in Chongqing in World War II . . . After Communist takeover, leader of women's movement . . . Elected to Central Committee in 1945 and to Politburo in 1978.
Nie Rongzhen
Born 1899 in Sichuan Province, son of a landlord . . . Trained in early 1920's in France, Belgium and Soviet Union . . . Political instructor under Zhou Enlai at Huangpu Military Academy in Canton in 1925-26 . . . Took part in abortive Communist uprisings in Nanchang and Canton in 1927 . . . After three years in Hong Kong, rejoined Communists and served under Lin Biao during Long March . . . Played key role in Communist seizure of North China, 1947 . . . Succeeded Ye Jianying as Mayor of Peking . . . As acting chief of staff in 1950, oversaw Chinese intervention in Korean War . . . Promoted to marshal in 1955 . . . A Deputy Prime Minister from 1956 to 1974 . . . Leading official in science and technology, including nuclear weapons program . . . Appointed to Politburo in 1967.
 
Li Desheng
Born 1916 in Hubei Province . . . Battalion commander at 20, regimental commander at 27 . . . In closing stages of civil war, divisional commander in Second Field Army, in which Deng Xiaoping was political commissar . . . Led 35th Division into battle in Korean War in 1951 . . . Active as army commander in support of Red Guards during early stages of Cultural Revolution . . . Rapid political rise during that period of turmoil . . . In 1969, named alternate member of Politburo . . . Appointed chief political commissar of armed forces in 1970 . . . Removed from Peking in 1974 and appointed commander of Shenyang Military Region . . . Reputation tarnished in 1980-85 by scandals among officers under his command.
 
Wang Dongxing
Born 1916 in Jiangxi Province . . . As a teen-ager served in security corps of Communists' early Jiangxi stronghold . . . Took part in Long March as member of Mao's bodyguard, beginning a close liaison that lasted until Mao's death in 1976 . . . In 1949, appointed head of security department of Central Committee, responsible for top leaders' protection . . . Appointed Deputy Minister of Public Security in 1955, heading regular and security police . . . In 1966, as an early supporter of Cultural Revolution, denounces Liu Shaoqi and Deng Xiaoping . . . Appointed Politburo alternate in 1969 . . . Involved in events leading to disappearance and death of Lin Biao in 1971 . . . Full Politburo member in 1973 . . . As security chief for Chinese leaders, played key role in arrest of Gang of Four in 1976 . . . In 1977, demoted by Deng Xiaoping to vice president of party school . . . Removed from Politburo in 1980, remaining in Central Committee.
