
China launched three satellites with a single rocket today to conduct scientific experiments, the official New China News Agency reported.
 
The press agency, in a two-paragrath dispat ch, said it was the first time that China had sent three satellit es into orbit around theEarth with a single rocket.

It said the satellites were launched to conduct physics experiments, and were continuously sending back scientific data. 
It said all the instruments were functioning normally, but gave no further details.
China launched its first satellite in 1970, and sent at least seven others into orbit in the next eight years. 
Pictures of Chinese astronauts in training have appeared in Shanghai newspapers, but the Government has not announced plans for manned spaced flights.
Delays in China's space program have been expected because of Government belt-tightening in the face of economic problems. 
The Chinese authorities have provided little information about the space, missile and nuclear weapons programs here. 
In 1980, China fired a missile 6,250 miles to a site in the South Pacific.
