
A radio station will start broadcasting Russian language lessons in August, the first Russian program broadcast to the public since relations between Peking and Moscow deteriorated 25 years ago, an official report said today.
Radio station in China will start braodcasting Russian language lessons (S)
The Government-run magazine Peking Review quoted officials of the Peking People's Broadcasting Station as saying the program was a step toward broadening foreign-language education.
''The Soviet Union is China's largest neighbor, and Russian is one of the languages used in the United Nations,'' Zhang Dazhen, deputy director of the station's public education department, was quoted as saying.
Russian will be the fourth foreign language to be taught on Chinese radio and television. 
English, Japanese and French lessons have been broadcast since the early 1970's.
