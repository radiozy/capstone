
China said today that it was considering applying soon to join the International Atomic Energy Agency, after a visit to Peking by the agency's Director General, Hans Blix.
A Foreign Ministry spokesman said Mr. Blix's discussions with Deputy Foreign Minister Qian Qichen last week on China's possible membership had been fruitful.
''Both sides expressed satisfaction that some issues had been clarified and mutual understanding enhanced,'' he said. 
''Both sides have agreed to continue their consultations. 
The Chinese side is considering an early application for membership of the agency.''
Peking's joining the agency, which is devoted to the peaceful use of nuclear energy, has been a possibility since 1971 when the Vienna-based body expelled the Government of Taiwan.
China announced last month that it was ready to take up the seat in the agency.
