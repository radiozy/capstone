
The People-to-People Sports Committee, the nonprofit organization that shipped the Brooklyn College soccer team to Nepal not long ago, announced yesterday that the C. W. Post baseball squad would tour Peking and Tianjin in a 10-day, five-game series against university teams there. 
The Chinese seem to believe that baseball stands a good shot at becoming an official Olympic sport in 1988, and, given their inexperience at it, they want to start learning a thing or two.
The Post players leave May 31, but first they have a thing or two to learn themselves. 
Yesterday they were having some problems at the plate: At a Chinese restaurant on the East Side, where the tour was announced, most of them had to be shown how to handle the chopsticks.
