
Scores of Peking residents have been hospitalized this summer because of food poisoning, mainly from spoiled meat.
Scores of Peking, China, residents are hospitalized because of food poisoning, mainly from spoiled meat; Epidemic Prevention and Control Department traces most of 17 major outbreaks in May to meat, especially pork (S)
The Peking Epidemic Prevention and Control Department traced most of 17 major outbreaks last month to meat, especially pork. 
The city called on residents and butcher shops to refrigerate meat or to limit exposure to hot summer temperatures to no more than eight hours.
Most Chinese homes lack refrigerators and butcher shops usually display meat in unrefrigerated cases.
