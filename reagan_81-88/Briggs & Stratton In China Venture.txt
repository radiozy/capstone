
The Briggs & Stratton Corporation has announced a joint venture to build small engines in China with a privately owned Chinese company.
The joint venture, with Chongquing Puling Machinery Works, will produce 10- and 16-horsepower gasoline engines in Chongquing, a city in southwestern China.
George A. Senn, executive vice president of Briggs, said the cast-iron engines would be used for various industrial and commercial purposes. 
He said Briggs & Stratton was the first United States small engine company to engage in a joint venture with the Chinese. 
The agreement was reached after 10 months of negotiations with Chinese officials.
