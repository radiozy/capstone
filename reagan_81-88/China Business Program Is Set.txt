
The Columbia Business School is cooperating with the University of International Business and Economics in Beijing to establish an executive program on joint-ventures management in China.
The program, announced this week by Columbia, is aimed at teaching Chinese business managers some American management methods and helping American executives understand how business works in China, particularly when joint ventures are involved.
''The People's Republic is beginning to sustain one of the highest economic growth rates in the world,'' said E. Kirby Warren, acting dean of the business school. 
''There will be a great need for well-trained managers and executives with responsibility for the management of joint ventures in China.''
The program is expected to begin this fall.
Columbia's part of the effort will be coordinated by the school's new Center for Chinese Business Studies and will be financed in part by a grant from E. I. du Pont de Nemours & Company. 
The China International Trust and Investment Corporation will pay for China's part of the costs.
