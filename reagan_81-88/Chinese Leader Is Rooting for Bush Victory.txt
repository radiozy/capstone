
Deng Xiaoping, China's senior leader, told American officials today that he hoped Vice President Bush would be the next President of the United States.
The apparent endorsement of Mr. Bush, who headed the United States liaison office in Beijing in 1974 and 1975, came in a meeting with Defense Secretary Frank C. Carlucci.
''Vice President Bush used to be Lord's predecessor so we had a lot of contact with him at the time,'' Mr. Deng said, referring to Winston Lord, the current United States Ambassador to China, in opening remarks at the meeting. 
''I hope he'll win the election.''
Mr. Carlucci did not comment on the 84-year-old Chinese leader's preference for the Republican nominee over his Democratic opponent, Gov. Michael S. Dukakis of Massachusetts.
China's official news service files occasional reports on the Presidential campaign, but has not taken sides or commented on how the outcome might affect China.
