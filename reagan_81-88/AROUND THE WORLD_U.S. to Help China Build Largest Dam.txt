
The United States and China have signed an agreement under which the Interior Department will help China design the world's largest hydroelectric dam, the department said today.
Experts from the department's reclamation bureau and several private American companies would visit China in late September to help begin designing the Three Gorges project on the Yangtze River, the department said.
US Interior Department reports US and China have signed agreement under which department will help China design world's largest hydroelectric dam, Three Gorges project on Yangtze River; experts from department's reclamation bureau and several private US companies will visit China in September to begin project's design (S)
China will pay all the consultants' costs.
Reclamation bureau engineers helped in early design work on the Three Gorges dam in the 1940's, but the project was shelved in 1947 during the civil war that ended with the Communist victory.
The dam is expected to be able to generate 13 million kilowatts of electricity, more than the 12.6 million kilowatts projected for the Iaipu dam being built by Brazil and Paraguay.
