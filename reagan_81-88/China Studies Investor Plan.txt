
A draft law intended to encourage foreign investment in China was considered today by the National People's Congress, the official New China News Agency said.
The National People's Congress has little power, adopting whatever measures the Communist Party presents to it, but its role as an advisory body has expanded recently.
The proposed law would protect foreigners' interests and expand international economic cooperation and technological exchanges, the press agency quoted Prime Minister Zhao Ziyang as saying. 
The report gave no details of the measure.
