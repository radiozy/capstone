
Torrents of water from the Yangtze River swept past China's largest dam today without damaging it and surged toward an area that suffered disastrous floods last year.
Upstream in the province of Sichuan, where floods from last week's rains have killed more than 3,000 people and have injured more than 50,000, rescue workers struggled to care for the 500,000 who have been left homeless as the authorities rushed in relief supplies.
Flood waters with 20-foot-high waves swept by the Gezhouba Dam, in the province of Hubei, but the $2 billion project was undamaged, the New China News Agency said.
