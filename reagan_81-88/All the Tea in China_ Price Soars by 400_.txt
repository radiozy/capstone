
The price of tea in China has soared up to 400 percent this year because an abnormally cold and rainy spring seriously cut production, the Government reported today.
The official New China News Agency said tea bushes in Zhejiang and Anhui Provinces, the country's main producing regions, sprouted late because of the weather, and picking was delayed.
