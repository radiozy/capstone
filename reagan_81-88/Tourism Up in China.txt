
China earned more than $800 million from foreign tourists last year, an increase of at least $20 million over 1981, the New China News Agency reported today. 
It said that the number of tourists in China increased last year despite the world economic recession, which reduced tourism in other countries. 
No figure was given for the total number of foreign visitors to China.
