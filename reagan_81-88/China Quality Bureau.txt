
China has set up a new state bureau to oversee efforts to reduce the number of shoddy, substandard goods on the market and make Chinese products more competitive on world markets, an official daily said today.
In announcing creation of the State Bureau of Technical Supervision, the state councilor, Song Jian, said substandard products remain a serious problem, The China Daily said. 
It said a spot-check of industrial products earlier this year found that only 20 percent fully satisfied national standards. 
The paper said the new bureau would strengthen supervision over technology and establish an authoritative system of standardization and measurement.
