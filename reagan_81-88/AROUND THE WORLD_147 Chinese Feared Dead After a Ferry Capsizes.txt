
A ferry carrying at least 232 people to a popular south China tourist area capsized today during an overnight thunderstorm, leaving at least 147 people missing and feared dead, officials said.
Officials in Guangdong Province said the ferry was on its regular run from Canton, the province capital, to Zhaoqing, 46 miles to the west, when winds heeled it sharply to port and capsized it at 2:40 A.M.
Most of the passengers were believed to have been asleep and had little time to grab life preservers before the boat turned over in a river called the Shan Shui.
