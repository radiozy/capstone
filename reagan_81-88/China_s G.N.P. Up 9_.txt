
China's gross national product will exceed $270 billion this year, up 9 percent from 1986, the official New China News Agency said. 
Zheng Jiaheng, deputy director of the State Statistical Bureau, said in an interview earlier this week that industrial output increased 14.5 percent while agricultural output was up 4 percent. 
China's G.N.P. 
- a broad measure of the value of all goods and services produced in the country - is the eighth largest in the world, although the country remains one of the world's poorest nations because of its population of more than one billion people.
