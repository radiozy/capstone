
A Texan was stabbed to death last week during a robbery on a train bound from Beijing to Canton, according to the American Embassy and the Chinese police. 
Two men have been arrested.
The police identified the victim as Qi Jihua, 61 years old, whose home town was thought to be San Angelo, about 250 miles southwest of Dallas. 
Mr. Qi was said to have married a Chinese woman in March.
It was the first time in more than 30 years that an American citizen has been murdered in China, according to Andrew C. Koss, a press secretary at the embassy.
Two Chinese men traveling in the same first-class compartment stabbed Mr. Qi and another passenger and stole their money.
The suspects were said to have been arrested a few days later with a large amount of money, including American currency.
