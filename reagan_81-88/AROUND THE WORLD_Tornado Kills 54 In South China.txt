HONG KONG, April 13 (Reuters) - A tornado killed 54 people and seriously injured 163 when it raged through two south China communes Monday, the New China News Agency said today.
It said 90 buildings collapsed in the storm that hit Jianyang county of Fujian Province. 
The agency quoted county authorities as saying the casualties included students and teachers in local schools.

CALCUTTA, India, April 13 (Reuters) - At least 50 people were killed and 1,500 injured when a cyclone hit 21 villages in a coastal district of India near here, officials said today.
More than 6,000 people were made homeless by the storm Tuesday night.
