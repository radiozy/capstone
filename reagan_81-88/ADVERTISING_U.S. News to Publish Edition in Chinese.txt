
U.S. News & World Report said yesterday that it would publish a Chinese-language version of the newsweekly in conjunction with United Daily News in Taiwan, which will translate the magazine's contents and print the Chinese edition.
U.S. News said the agreement would make it the first American news magazine with a Chinese-language edition. 
For the first issue, dated Sept. 
22, 1986, United Daily News will print 40,000 copies. 
It will be printed in Taipei and distributed there and in Hong Kong, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, the Philippines, Indonesia, Paris, London, Amsterdam, New York, Dallas and elsewhere.
