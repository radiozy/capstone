
Israel has signed a secret deal to supply advanced missile technology to China after sending an arms delegation to Beijing, The Sunday Times reported.
The newspaper reported other signs that Israel has been helping China to develop a new fighter plane using technology derived from the canceled Lavi warplane.
The Israeli Defense Minister, Yitzhak Rabin, denied both elements of the Sunday Times report today.
The newspaper said the Israeli-Chinese deal, reached in November, called for supplying China with technology for advanced trajectory-corrected missiles of unspecified designation, laser-guided armor-piercing warheads and artillery shells. 
The paper said the deal could lead to new Israeli arms sales to China worth hundreds of millions of dollars.
The Sunday Times report coincided with diplomatic concern in the West over Saudi Arabia's purchase of Chinese missiles. 
The United States has warned Israel not to attack the Saudi missiles.
Israel and China do not have diplomatic relations but Western intelligence experts have been widely quoted as saying Israel has established important military ties with China since 1980.
