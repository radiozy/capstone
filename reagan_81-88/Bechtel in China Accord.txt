
The Bechtel Group Inc., an engineering company based in San Francisco, signed a $3 million joint venture agreement today with the state- owned China National Coal Development Corporation. 
The New China News Agency said the new company, China American International Engineering Inc., would work on coal mines, pipelines, engineering, energy and communications projects.
