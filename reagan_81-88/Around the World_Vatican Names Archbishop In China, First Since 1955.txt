
The Vatican today named Msgr. Dominic Tang Yiming, who was freed last year after 22 years in a Communist prison, as its first archbishop in China since 1955.
Vatican names Msgr Dominic Tang Yi-ming as its first archbishop in China since 1955He will serve as Archbishop of Canton.
Vatican sources said that Monsignor Tang, who is 73 years old, would probably not have been named without the approval of Chinese authorities.
The appointment was seen as a sign of improving ties between the Roman Catholic Church and China's Communist Government. 
Italian newspapers describe Monsignor Tang as potentially the key liaison in the Vatican's efforts to improve relations with Peking. 
The Holy See maintains diplomatic ties with Taiwan, which has been a stumbling block to normalization of relations with Peking.
Monsignor Tang was released from prison June 9, 1980, where he had been held as an accused counterrevolutionary, and was named Bishop of Canton by the pro-Government Peking Patriotic Catholic Association.
