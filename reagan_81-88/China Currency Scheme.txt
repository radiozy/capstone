
A group of ''entrepreneurs'' in central China made a fortune by buying $480,000 at the official rate and then selling the currency on the black market for nearly a 100 percent profit, a newspaper said. 
The official Hubei provincial daily said the four participants had set up a front company and obtained approval to import machinery valued at $480,000. 
They were thus permitted to buy the currency at official rates.
