
A group of scientists has recommended that China establish a body to oversee technology imports and control the approval of key state projects involving imports, the official New China News Agency reported.
The scientists made the proposal today to the science and technology section of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference, an advisory body. 
Sometimes the same type of equipment has been imported by several plants simultaneously, the report said. 
It said the problems had occurred because of lack of overall state control and neglect of experts' opinions.
