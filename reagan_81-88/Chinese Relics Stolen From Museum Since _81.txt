
Thieves have stolen more than 115 cultural relics from a museum in northeastern China since 1981 and local authorities blamed lax security, a Chinese newspaper reported today.
In one theft, two Buddhist relics -a silver bottle and a silver box -were taken from a display case at the Heilongjiang provincial museum in broad daylight late last year, said the newspaper, the Guangming Daily.
No value was given for any of the stolen pieces.
Only three guards were posted at the museum until April, although it was supposed to have 14 guards, the newspaper quoted officials as saying. 
There were no guards at night for years, and security alarms intended for a warehouse were installed instead in an office, the report said.
