
An Indian Parliament delegation, which last week canceled a proposed visit to China in a controversy over disputed territory, will now travel to Peking under a mutually acceptable formula.
Satpal Mittal, the leader of the delegation, said today that the Chinese had agreed to a suggestion to grant visas to all 20 members of his group, which will be attending a population conference in Peking.

Rajkumar, a legislator from the northeastern territory of Arunachal Pradesh, whose original presence touched off Chinese objections since China has staked its claim to the frontier area.
The Indian delegation said last week that it would boycott the conference because the Chinese had refused to issue a visa to Mr. Rajkumar. However, Chinese officials later agreed to give him an entry visa, not on his passport but on a separate sheet of paper. 
Mr. Mittal then suggested the same formula for the rest of the delelation and the Chinese consented.
