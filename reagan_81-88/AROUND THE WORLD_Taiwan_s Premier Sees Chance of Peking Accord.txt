''If the political, economic, social and cultural gaps between the Chinese mainland and free China continue to narrow, the conditions for peaceful reunification can gradually mature,'' Mr. Sun said at a reception.
TAIPEI, Taiwan, June 10 (UPI) -Prime Minister Sun Yun-suan of Taiwan said today that reunification with China could become possible as differences between the two sides narrow.''The obstacles to reunification will be reduced naturally with the passage of time.''
Government spokesmen said Mr. Sun's remarks did not mean Taiwan was altering its refusal to begin talks with Peking. 
In the past, high-ranking officials of the Nationalist Chinese Government have publicly discussed the reunification issue only after first specifying that Peking must renounce Communism.
