
Sometime in June Ogilvy & Mather Worldwide expects to open an office in Peking, becoming the first American agency with an outpost in the Chinese capital.
It will be a subsidiary of Ogilvy & Mather China that operates in Hong Kong and specializes in advertising in China itself.
The clients involved are General Foods, American Express, Martell Cognac, Nike, Parker Pens, British American Tobacco, Polariod and Boeing. 
As it has since 1980, Ogilvy will continue to work with the state-owned Beijing Advertising Corporation.
