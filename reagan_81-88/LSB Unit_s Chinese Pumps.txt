
LSB Industries Inc. said yesterday that the Hercules Energy Corporation, its newly established oilfield service division, had started marketing in the United States a full line of pumps manufactured in China. 
LSB, which makes air-conditioning and heating equipment and markets machine tool products, said it had been involved in the machine tool business with China for about seven years.
