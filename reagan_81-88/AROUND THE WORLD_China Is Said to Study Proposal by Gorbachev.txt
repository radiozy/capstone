
Deng Xiaoping, China's top leader, said today that a speech by Mikhail S. Gorbachev calling for improved Chinese-Soviet ties contained positive elements but that China needed to study it carefully, Japanese sources said.
It was the first reported high-level Chinese reaction to the Soviet leader's speech, which Western diplomats say offered several important concessions aimed at improving relations.
The sources said Susumu Nikaido, senior adviser to Japan's ruling Liberal Democratic Party, met today with Mr. Deng and the Chinese Communist Party leader, Hu Yaobang, at the northern Chinese resort of Beidaihe.
