WASHINGTON, June 3 (AP) - The Pentagon says that it is reviewing its regulations on the transfer of technology in line with President Reagan's directive calling for a more liberal approach to trade with China.
Asked whether the thrust of the Reagan directive called for more liberal trade with China, Mr. Welles replied, ''That is my understanding.''
Defense Secretary Caspar W. Weinberger and other senior Pentagon officials of this and previous administrations have generally favored a tight curb on the sale or provision of military-related technology to China or any other Communist country. 
Commerce Secretary Malcolm Baldrige visited Peking last month and said there that the Reagan Administration would prepare new guidelines within 60 days for speeding decisions on sales of high technology to China, including equipment with possible military applications.
