
China, which is just beginning to discover advertising, has already decided to regulate it. 
How's that for learning fast?
According to Business Asia, a newsletter published by the Business International Corporation, China's State Council recently passed legislation that will, among other things, require that indiginous agencies to be licensed by the state and that food and medicine advertising be certified by the public health authorities.
Finally - and this may be going too far - the rules forbid advertising that exaggerates and covers up defects.
