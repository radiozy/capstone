
Governor Kean said today that he will leave Sept. 
7 for a 17-day trip to China and South Korea to promote business for New Jersey companies and foreign investment in the state. 
He will visit Hong Kong, Hangzhou and Peking in China, and Seoul, South Korea, before returning on Sept. 
24.
''New Jersey's economic growth has been aided substantially by foreign investment and the jobs that investment creates, as well as by our growing export activities,'' Mr. Kean said.
The Governor is scheduled to meet with business and political leaders, including Zhang Wen Jin, former Chinese Ambassador to the United States and now chairman of the Chinese People's Association for Friendship with Foreign Countries. 
He will also meet with United States Ambassador Winston Lord in China, and with the chief executive officers of Samsung, Lucky-Goldstar, Hyundai and Daewoo International when he reaches Korea.
