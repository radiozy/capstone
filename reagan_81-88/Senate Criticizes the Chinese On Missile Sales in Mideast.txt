
The Senate today approved $15 billion for the State, Justice and Commerce Departments after calling on the Reagan Administration to reassess relations with China if Beijing does not stop selling missiles in the Middle East.
The bill, approved by a 80-16 vote, now goes to conference with the House.
Acting on amendments, the Senate adopted, 97-0, a proposal by the Senate Republican leader, Bob Dole of Kansas, declaring: ''If these sales and policies are not discontinued, the United States should reassess its relations with the People's Republic of China and re-examine agreements or contemplated agreements providing for arms and technology transfers to the People's Republic of China.''
