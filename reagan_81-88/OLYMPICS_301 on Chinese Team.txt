
China will send 301 athletes, its largest delegation ever, to the Seoul Olympics and will compete in 20 of the 23 sports, the official Xinhua News Agency said today.
China sent 225 athletes to the 1984 Games in Los Angeles, the first Olympics in which it participated in more than three decades, and won 32 medals, 15 of them gold. 
The team was announced at a joint meeting of the All-China Sports Federation and executive committee of the Chinese National Olympic Committee. 
Accompanying the athletes will be 142 officials.
