
The Netherlands, under pressure from China, said it would reconsider its decision to export two submarines to Taiwan, a Foreign Ministry official said today.
The official said that the Peking regime had threatened to reduce relations with the Netherlands to the lowest possible level if the submarines and a variety of nuclear power station components were exported to Taiwan.
Netherlands reconsiders its decision to export two submarines to Taiwan following warning from Peking
''The Chinese Government has pointed out that it regards the export order as a rejection of the 1972 communique in which the Netherlands recognized that Taiwan was a province of China,'' he said. 
''That communique was the basis for full diplomatic relations between the two countries.''
The official said that the Dutch Ambassador in Peking, Johannes Kneppelhout, had been summoned to the Chinese Foreign Ministry last week to receive the warning and a Chinese representative had been received at the Foreign Minister here at the same time.
