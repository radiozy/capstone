
Huang Zhicheng, a former member of the Taiwan air force who defected to China in a United States-built F-5 jet 11 days ago, today joined the Chinese air force.
The chief of the air force, Zhang Tingfa, personally welcomed the former Taiwan major as a new recruit in Peking. 
Mr. Zhang told Mr. Huang that he was the 90th member of the Nationalist forces to defect to China by plane since the Communist victory in 1949 and said that the defectors had brought 40 aircraft with them.
