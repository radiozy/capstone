HONG KONG, Monday, Aug. 
30 (UPI) - China has convicted eight supporters of the so-called Gang of Four in Shanghai, a pro-Peking newspaper here reported today.
The newspaper, Wen Wei Po, said the officials were given up to 18 years in prison for joining the ''Jiang Qing clique'' and trying to overthrow the Government. 
Miss Jiang, Mao's widow, was the leader of the ''Gang of Four'' radicals who are imprisoned for actions taken in the Cultural Revolution.
Announcement of the convictions represented a show of strength before the 12th Communist Party congress, which begins Wednesday in what is viewed as a final consolidation of the policies of Deng Xiaoping, China's paramount leader.
