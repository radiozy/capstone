PEKING, May 23 (Reuters) -Japan has made $1 billion in loans to China under a five-year agreement signed in 1979, the New China News Agency said today.
The funds are being used partly to extend China's biggest coal port at Qinhuangdao and to build the first phase of a steel mill near Shanghai.
The first round of loans - expected to total about $1.5 billion by the end of the five years - will expire at the end of this year. 
The two countries have begun negotiations on the second round, which Yao Yilin, a Deputy Prime Minister, said last month would provide about $2 billion more.
