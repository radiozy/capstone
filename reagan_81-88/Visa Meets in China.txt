
Visa International Inc.'s board met today in China, where capitalist conveniences like credit cards were unheard of until a few years ago. 
The official New China News Agency quoted Bu Ming, director of the Bank of China, as saying the Peking meeting would promote business cooperation between the state-run bank and Visa, which is one of the world's largest credit card systems, with about 104 million card holders. 
Visa cards have been used in China since 1981, and now 70 branches and sub-branches of the Bank of China and 300 hotels and businesses honor the credit cards.
