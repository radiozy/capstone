
Deng Xiaoping, the Chinese leader, was quoted today as having said that the deployment of United States missiles in Western Europe was inevitable.
Gaston Thorn, president of the European Community Commission, said Mr. Deng had told him, ''It is inevitable that the Americans will deploy missiles in Europe.''
