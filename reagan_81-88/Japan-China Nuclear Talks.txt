
Japan and China opened the way today for the first sale of a Japanese-built nuclear power plant component to China, a Foreign Ministry official said.
After four days of talks on an overall nuclear cooperation agreement, the two nations reached partial agreement on terms for the sale of a pressure vessel component to China's first commercial nuclear power plant, said the official, who declined to be quoted by name.
Japan and China open way for first sale of Japanese-built nuclear power plant component to China; nations reach partial agreement on terms for sale after four days of talks on overall nuclear cooperation agreement; Mitsubishi Heavy Industries is competing with West German company to provide pressure vessel component for China's first commercial nuclear power plant (S)The nuclear power plant is under construction in central China, the official said.
He said Mitsubishi Heavy Industries was competing with a West German company for a contract to build and export the component, worth about $4.3 million, to China.
If Mitsubishi wins the contract, it will be the first Japanese export of a nuclear power plant component to China, a market that one equipment maker described as having ''great potential'' for Japanese makers.
The official said further talks were necessary before a long-term overall agreement could become final.
He said China and Japan reached agreement on the sale after ''China committed itself to a peaceful utilization of nuclear energy'' and agreed to allow Japan to send scientists and technicians to the power plant upon request.
