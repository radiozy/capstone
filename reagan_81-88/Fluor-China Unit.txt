
The Fluor Corporation, the giant engineering company, said it had begun a joint operating venture with China that calls for the building of three large petrochemical plants in the Shanghai area as its first project. 
The venture agreement, Fluor's first such contract in China, was formalized in a signing ceremony in China this week. 
The new company will employ from 250 to 300 Chinese on the Shanghai projects, and up to 40 Fluor engineers, according to Thomas A. Howell, a senior vice president for Fluor.
