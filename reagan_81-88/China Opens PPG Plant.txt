
Chinese officials have formally opened a $100 million glass factory here, praising the American-Chinese project as an example of their nation's success in opening its doors.
The Guangdong Float Glass Company, featuring technology by Pittsburgh-based PPG Industries, is one of the largest and most sophisticated foreign projects that China has attracted since it began to modernize its economy in 1979.
PPG Industries owns 35 percent of the factory.
The general manager, Robert Niederberger, said, ''The investment has gone generally very well, much better than we expected.''
He added that the enterprise could be profitable as early as next year.
The factory's 350 Chinese employees work under American managers and advisers, but Mr. Niederberger said that management eventually would be entirely Chinese.
