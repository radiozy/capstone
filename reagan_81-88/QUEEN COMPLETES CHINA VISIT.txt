
Queen Elizabeth sailed from China today, ending a weeklong visit that the Peking Government called a success despite controversy over the Duke of Edinburgh's description of the capital as ''ugly'' and the Chinese people as having ''slitty'' eyes.
The Queen, the first British monarch to visit China, and her husband left Canton's port of Huangpu in darkness tonight aboard the royal yacht Britannia.
Before their departure, the royal couple gave a reception attended by Foreign Minister Wu Xueqian and Gov. Ye Xuanping of Guangdong Province. 
Officials had earlier feared the Britannia would be unable to sail because of Typhoon Ellen, which brushed past the area today.
The royal couple are expected to arrive in Hong Kong Tuesday for a two-day visit.
''The queen and prince have stated on many occasions that they have had a very good time in China,'' a a Chinese Foreign Ministry official said when asked if Peking had a comment on the Duke's remarks. 
''As for the remarks you mentioned, I was not present on the occasion, so I did not hear them.''
