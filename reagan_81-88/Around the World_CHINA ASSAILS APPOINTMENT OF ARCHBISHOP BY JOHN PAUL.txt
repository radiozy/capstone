China's Roman Catholic Church says Pope John Paul II's appointment of Archbishop constitutes interference in its sovereign affairsDominic Tang as Archbishop of the southern province of Guangdong constituted interference in its sovereign affairs.
Bishop Michael Yang Gaojian, spokesman for the Chinese Patriotic Catholic Association and its Bishops College, said in a statement: ''The Holy See's move rudely interferes in the sovereign affairs of the Chinese church. 
This cannot be tolerated.''
Bishop Tang, 73 years old, was freed in Canton a year ago after 22 years in a Communist jail. 
He was then reappointed bishop by the Chinese church.
