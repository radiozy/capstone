
Ivan V. Arkhipov, a Soviet First Deputy Prime Minister, will go to China on May 10 for a 10-day visit, the Soviet press agency Tass said today.
Soviet Union reports First Deputy Prime Min Ivan V Arkhipov plans to travel to China on May 10 for 10-day visit; trip will be first by high-ranking Soviet official to China in 15 years (S)
The trip will be the first by a high- ranking Kremlin official to China in 15 years. 
Tass said Mr. Arkhipov would travel to Peking at the invitation of the Chinese Government.
The Chinese have said the talks with Mr. Arkhipov will focus on trade. 
In mid-February, China and the Soviet Union signed an agreement to increase trade in 1984 by 60 percent to $1.12 billion. 
Cultural, sports and tourist exchanges are also expected to increase.
