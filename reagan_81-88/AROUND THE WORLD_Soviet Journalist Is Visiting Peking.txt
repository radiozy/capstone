PEKING, Feb. 
17 (AP) - A Soviet political commentator is visiting Peking, and foreign diplomats here say he may be involved in an effort to evaluate the recent trip to China of Secretary of State George P. Shultz and sound out Chinese intentions for the next round of Chinese-Soviet talks scheduled for early March in Moscow.
The commentator, Aleksandr Bovin, a top commentator of the Government newspaper Izvestia, is the first high-ranking Soviet journalist to visit China since the rupture between the two countries in the 1960's.
Mr. Bovin, who arrived Feb. 
9, is on a private visit as the guest of the Soviet Ambassador to Peking. 
He has called on the information department of the Chinese Foreign Ministry and other organizations.
