
Gen. John W. Vessey Jr., chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, has accepted an invitation to visit China early next year. 
Defense Secretary Caspar W. Weinberger was in Peking in 1983. 
General Vessey's trip is believed to be the first by a chairman of the Joint Chiefs to China.
