The Eastman Kodak Company said it had entered into a cooperative agreement with China to assemble slide projectors at a plant in Shanghai.The plant, scheduled to begin production late this year, will assemble carousel projectors similar to the company's Carousel 4400.
A Kodak spokesman said the projectors would be sold exclusively in China, mostly to educational institutions, and that Kodak was making no capital investment in the venture.
''We see it as an opportunity to enter a promising market and expand our knowledge of its workings,'' said Henry J. Kaska, public information director. 
Kodak already sells amateur photographic film in China, as well as developing paper and other products.
