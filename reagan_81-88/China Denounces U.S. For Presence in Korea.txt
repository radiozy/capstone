TOKYO, June 21 (UPI) - Defense Minister Geng Biao of China demanded today that the United States pull out of South Korea and said China and North Korea would share ''life and death in the same boat'' no matter what the future holds, the official North Korean press agency reported today.
''The United States must immediately withdraw its troops and military equipment from South Korea,'' the report quoted Mr. Geng as saying.
He said the United States presence in South Korea is a big hurdle in the way of dialogue and reunification between South and North Korea.
''No matter what storm and change may come in the world in the future, the Chinese people and army will as ever unite with the Korean people and army, sharing life and death with them in the same boat,'' Mr. Geng said.
