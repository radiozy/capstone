
United States exports to China, hurt by sharply declining agricultural sales, dropped 26 percent in the first quarter of 1983, and the United States trade surplus plunged 61 percent from a year ago, the United States Embassy said today. 
The embassy said exports totaled $668.6 million, compared with $905.2 million in January-March 1982, while imports from China fell from $556.6 million, to $534.3 million. 
The surplus dropped from $348.6 million, to $134.3 million, the embassy said.
