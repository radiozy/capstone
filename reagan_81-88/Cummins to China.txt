
The Cummins Engine Company said it had signed an agreement to establish a distribution and technical service center in Peking. 
It said it signed the three-year, non-exclusive agreement in July with the China National Technical Import Corporation, which produced Cummins diesel engines last year under license, and with the China National Automotive Industry Corporation, which will operate and manage the center.
