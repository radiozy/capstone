
An 18-member delegation from the National Council of Churches will visit China next month, the first group of United States church leaders to be officially invited by China's two major Christian organizations.
The delegation includes leaders of both the National Council of Churches and top officials from several Protestant and Orthodox denominations, council officials said.
Council officials said the invitation was the first official one extended by the two major Christian groups in China, th e C hina Christian Council and the Protestant Three-Self Patriotic Movement.
