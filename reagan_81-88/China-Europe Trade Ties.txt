
China is prepared to open its markets further to imports and investment and wants to strengthen its economic and technical relations with the Common Market, its Prime Minister, Zhao Ziyang, said here today.
The Prime Minister, on a visit to the headquarters of the European Economic Community's commission, added that China wanted to enlarge the 1978 trade agreement with the Common Market to include economic cooperation, officials of the commission reported.
Prime Minister Zhao Ziyang, visiting headquarters of European Economic Community's commission, Brussels, says China is prepared to strengthen its economic and technical relations with Common Market (S)
The commission has proposed a new agreement that would cover cooperation in such areas as energy, mining, advanced technologies and agriculture.
China's trade with Western Europe is growing faster than with the rest of the world, Common Market officials said. 
In 1983, China's total imports rose 20 percent while those from Western Europe were up by 37 percent, they added.
