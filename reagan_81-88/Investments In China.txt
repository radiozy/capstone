
Foreign concerns invested nearly $5 billion in China between 1979 and 1982, including nearly $1 billion in offshore oil exploration, the China Daily reported today.
China also got $7.65 billion in loans from foreign governments, international monetary organizations and other sources during the period, the English-language newspaper said.

Foreign concerns invested $2.7 billion in cooperative management projects, including agricultural projects and joint ventures in China's special economic zones, the paper said. 
It said at least 80 percent of the joint ventures were in southern China.
There also was $725 million of investment in compensation trade projects, $141 million in joint enterprises and $367 million in other projects, it added.
Of the loans, the China Daily said, $5.2 billion came in medium- and long-term loans by the governments of Japan, Belgium, Kuwait and Italy.
