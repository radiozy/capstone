
The city's first Chinese-American candidate for a judgeship was proposed yesterday by Mayor Koch.
Randall T. Eng, 35 years old, inspector general of the Correction Department since March 1982, was proposed for a post in Criminal Court.
Born in China, he came to the United States when he was 5 months old and was brought up in Queens. 
Mr. Eng is a 1972 graduate of the St. John's University Law School. 
After eight years in the Queens District Attorney's office, he moved to the Correction Department in 1980.
The Mayor's counsel, Patrick F. X. Mulhearn, said research had determined there have been no Chinese- American judges in the city. 
Mr. Mulhearn said Mr. Eng's appointment, which must await a hearing Sept. 
14 by the Mayor's committee on the judiciary, would leave four court vacancies to be filled by appointment.
This year, there have been 26 Criminal Court and Family Court appointments, along with 9 interim appointments to the Civil Court, where full terms are filled by election.
