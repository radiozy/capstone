
India and China start new talks Monday on a longstanding border dispute, but an Indian negotiator has already ruled out any dramatic results.
The Chinese team, led by a Foreign Ministry adviser, Fu Hao, arrived Saturday night for the talks, which will also focus on ways to improve the often sour relations between the two nations.
Foreign Affairs Secretary Eric Gonsalves, who heads the Indian delegation, told reporters Friday that he did not expect a breakthrough.
