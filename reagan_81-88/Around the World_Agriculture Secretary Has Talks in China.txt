
Agriculture Secretary John R. Block of the United States held talks with Chinese officials in Peking today on expanding trade in farm and forestry products, the New China News Agency reported.
Agriculture Secretary John Block holds talks with Chinese officials in Peking (China)
Mr. Block, who arrived yesterday, met Deputy Prime Minister Wan Li, who is also head of the Agriculture Commission that oversees agriculture in China.
The report said the men discussed exchanges in agricultural science and technology and cooperative projects in agriculture and animal husbandry. 
Mr. Block later met Minister of Light Industry Song Jiwen to discuss cooperation in the food industry, the agency said.
Mr. Block is to return to the United States tomorrow.
