
The American Motors Corporation and a Chinese company will begin production today of Jeep Cherokees in Peking, A.M.C. officials said. 
The joint venture company, the Beijing Jeep Corporation, plans to produce 750 Jeep Cherokees by the end of 1985 and about 4,500 in 1986, A.M.C. said. 
The Chinese version of the Cherokee, a four-wheel-drive station wagon, will be virtually the same as those produced in Toledo, Ohio.
