
A small Chinese naval squadron has moved to the disputed Spratly Islands in the South China Sea, possibly prompting a show of naval force from Vietnam and the Soviet Union, an Indonesian Government official said today.
Small Chinese naval squadron has moved to disputed Spratly Islands in South China Sea, possibly prompting show of naval force from Vietnam and Soviet Union; island group is claimed by China, Vietnam and other governments (S)
The Spratly Island group is claimed by China, Vietnam and other governments.
The official said that in the last two or three days four Chinese vessels - two frigates, a landing craft and an oil supply ship - had approached the islands. 
He said the reason for the Chinese squadron's presence was not known.
But he added that there had been reports of Vietnamese and Soviet naval movements in the area, probably to counter the Chinese presence.
