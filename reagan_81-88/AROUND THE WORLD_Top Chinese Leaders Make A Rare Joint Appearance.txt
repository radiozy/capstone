
In an apparent show of unity, the Communist Party chairman, Hua Guofeng, appeared in public today with the deputy chairman, Deng Xiaoping, for the first time in almost five months.
The two visited the hospital where the body of the writer Mao Dun awaits burial. 
Mr. Hua's removal from his post has been predicted for months,
The state radio identified other visitors at the hospital as Li Xiannian, a deputy chairman; Gen. Hu Yaobang, the party secretary, and Prime Minister Zhao Ziyang. 
The absence of Ye Jianying, a deputy chairman who is on Mao Dun's funeral committee, was not explained.
