
Senator Howard H. Baker Jr., the majority leader, arrived here today to discuss strained relations over Washington's arms sales to Taiwan and to convey a message from President Reagan to Chinese leaders. 
There was no indication what Mr. Reagan's message contained.
Senator Baker's visit coincided with a trip to Taiwan by Senator Barry Goldwater, a longtime friend of the island, which China's leaders consider a rebel province. 
Mr. Goldwater assured Taiwan's Nationalist Government of a commitment to the island.
