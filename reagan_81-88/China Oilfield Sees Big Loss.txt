
This year may be the first in which China's biggest oilfield reports an operating loss, the official New China News Agency said today.
It said the state-run Daqing oilfield in the northeast province of Heilongjiang, which contributed 2.39 billion yuan to the Government last year, would suffer from low domestic prices for oil and high development and operating costs.
It quoted officials at the oilfield, which produced 55.5 million tons of crude oil last year, as saying that they will receive 110 yuan per ton of oil this year, up from 100 yuan last year. 
But that will not be enough to cover costs, the agency said.
