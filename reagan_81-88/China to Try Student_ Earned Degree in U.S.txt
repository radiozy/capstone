
A visiting Chinese official has disclosed that a Chinese student who was arrested in protests in Shanghai would be tried for criminal activities soon.
The 31-year-old student, Yang Wei, who earned a master's degree from the University of Arizona and then returned to China, was arrested Jan. 
11 after taking part in student demonstrations advocating democracy there.
The Chinese official, He Dongchang, a vice chairman of China's State Education Commission, said late last month that Mr. Yang would be prosecuted for an offense he said was ''totally unrelated'' to the protests.
Mr. Yang is the only recent Chinese graduate from an American university known to have been arrested in the crackdown on dissent in China last winter. 
He went back to China a year ago to marry Che Shaoli, who is now studying immunology at Baylor College of Medicine in Houston, and was making arrangements to return to the United States to earn his doctoral degree at the time of his arrest.
