PEKING, July 23 (Reuters) -There are at least nine people over 100 living in Peking, seven of them women, but the exact number must await the results of China's current census, the New China News Agency said today.
The oldest is known as Mrs. Zhang. She will be 105 next November and still has good eyesight. 
Last year, the agency said, after her great-great grandson was born she taught her great granddaughter-in-law to sew a cotton quilt for the baby and could thread the needle without glasses.
Shanghai, China's biggest city, has 23 centenarians, all but one of whom are women, according to a recent report from the municipal census bureau.
