
The Yangcheng Evening News of Canton said three men were executed in Sihui County on Thursday for raping 23 women. 
In addition, The Workers' Daily said Gu Longxi was executed in Shanghai on Wednesday for stealing 70 tons of iron and steel and shipping it to factories in Zhejiang Province. 
Mr. Gu and his accomplices made $11,000 profit, the paper said. 
Thousands of people have been sentenced to death in a drive against crime that began in August.
