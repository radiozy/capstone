
Anna Chennault, a member of the Republican Party's finance committee and long a staunch backer of the Nationalist Chinese Government on Taiwan, arrived in Peking today.
Mrs. Chennault, the widow of Gen. Claire Chennault, the leader of the China-based American ''Flying Tigers'' in World War II, came here with Senator Ted Stevens, Republican of Alaska, and Mrs. Stevens.
Anna Chennault, member of Republican Party's finance committee, arrives in Peking (China)
The three met with Hao Deqing, president of the People's Institute of Foreign Affairs. 
The official New China News Agency said that Mr. Hao gave a banquet for the three and that his institute had invited them to China.
Mrs. Chennault has long been known as a member of Washington's socalled China lobby, which has worked actively in behalf of the Nationalist Chinese.
Taiwan has been a sensitive point between the Chinese and President-elect Ronald Reagan. 
Early in his campaign, the Chinese press denounced Mr. Reagan, saying he wanted to upgrade relations with Taiwan.
