
The State Council, China's Cabinet, dismissed Forestry Minister Yang Zhong today, saying he bore responsibility for a huge forest fire that killed 191 people and charred a huge area of northeastern China, the official New China News Agency said.
The fire, in Heilongjiang Province on the Soviet border, injured 221 people, left 51,000 homeless, destroyed several villages and burned more than 2.47 million acres.
The decision to dismiss Mr. Yang, 55 years old, will be submitted to the standing committee of the National People's Congress for approval, the agency said. 
No replacement was immediately named.
The dismissal indicated displeasure with the announced cause of the fire -carelessness by Forestry Bureau employees - and efforts to extinguish it.
