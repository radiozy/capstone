
The Public Health Ministry said today that an Argentine tourist had died of acquired immune deficiency syndrome, or AIDS, and it announced an emergency quarantine to stop the disease from spreading.
The ministry said AIDS had never before been reported in China.
Chinese Public Health Ministry reports that 34-year-old Argentine tourist Oscar Messina, who had been US resident for 15 years, died of acquired immune deficiency syndrome on June 6 at Peking Union Medical College Hospital; announces that emergency quarantine has been established to stop spread of disease (S)
The ministry report said the Argentine, Oscar Messina, 34 years old, died of a severe lung infection and respiratory failure on June 6 at the Peking Union Medical College Hospital. 
The symptoms are associated with AIDS.
''According to his family, the patient was diagnosed in the United States as having contracted AIDS,'' the ministry's quarantine office said.
An autopsy could not confirm the AIDS diagnosis because the hospital was not equipped to detect the disease, the report said.
Mr. Messina, an Argentine citizen who moved to the United States 15 years ago, joined a 21-member San Francisco-based tour group that arrived in Shanghai on May 27, the report said.
