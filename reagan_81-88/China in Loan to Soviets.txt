
The state-run Bank of China has joined in a syndicated $50 million loan to a Soviet bank, marking the first time China has provided such credit to the Soviet Union, the official China Daily said today.
The daily said the bank was participating in a loan repayable over seven years to the Soviet Vneshekonom Bank. 
Other participants include two banks each from Finland and Denmark and a bank from the Netherlands. 
The report did not say how much the Bank of China was contributing to the loan package.
