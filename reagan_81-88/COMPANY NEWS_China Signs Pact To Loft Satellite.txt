
China has signed its first contract to launch a foreign satellite. 
The official New China News Agency said the contract had been signed by Wu Keli, vice president of the China Great Wall Industrial Corporation, and Henry A. Schwartz, chairman of the board of Terasat Inc. of Houston.
The communications satellite is expected to be launched in 1988. 
Terms were not disclosed.
The report said a Long March-3 rocket would be used to put a Westar satellite in a geostationary orbit. 
The satellite, weighing 2,915 pounds, was to have been launched on an American space shuttle flight last June, but there have been no shuttle flights since the Challenger disaster last January. 
China has also reached preliminary agreements to launch satellites for other American companies.
