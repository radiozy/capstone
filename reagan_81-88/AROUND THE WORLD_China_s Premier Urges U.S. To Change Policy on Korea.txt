
Prime Minister Zhao Ziyang accepted an invitation today to visit North Korea and said the United States should withdraw its troops from South Korea and conclude a peace agreement with the Pyongyang Government.
At a banquet honoring a North Korean delegation headed by Prime Minister Li Chong Ok, who arrived today for a five-day visit, Mr. Zhao also called on the United States to ''stop pursuing a 'two Koreas' policy.''
The date for the visit was not settled.
