
The Government today rebuffed the latest attempt by Pope John Paul II to improve ties with Peking, and it accused the Vatican of interfering in China's domestic affairs.
Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesman Li Zhaoxing rebuffs latest attempt by Pope John Paul II to improve ties with China and accuses Vatican of interfering in China's domestic affairs (S)
A Foreign Ministry spokesman, Li Zhaoxing, said China had taken note of the Pope's statement last Thursday, in which John Paul said the Roman Catholic Church was sympathetic to China's modernization drive.
But Mr. Zhaoxing said, ''We have also taken note that the Vatican still maintains so-called diplomatic relations with Taiwan and continues to interfere in China's internal affairs.''
China has repeatedly said that relations with the Vatican cannot improve until the church cuts ties with Taiwan.
