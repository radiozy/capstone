PEKING, March 29 (Reuters) -China today accused the United States of blocking its application to join the Manila-based Asian Development Bank and reiterated that Taiwan should be expelled from the organization.
He said Mr. Romberg's statement ''shows that the United States is actually placing obstacles in the way of the bank's admission of the People's Republic of China.''
Peking has requested that it replace Taiwan, and the bank is considering the question. 
Mr. Qi said that Mr. Romberg recognized the Government of the People's Republic as the only legitimate Government of China and the representative of China at international organizations, and that Mr. Romberg had said Peking was qualified for membership in the bank.
