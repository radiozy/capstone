
Chinese and Mongolian officials have just ended six weeks of talks on border problems, the first such meeting since the two countries agreed to such discussions in 1964, the New China News Agency reported today.
The joint Boundary Inspection Commission met in Ulan Bator, the Mongolian capital, between Feb. 
18 and April 3, the agency said. 
''The meeting was held to complete the task of joint boundary inspection specified in the boundary protocol signed in 1964,'' it added without elaboration.
