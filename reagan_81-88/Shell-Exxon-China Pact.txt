
Royal Dutch/Shell said it and the Exxon Corporation of the United States signed a contract with the state-owned China National Offshore Oil Corporation to explore for oil in the Pearl River in south China.
A Shell spokesman said Friday that the two companies would bear 50 percent of the exploration cost. 
''We have not been encouraged by our earlier exploration, also in the Pearl River basin,'' she said, ''but we will continue drilling.''
This is the second contract to be awarded in the second round of bidding for offshore exploration rights that started last November.
