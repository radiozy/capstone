
Exports of manufactured goods to China totaled $319 million in the first quarter of this year, up sharply from the $251 million sent in the first three months of 1980, the Commerce Department said today. 
Among the major exports to China, the department said, were polyester yarns, paper and paper-base stocks, phosphate, mining and drilling equipment, scientific and controlling instruments, chemical elements and compounds, kraft liner board, and iron and steel products.
