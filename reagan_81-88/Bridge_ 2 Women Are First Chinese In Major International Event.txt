
In this decade, bridge has boomed in China, but its players have never taken part in a major international invitational event. 
That barrier will be broken when Gu Ling and Zhang Yalan of Canton play tomorrow and Sunday in the prestigious Invitational Charity Pairs at the Cavendish Club, at 105 East 73d Street in Manhattan.
Gu and Zhang were members of the team that won the Far East women's championship in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, six months ago.
In the 1986 world championships in Miami Beach, they finished 12th in the women's pairs, the best result by players outside North America and Europe.
