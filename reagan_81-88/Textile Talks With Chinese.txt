GENEVA, July 25 (Reuters) - The United States and China resumed negotiations in Geneva today on a textile agreement, trade sources said, with both sides optimistic that a pact could be reached to replace the one that expired last December.
When earlier talks failed to produce a new accord, Washington unilaterally imposed textile import restrictions and Peking threatened to reduce its purchases of United States farm products. 
Near-record harvests in China are expected this year.
American farming interests hope the textile dispute ends soon, but American textile manufacturers are seeking tighter restrictions.
