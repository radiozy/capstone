
Australia and China signed a joint-venture agreement today to mine iron ore in Western Australia. 
The project at Mount Channar, worth $250 million (Australian), or about $170 million (United States), is China's biggest overseas investment, Prime Minister Bob Hawke said after the signing ceremony here. 
The venture between Channar Mining and the China Metallurgical Import and Export Corporation is designed to produce high-grade iron ore for export by 1990.
