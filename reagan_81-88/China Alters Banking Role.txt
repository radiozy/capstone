
China announced a shake-up of its banking system today, saying a commercial bank would be established to take over the commercial role of the People's Bank.
China reorganizes its banking system by establishing commercial bank to take over commercial role of People's Bank; People's Bank would then concentrate on monetary policy making (S)
The official New China News Agency said the People's Bank, as China's central bank, would concentrate on monetary policy making. 
The new commercial bank is to handle credit for industrial enterprises and personal savings. 
Deposits in the People's Bank held by overseas Chinese and foreigners would not be affected. 
The function of the Bank of China in handling foreign exchange is unchanged, the press agency said.
It quoted a State Council decree as saying the shake-up was part of a restructuring of the Chinese economy, including opening it to the West.
