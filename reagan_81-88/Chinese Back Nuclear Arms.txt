
A senior Chinese official told a European Parliament delegation today that China supports a nuclear deterrent in Western Europe against the Soviet Union. 
''If a lion is going to attack you, you've got to be in a position to frighten it away,'' Peng Zhen, chairman of the National People's Congress, was quoted as telling the delegation from the legislative branch of the Common Market. 
It was the first time a Chinese leader has been quoted as endorsing nuclear weapons in Western Europe.
