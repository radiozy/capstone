
A Taiwan Air Force major the Chinese say defected to the mainland was given a $433,000 reward and a position in China's Air Force today, the Peking radio reported.
Huang Che-cheng, 29 years old, wore his Chinese Air Force uniform at a rally of more than 2,000 people in Fuzhou, 150 miles northwest of the Taiwan field where he took off Saturday for the mainland, the radio said.

''I certainly will live up to the expectations and trust placed in me by the motherland and the people,'' Major Huang said in a statement broadcast over the radio. 
He said he would work for the reunification of China and Taiwan.
The defection was reported here yesterday. 
In Taiwan, military officials said Major Huang had been forced to land after his American-made F-5F two-seater ran low on fuel.
