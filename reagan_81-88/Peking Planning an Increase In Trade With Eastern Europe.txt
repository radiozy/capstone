
China is to increase trade ties with Eastern Europe, a Chinese official indicated in an interview published today.
Chinese Deputy Minister of State Economy, Zhu Rongji, says China is to increase trade ties with Eastern Europe; says economic delegation that recently returned from visits to five Eastern European countries agreed on projects worth $80 million to help upgrade Chinese factories (S)
Zhu Rongji, Deputy Minister of the State Economic Commission, told The China Daily that an economic delegation recently returned from visits to five Eastern European countries.
They agreed on projects worth $80 million to help upgrade Chinese factories. 
In return, China will provide the five countries - Hungary, Bulgaria, Czechoslovakia, East Germany and Poland - with agricultural and light- industry products, including textiles.
