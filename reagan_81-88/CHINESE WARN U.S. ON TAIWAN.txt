
Chinese-American relations will be ''gravely impaired'' if the Reagan Administration continues its staunch support of Taiwan, according to an article published today in an offical magazine.
The weekly Peking Review said it was a mistake to assume that China would tolerate American support for Taiwan in exchange for receiving economic help.
Official Chinese magazine warns that Chinese-American relations will be "gravely impaired" if US continues its strong support of Taiwan
''If the United States stubbornly adheres to the Taiwan Relations Act in its actions, then normal relations between China and the United States, established only after long and painstaking efforts by the two countries, will certainly be gravely impaired,'' it said.
 
U.S. Is Said to Violate Accord
China maintains that the American measure, which provides for continued United States military sales and other support to Taiwan, violates the communique that established relations between Peking and Washington on Jan. 
1, 1979.
In the communique, the United States acknowledged that there was only one China and ''Taiwan is part of China.''
The Peking Review, implying that China was prepared to break relations with the United States over the Taiwan issue, said the history of Chinese-Soviet relations in the 1960's should be a ''salutary lesson.''
