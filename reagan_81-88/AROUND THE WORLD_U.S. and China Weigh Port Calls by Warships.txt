
Top United States and Chinese military officials have discussed the possibility of port calls by American warships in China, but there has been ''no firm agreement yet,'' the Pentagon spokesman, Michael I. Burch, said today.
Mr. Burch was replying to a question about published reports that China appears ready to allow United States naval ships to visit its ports for the first time in more than 30 years and that this possibility arose after recent discussions between the Secretary of the Navy, John F. Lehman, and the Chinese authorities.

''The subject of port visits was discussed'' when Secretary of Defense Caspar W. Weinberger visited China last fall, Mr. Burch said.
There were discussions again when the Chinese Defense Minister, Zhang Aiping, was here in June.
Asked about the value of such naval visits by American warships to China, Mr. Burch said they would ''show close cooperation between China and the United States.''
