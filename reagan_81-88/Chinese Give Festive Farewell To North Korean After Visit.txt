PEKING, Sept. 
25 (AP) - China gave Kim Il Sung, the North Korean leader, a festive sendoff today as he left by special train after a visit in which he and Chinese leaders proclaimed lasting friendship and identical world views.
Several thousand cheering citizens saw him off at the Peking railway station, cleared of ordinary passengers for the occasion. 
Little is known about the substance of his visit.
In a dinner toast on Friday, Mr. Kim said China and North Korea are ''fully unanimous in our view on all the issues discussed.''
