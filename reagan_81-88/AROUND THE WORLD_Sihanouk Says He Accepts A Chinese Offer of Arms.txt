
Prince Norodam Sihanouk, the former Cambodian head of state, said today that China had offered him enough arms to equip an army of 3,000 men to fight the Vietnamese and that he had accepted.
Prince Sihanouk announced the offer after he met here with Deng Xiaoping, Deputy Chairman of the Chinese Communist Party, for more than an hour.
