
Muhammad Ali, visiting Peking at the invitation of the Chinese Government, put on the Ali shuffle and jabbed his way through five one-minute rounds in a makeshift ring yesterday before about 500 students at the Peking Physical Education Institute. 
Ali, 43 years old, did not seem affected by his Parkinson's Syndrome, but one boxer said: ''The coaches told us only to hit him in the stomach. 
We were warned he is not in good health.''
