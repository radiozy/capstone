
Yugoslavia regained the men's double title from China today at the 37th world table tennis championships. 
Dragutin Surbek and Zoran Kalinic defeated China's Xie Saike and Jiang Jialiang, 21-15, 19-21, 20-22, 21-17, 22-20.
TOKYO, May 8 (UPI) - Yugoslavia regained the men's double title from China today at the 37th world table tennis championships.Yugoslavia won the doubles title at the 35th championships held in Pyongyang, capital of North Korea, in 1979. 
But China took back the title in 1981 in Novi Sad, Yugoslavia, in the course of its unprecedented clean sweep of all seven championships at stake.
Eric Boggan, the 19-year-old North American champion from Merrick, L.I., who had the best performance for the United States in 25 years, was eliminated by the top-seeded defending champion Guo Yuehua of China, 19-21, 14-21, 16-21, in the fourth round of the men's singles.
The Chinese women won the doubles competition as Shen Jianping and Dai Lili defeated Geng Lijuan and Huang Junqun, 23-21, 21-12, 15-21, 10-21, 21-16, in an all-Chinese final today.
Earlier, China retained both men's and women's team titles.
