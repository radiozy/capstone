NEW DELHI, May 20 (Reuters) -India and China ended four days of talks today, still sharply divided over a 20-year-old boundary dispute, a spokesman for the Indian Government said.
Although the differences were still very wide, the spokesman said, there had been a narrowing of differences on how the dispute should be solved. 
He said both sides, which held a first round of talks in Peking in December, have agreed to meet again.
India says China occupies 14,000 square miles of its territory in the rugged northwestern Ladakh region, while the Chinese claim nearly 50,000 square miles in northeastern India.
