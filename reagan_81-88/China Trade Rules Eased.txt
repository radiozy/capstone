
The Reagan Administration said today that it was relaxing restrictions on many goods exported to China.
Commerce Secretary Malcolm Baldrige said licensing changes would expand to 27, from seven, the number of categories of products that may be shipped to China without special review. 
Up to 75 percent of all products now awaiting Commerce Department approval for shipment to China will benefit, he said. 
Items eligible for the speeded-up processing include computers, machine tools, semiconductors, robotics and electronic instruments.
The ban on exporting materials to China that could have military use will continue, Mr. Baldrige said.
