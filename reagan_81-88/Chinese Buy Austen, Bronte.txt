
Jane Austen's ''Pride and Prejudice,'' Charlotte Bronte's ''Jane Eyre'' and Romain Rolland's ''Jean Christophe'' were among the most popular translations sold in China's state-run bookshops last year, the New China News Agency reported today. 
It said that books on economics had also become more popular, but gave no figures.
