
Twenty-five people were trampled to death in two Chinese cities, and at least 220 others were injured during celebrations Wednesday of the Lantern Festival. 
Eighteen people were crushed to death in Xining, capital of Qinghai province, when 100,000 people who turned out to watch a lantern procession panicked, the official New China News Agency said today. 
Seven people were trampled to death in Lankao county, Henan province, as a crowd of 20,000 dispersed after watching a fireworks show, it said.
