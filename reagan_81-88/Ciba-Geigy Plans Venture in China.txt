
Ciba-Geigy A.G., Switzerland's biggest chemical and pharmaceuticals company, said it plans to begin production in China in 1989 with a large local manufacturer, the Beijing General Pharmaceuticals Corporation.
The initial capital investment for the venture will be the equivalent of $12 million at current exchange rates, according to a Ciba-Geigy statement.
A company spokesman, Jan Krieger, said Ciba-Geigy would supply the equipment and the new company's president would be Chinese. 
The plant, which will be near Beijing, will supply the domestic market and will be able to produce the full range of Ciba-Geigy drugs, the company said. 
These include cardiovascular, infection-fighting and anti-rheumatism products.
