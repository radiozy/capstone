
Some of the 65 school children who arrived in New York last night from Peking and Shanghai to begin one of the largest cultural exchanges ever between the United States and China. 
Jacques d'Amboise of the New York City Ballet recruited the children in China for a program sponsored by the National Dance Institute. 
Mr. d'Amboise is to direct them and 1,500 young American dancers in a production at the Felt Forum June 1 and 2. 
(NYT/Larry C. Morris)
Correction
A picture caption in Metropolitan Report on Wednesday misstated the number of schoolchildren from China who arrived in New York for a cultural exchange. 
There were 56.
Correction-Date: May 23, 1986, Friday, Late City Final Edition
