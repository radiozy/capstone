
The Chinese Foreign Ministry has denied that a submarine exploded during a missile firing test and called the report ''sheer fabrication,'' the English-language newspaper China Daily reported today.
Chinese Foreign Ministry reportedly denies submarine exploded during test in North China Sea in early September
Foreign military sources said last week that a nonnuclear submarine in the North China Sea exploded in early September during a unsuccessful attempt to launch a missile. 
They said that about 100 people were killed.
The sources reconfirmed the report today.
