
A senior North Korean official today rejected United States suggestions that China take part in talks on a peace treaty to put a formal end to the Korean War.
Senior North Korean official rejects US suggestion that China take part in discussions among North and South Korea and US on peace treaty to put formal end to Korean War (S)
North Korea's recent proposal that the two Koreas and the United States discuss Korea's problems was ''a most reasonable formula,'' Deputy Prime Minister Ho Dam said. 
The idea of enlarging the talks ''cannot be regarded as a stand aimed at solving the question in good faith,'' he declared.
The official North Korean press agency reported, in a dispatch monitored here, that Mr. Ho was speaking in Parliament in Pyongyang today.
