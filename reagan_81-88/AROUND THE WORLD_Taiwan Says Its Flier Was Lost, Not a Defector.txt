TAIPEI, Taiwan, April 24 (UPI) -The Defense Ministry today disputed China's report that a Taiwan military pilot had defected, saying it was ''obvious'' he had lost his way in thick fog and flew his propeller-driven plane to China by mistake.
Peking said Saturday that Maj. Li Dawei, dissatisfied with the Chinese Nationalists in Taiwan, flew his U-6A artillery spotter plane to China.
''Communist China claimed Maj. Li Dawei landed in a coastal area in Fujian Province instead of an airfield,'' a Taiwan statement said. 
''It was an obvious crash landing in thunderstorm and heavy fog.''
