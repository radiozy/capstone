
Defense Minister Zhang Aiping is scheduled to leave Tuesday for a trip that is to include a United States visit designed to improve relations ''between the two countries and the two armies,'' the Defense Ministry said today. 
Mr. Zhang is reciprocating for Defense Secretary Caspar W. Weinberger's visit here last September. 
Full details of Mr. Zhang's itinerary were not disclosed.
