
The Archbishop of Canterbury, the Most Rev. Robert Runcie, arrived in Hong Kong today for a three-day visit after his first official trip to China. 
Dr. Runcie said that he held talks with political leaders in China on a variety of issues but that the question of Hong Kong's future was not raised.
