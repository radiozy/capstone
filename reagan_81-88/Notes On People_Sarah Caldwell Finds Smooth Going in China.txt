
Sarah Caldwell, the American conductor and director, has found directing the China's Central Opera Company in Peking in her version of Verdi's ''La Traviata'' to be an experience devoid of some of the problems she usually encounters.
Notes on People column says Sarah Caldwell is finding easy going in the direction of China's Central Opera Company in Peking
''There is no prima donna-ism whatsoever,'' Miss Caldwell said Tuesday. 
''The chorus helps move the scenery. 
In this world, it is rare to find people who do what you ask instantly without arguing or having to prove something.''
Miss Caldwell, who directs the Boston Opera Company, said she was becoming accustomed to hearing ''La Traviata,'' called ''Camellia Girl'' in Peking, sung in Chinese.
''It's going to sound funny in Italian,'' she said.
