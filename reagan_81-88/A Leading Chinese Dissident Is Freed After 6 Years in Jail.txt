
A prominent dissident active in the Democracy Wall movement in the late 1970's has been freed after a six-year jail term, Chinese sources said today.
The dissident, Lu Lin, a former editor of the unofficial journal Exploration, was released from Peking No. 1 Prison in August but is still under strict police surveillance.
The sources, who asked not to be identified, also denied rumors circulating in Beijing and abroad that China's best-known dissident, Wei Jingsheng, had died while serving a 15-year sentence imposed in October 1979.
The trial of Mr. Wei, chief editor of Exploration, marked the end of a brief period of political liberalization when the Communist authorities tolerated a limited freedom of speech.
Exploration was one of the movement's most outspoken magazines, and it continued to publish under Mr. Lu and other editors after Mr. Wei's imprisonment.
