
China played Bolivia to a 1-1 tie, and Guinea scored a 1-0 victory over the United States team coached by Angus McAlpine, but the most important competition in the 16-nation youth soccer tournament being held in four Chinese cities may not be taking place on the field but in the stands. 
In an effort to head off the kind of violence that marred a World Cup match in Peking in May, the Chinese Government is sponsoring a contest among the four host cities with a prize for the one that produces the most decorous crowds.
China, in effort to head off kind of violence thar marred World Cup match in Peking in March, sponsors contest among four cities hosting 16-nation youth soccer tournament with a prize for one that produces the most decorous crowds (S)Under the rules of the contest, a city wins points when its fans engage in such model behavior as ''civilized clapping'' and loses points for such fan transgressions as ''jeering in unison.''
The fans were generally docile at the opening match between China and Bolivia at Workers Stadium in Peking, but the chance to win a Government trophy may not have been the only factor in the crowd's calm. 
In addition to sponsoring the contest, the Government also searched the spectators and surrounded the stadium with thousands of policemen.
