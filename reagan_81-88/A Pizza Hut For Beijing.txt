
Pizza Hut will soon join Kentucky Fried Chicken in bringing American fast food to China's capital.
The chain will open the first pizza restaurant in China by the end of the year, the official New China News Agency said Thursday.
 
Pizza is available now only at
Chinese hotels catering to foreigners.
Kentucky Fried Chicken, which opened in November, was China's first Western fast-food chain. 
Business has been so good that two more restaurants are planned in the city.
 
Both Pizza Hut and Kentucky
Fried Chicken are subsidiaries of Pepsico Inc.
