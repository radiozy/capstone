
Panic buying has forced 19 provinces and cities to impose rationing on salt, the manager of the China National Salt Company was quoted today as saying. 
The official China Daily quoted Zhang Tiecheng as saying the company stopped sending wholesale salt supplies to enterprises and individuals this week, limiting supplies to retail outlets in an effort to stop hoarding and panic buying. 
He was quoted as saying the shortage resulted from a rapid increase in salt consumption by the chemical industry and panic buying in 26 cities and regions.
