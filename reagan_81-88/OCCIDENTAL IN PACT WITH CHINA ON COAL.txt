PEKING, March 8 (Reuters) -China and the Occidental Petroleum Corporation signed an interim agreement today to develop a huge coal mine that could become China's largest joint venture with an American company.The signing of the agreement followed completion of a feasibility study begun last year.
The project involves the Pingshuo mine in Shanxi province, China's major coal-producing region. 
Construction of the open-pit mine would begin July 1 and production would start in July 1985, according to officials from Occidental. 
The project, to include a $230 million investment by Occidental, is to produce 15 million metric tons of coal annually by 1986, the officials said. 
It will be developed by Occidental's Island Creek subsidiary.
China's coal output last year totaled 644 million tons.
