
The Chrysler Corporation hopes to add passenger cars to the line of Jeeps that it produces in China, a top official of the auto maker said. 
Gerald Greenwald, head of the Chrysler Motors division of the Chrysler Corporation, said his company had been negotiating with First Automotive Works of Changchun, in northeast China, to produce higher-priced autos for use as official cars and taxis.
First Automotive hopes to begin construction by the end of the year on a plant that would initially produce 30,000 cars a year, with potential capacity of 150,000, Mr. Greenwald said. 
China produced about 400,000 motor vehicles last year, almost all trucks and buses. 
Only about 20,000 passenger cars were built. 
Chrysler inherited a stake in China last year when it acquired the American Motors Corporation, which has been producing Cherokee Jeeps as part of a joint venture known as the Beijing Jeep Company.
