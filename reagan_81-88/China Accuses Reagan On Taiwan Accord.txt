PEKING, Oct. 
9 (Reuters) - China accused President Reagan today of violating the terms of the recent Chinese-American communique on Taiwan by making China's peaceful reunification a prerequisite for ending arms sales to the Nationalist-ruled island.
The agency quoted the President as saying that the Chinese Nationalists would ''get everything they need for their defense.''
It said he arbitrarily linked continuing American arms sales to how China achieved reunification, a problem that the agency said was ''an entirely internal affair of China.''
It said Mr. Reagan's remarks were the latest of many by United States Government officials misrepresenting the spirit and letter of the Aug. 
17 joint communique.
