
For the first time, a Western pop band has been invited to tour China.
Wham, the British group, has been invited to tour China; group will play two concerts in China in April (S)The British group Wham, whose album ''Make It Big'' is currently the No. 1 album in the United States, has announced that it will play two concerts in China next month.
The band is to perform April 7 at the 15,000-seat People's Gymnasium in Peking, to which it was invited by the Youth Federation of China. 
On April 10, Wham is to perform in Canton, on the invitation of Canton's Minister of Culture. 
Tickets for each concert will be about $25 to $30, the group reported.
Five Chinese groups have recorded Wham's latest British and American hit, ''Careless Whisper,'' in Cantonese and Mandarin.
