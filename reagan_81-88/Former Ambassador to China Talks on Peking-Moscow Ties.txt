
The United States should be careful not to use China's uneasiness with the Soviet Union as a ''counterweight'' in foreign policy, says Leonard Woodcock, a former United States Ambassador to China.
Mr. Woodcock said Thursday that strong cultural and ideological differences between the two Communist countries will keep them from establishing a warm relationship.
He also told a University of Connecticut audience that Taiwan and Chinacould ''find a mutually acceptable political accommodation'' to their differences if left alone.
