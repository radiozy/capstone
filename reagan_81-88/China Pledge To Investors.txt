
Prime Minister Zhao Ziyang has told Western diplomats and business executives that China was ready to improve conditions for foreign investment.
Mr. Zhao met with three diplomats and 12 executives from Japan, the United States, West Germany and Britain on Thursday. 
He said China was aware of serious criticism of its investment climate, the New China News Agency said.
''It is very significant that the head of the Government should tell foreign businessmen and diplomats of a commitment to solve problems at a very high level,'' said the American Ambassador, Winston Lord, who attended the meeting.
