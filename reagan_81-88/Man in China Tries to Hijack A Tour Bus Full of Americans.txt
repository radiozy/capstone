PEKING, Oct. 
11 (UPI) - A man tried to hijack a bus full of American tourists in north central China Sunday but was overpowered by the police before he could carry out his plan to defect to Taiwan, tour sources said today.Sources said the man boarded the bus clutching a package and handed a note written in Chinese to one of the American tourists. 
The note reportedly said the package was a bomb and added: ''Drive to the airport. 
I want to go to Taiwan.''
The American handed the note to a Chinese guide, who stalled and did not announce anything.
As the man anxiously anticipated going to the airport, the sources said, the bus driver slipped out and alerted the police, who quickly overpowered the hijacker.
A Chinese Government spokesman confirmed the attempted hijacking but said details were not available.
