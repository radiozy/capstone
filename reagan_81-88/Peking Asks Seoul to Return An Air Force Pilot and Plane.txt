
China asked South Korea today to return a Chinese Air Force pilot and the MIG-19 jet fighter he flew to a South Korean air base.
The jet was intercepted Friday afternoon over the Yellow Sea and escorted to an air base near Chongju, 70 miles southeast of Seoul, a South Korean Defense Ministry spokesman said.
He said the plane and pilot were under the protection of South Korea, adding, ''The case will be dealt with according to our law.''
In Taiwan, the Foreign Ministry said it had instructed its embassy in Seoul to allow the pilot to come to Taiwan if he wishes.
China and South Korea have no diplomatic relations, but the two nations have had increasing unofficial contacts.
