
Commerce Secretary Malcolm Baldrige said today that a new policy on China trade was nearly complete and could sharply increase technology exports to China.
Mr. Baldrige said that Government committees had nearly completed the so-called technological transfer guidelines and that the details would be made available after obtaining clearance from United States allies.
US Commerce Secretary Baldrige says new policy on China trade is nearly complete and could sharply increase technology exports to China, House subcommittee hearing (S)
He outlined the new guidelines at a hearing by a special House subcommittee on trade with China.
The difficulties in obtaining clearances for exports of technology that could be used for military purposes has been ''the single most important impediment'' to developing trade with China, said the subcommittee chairman, Al Swift, a Washington Democrat.
Mr. Baldrige said the new guidelines would be divided into ''green, intermediate and red zones,'' with green representing products that would receive routine clearances. 
''Almost 75 percent of People's Republic of China applications will fall within the green zone,'' he said.
