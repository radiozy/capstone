
Dancer Fitzgerald Sample said it has taken an equity position, for an undisclosed amount, in China/USA Communications Inc., the American partner in a venture with China Central Television, China's state-owned and sole television network. 
The venture will provide production, programming and consulting services.
It is the only company with an exclusive license to sell prime commercial time on Chinese television.
Clifford Jones Jr., who most recently served as president of Videocom, a Boston-based video-film production company, has been named chairman and chief executive officer of the concern. 
Peter F. McSpadden, president of DFS Holdings, and William McC. 
Vickery, vice chairman, will serve as directors.
''The greatest strength of our company is that we are able to deliver U.S. products and services for and with the Chinese,'' Mr. Jones said. 
''Our CCTV partners are comfortable with us and open to suggestions. 
We, in turn, have learned from them the kinds of approaches that work, and don't work, in reaching the Chinese television audience.''
The company's services will be available to Dancer Fitzgerald clients and non-clients alike. 
''Those companies not currently handled by DFS or DFS International will be represented by Cliff Jones,'' said Mr. McSpadden. ''Since most advertising in China tends to be corporate or informational, and since China/USA Communications is a conduit to the Chinese market, not a substitute for an advertising agency, we foresee few, if any, conflicts.''
There are some 45 million television sets in China, with the viewing audience estimated at between 300 million and 600 million.
