
China's deeply divided leadership appeared today to have delayed a decision on whether to execute Jiang Qing, the widow of Mao Zedong, as officials sought to reach a consensus on her fate.
Diplomatic sources had said that the verdicts and sentences against Miss Jiang, three other members of the radical ''Gang of Four'' and six former military officials would be handed down today.
Chinese leadership is divided over whether to execute Jiang Qing, widow of Mao Zedong, who was tried in 'Gang of Four' trialBut the day passed with no courtroom activity and a Government spokesman said merely, ''There is no session today.''
He did not indicate when sentencing would take place. 
The diplomatic sources said that the sentencing had been postponed because the leadership was still locked in a struggle over Miss Jiang's fate, arguing over whether she should be executed for abuse of power and treason during the Cultural Revolution, or given a long prison term.
