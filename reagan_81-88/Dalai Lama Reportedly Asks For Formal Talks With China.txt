
The Dalai Lama, the exiled Tibetan leader, has sent a message to Chinese leaders seeking formal talks to resolve the political difficulties in his homeland, a Tibetan spokesman said today.
The move follows a speech by the Dalai Lama on June 15 in which, for the first time in 29 years of opposition to Chinese rule over Tibet, he acknowledged China's right to maintain troops there and to control Tibet's foreign affairs. 
Tibet was shaken last fall and in March by anti-Chinese riots. 
A Chinese spokesman here said Beijing was prepared to ''discuss everything except independence'' with the Dalai Lama.
