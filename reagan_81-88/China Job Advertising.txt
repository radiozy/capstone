
China's Shenzhen Special Economic Zone, a showcase for economic changes, has turned to newspaper advertising to fill 12 local government posts, a Hong Kong newspaper said Friday. 
The pro-Beijing Ta Kung Pao said in a report from Shenzhen that more than 320 people responded to newspaper advertisements for the posts. 
Government positions are usually filled through official channels, although state corporations have begun advertising to fill vacancies, Western diplomats said.
