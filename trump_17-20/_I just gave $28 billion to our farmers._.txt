
Farm aid is projected to set a record this year.
— Mr. Trump
This is true.
From 2018 through 2019, Mr. Trump's administration did give about $28 billion to farmers. 
He did so using a Department of Agriculture fund for the purpose of mitigating the impact of his trade wars with China and Europe. 
Total farm aid is projected to increase this year to a record $46 billion. 
Mr. Trump was incorrect, however, when he suggested that China was covering these costs: It is American taxpayer money.
