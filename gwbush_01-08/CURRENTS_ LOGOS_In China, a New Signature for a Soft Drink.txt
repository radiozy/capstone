
Coca-Cola has unveiled a curvy logo in Shanghai, its first Chinese script logo since 1979, when Coca-Cola re-entered the Chinese market after a 30-year absence. 
"With the new logo, we wanted to inject some new energy, so it looks more consistent with the English Spencerian script -- with the curves on the bottom and the top," said John Cheung, director of division marketing of Coca-Cola in China.
The 1979 logo, he said, "wasn't a very specific design."
In the United States, the per capita consumption of Coke is 425 8-ounce servings a year. 
The Chinese drink about nine servings of Coca-Cola, which they pronounce keh-koe keh-luh. 
The logo's characters, designed by Alan Chan of Hong Kong, loosely translate to delicious and enjoyable.
