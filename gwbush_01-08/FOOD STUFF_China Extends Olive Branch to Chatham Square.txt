
When Colette Rossant and Veronica Leung, the partners in Dim Sum Go Go in Chinatown, went to Nanning in China last spring, they discovered an unusual dish, steamed pork topped with Chinese black olives, of all things. 
So they brought the recipe home, and they serve the dish for $15.95 in their restaurant, at 5 East Broadway (Chatham Square).
It is also easy to prepare at home. 
You will need a pound of minced boneless pork leg or butt mixed with two tablespoons each minced cilantro leaves, scallions and Chinese celery (or regular celery). 
Season with salt and pepper, spread it on an oiled nine-inch plate and cover it with a quarter-cup of finely chopped cured black olives. 
The olives do not have to be Chinese; Italian or French ones do nicely, and you could even use tapenade. 
Place the plate in a steamer over two inches of water and steam 15 minutes. 
Cut in wedges and serve directly from the steamer as an appetizer for two or more.
