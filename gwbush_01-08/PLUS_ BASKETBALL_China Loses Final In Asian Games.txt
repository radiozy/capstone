
Yao Ming and China were upset by South Korea, 102-100, in overtime of the gold medal game at the Asian Games in Pusan, South Korea. 
Yao, taken first over all in the 2002 N.B.A. draft by the Houston Rockets, had 23 points and 22 rebounds for China, which led by 84-71 with slightly more than three minutes left.
China's other N.B.A. center, Menk Bateer of the San Antonio Spurs, played less than five minutes and scored 4 points.
The last time China lost an Asian Games men's basketball final was also against South Korea, in 1982.
ILGAUSKAS IS SUSPENDED: Cleveland Cavaliers center Zydrunas Ilgauskas was suspended by the N.B.A. for one game after being arrested last month on drunken driving charges.
Ilgauskas will be suspended without pay and will miss the regular-season opener Oct. 
29 at Sacramento, the league said.
Last week, the 7-foot-3 Ilgauskas pleaded no contest to drunken driving and was sentenced to one year's probation.
Ilgauskas played in 62 games last season, averaging 11.1 points and 5.4 rebounds.
YES TO SHOW SETON HALL GAMES: Seton Hall and the YES Network reached agreement yesterday to broadcast a number of the university's athletic events and a show with the men's basketball coach Louis Orr.
The network will broadcast 10 magazine-format shows beginning in December, giving viewers an insight into Seton Hall basketball and the university's athletic program.
Included in the agreement are telecasts of Seton Hall men's and women's soccer games and women's basketball games. 
More sports will be added.
