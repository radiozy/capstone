
The San Diego Zoo said today that because of the outbreak of respiratory illness, it would put off handing over to China the first giant panda born in the Western Hemisphere in a decade.
The 3-year-old panda, called Hua Mei, had been scheduled to leave in June for a wildlife preserve in China to fulfill the zoo's loan contract with the Chinese government, the zoo's spokeswoman, Yadira Galindo, said. 
She said that China and the zoo "have jointly decided this is not the right time to send Hua Mei to China because of the risk to the people who would travel with her to China."
