
An American team searching for a C.I.A. plane that went down 50 years ago in northeastern China has reached the crash site and is searching with metal detectors to locate the wreckage, American Embassy officials said today.
The two pilots, Robert C. Snoddy and Norman A. Schwartz, were about to pick up an anticommunist Chinese spy in the region formerly known as Manchuria when their plane was shot down on Nov. 
29, 1952.
China has told the Pentagon that the pilots were buried at the crash site. 
Two C.I.A. officers aboard the plane were captured and imprisoned by China for two decades.
