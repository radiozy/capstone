
When Jean-Paul Viguier, a Paris architect, wants to woo clients, he does not show them detailed drawings. 
Instead, he creates tiny jewel-like models that represent ''the imagination of what the building is going to be,'' which he assembles in front of them. 
''As the client watches what I am doing, little by little, I get into the concept of the project,'' Mr. Viguier said. 
''If he loves it, he will never forget about it.''
Seven models, including the plexiglass and aluminum versions of the Sofitel Chicago Water Tower, top, and the proposed Franco-Chinese Centre for Education and Industry in Shanghai, above, will be part of an exhibition of his work, Feb. 
4 to 29, at the French Embassy, 972 Fifth Avenue (78th Street), (212) 439-1417.
