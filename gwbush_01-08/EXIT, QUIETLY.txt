
Not quite seven months after taking the top job at 3com, R. Scott Murray quit last week. 
Mr. Murray's employment contract could have let him fight for hundreds of thousands of dollars in cash and millions of stock options, but he agreed to leave with only his last paycheck.
Why leave so quickly and quietly?
To spend more time with his family. 
Really. The company, which has not posted a profit since 2000, is expanding rapidly in China, and Mr. Murray concluded he could not travel as much as needed from 3com's headquarters in Marlborough, Mass., and still spend time with his children.
Edgar Masri, a former 3com manager who worked at the venture capital firm Matrix Partners, succeeded Mr. Murray as chief executive. 
The company also hired Robert Y. L. Mao to run its China-based joint venture.
