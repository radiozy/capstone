
Praxair, a leading producer of industrial gases, said yesterday that it had a first-quarter profit as sales rose in Europe and China.
Net income was $130 million, or 79 cents a share, contrasted with a net loss of $12 million, or 7 cents a share, in the period in 2002. 
Results in the year-earlier quarter were reduced by $139 million by an accounting change. 
Sales rose 8.5 percent, to $1.34 billion. 
A stronger euro pushed sales up 25 percent in Europe, and new business increased sales 15 percent in China.
