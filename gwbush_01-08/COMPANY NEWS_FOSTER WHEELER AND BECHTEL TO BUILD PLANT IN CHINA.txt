
Foster Wheeler Ltd., a power-plant and refinery builder, the Bechtel Group and a Chinese engineering company won a contract to build a $4.3 billion petrochemicals plant in China's Guangdong Province. 
Specific allotments of funds under the contract were not disclosed. 
Closely held Bechtel, the world's largest construction company, and the others in the group, which is called BSF, will build the plant for CNOOC Ltd., a unit of the China National Offshore Oil Corporation and the Royal Dutch/Shell Group's Shell Petrochemicals Company, a spokesman, Richard Tauberman said. 
The group, which includes Sinopec Engineering Inc., a unit of the China Petrochemical Corporation, won the design award for the plant in March 2001. 
The plant is scheduled to begin operations by the end of 2005.
