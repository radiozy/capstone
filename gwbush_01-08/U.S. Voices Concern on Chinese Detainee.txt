
The State Department expressed concern on Monday over the detention of Zhao Yan, a Chinese research assistant in the Beijing bureau of The New York Times, saying American officials had raised his case with the Foreign Ministry in Beijing and with the Chinese Embassy in Washington.
''We are seeking clarification of his status, and expressing our concern for his welfare and underscoring our view that the role of a free press is critical in providing information to build a strong civil society,'' said Adam Ereli, the deputy State Department spokesman, speaking at the regular daily briefing in response to a question about Mr.
Zhao's status.
Mr. Zhao was seized Sept. 
17 in Shanghai on suspicion of revealing state secrets. 
His family was formally notified of his detention four days later. 
The New York Times has contacted the White House, the State Department and the Chinese government on Mr. Zhao's behalf.
