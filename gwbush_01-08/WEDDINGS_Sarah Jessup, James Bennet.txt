
Sarah Huntington Jessup, a daughter of Anne M. Hamilton of West Hartford, Conn., and John Knox Jessup Jr. of Seal Harbor, Me., was married in West Hartford yesterday to James Douglas Bennet, a son of Douglas J. Bennet of Middletown, Conn., and Susanne K. Bennet of Washington. 
Judge John P. Maloney of the Superior Court of Connecticut in Hartford, who is the bride's stepfather, officiated.
Ms. Jessup, 36, is keeping her name. 
She graduated from Vassar College and received a master's degree in cultural anthropology from the University of Wisconsin and a doctorate in that subject from the University of Michigan. 
As a Fulbright fellow in 1997 and 1998, she studied Chinese opera in Shanxi Province, China. 
Her father retired as the director of training policy for the Agency for International Development in Washington. 
Her mother, until February a reporter for The Hartford Courant, is a freelance writer. 
The bride's stepmother, Pamela Russell Jessup, is an architect in Seal Harbor.
Mr. Bennet, 35, is the Washington staff writer for The New York Times Magazine. 
In September, he is to become the Jerusalem bureau chief for The Times. 
He graduated summa cum laude from Yale. 
His mother teaches English as a second language at Language ETC, a nonprofit organization in Washington. 
His father, from 1983 to 1992 the president of National Public Radio, is the president of Wesleyan University. 
The bridegroom's stepmother, Midge Bowen Bennet, a former NPR executive, is the consultant to Wesleyan's board of trustees on fund-raising and student and alumni relations.
