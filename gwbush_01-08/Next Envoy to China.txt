
President Bush is expected to name Clark Randt Jr., a lawyer and businessman based in Hong Kong, as the next American ambassador to China. 
Mr.
Randt, a classmate of Mr. Bush at Yale, would succeed Joseph W. Prueher, a retired Navy admiral, who was named to the post by President Bill Clinton in 1999
Admiral Prueher played a leading role in talks that led to the release of the American crew of a surveillance plane that collided with a Chinese fighter jet on April 1 and landed in southern China.
The collision is the focus of talks opening Wednesday in Beijing, but Admiral Prueher was not named to the delegation.
