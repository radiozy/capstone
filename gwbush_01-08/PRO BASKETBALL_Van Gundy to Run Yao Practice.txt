
Jeff Van Gundy has been chosen to conduct Yao Ming's official workout for N.B.A. teams tomorrow in Chicago. 
Yao, a 7-foot-5 Chinese center who is expected to be among the top three players selected in the June draft, flew into Chicago last night.
His practice will be seen by at least 22 teams.
Van Gundy, who stepped down as Knicks coach after 19 games this season, was asked to run the workout by Stu Jackson, the league's senior vice president of operations and a longtime friend of Van Gundy.
Yao has not performed in front of an American audience but his skills and talent were evident at the 2000 Olympic Games in Sydney, Australia.
Many details need to be worked out before he competes in the N.B.A., including financial arrangements with the Chinese government. 
But the potential of having a bona fide big man in a league with few elite centers has general managers salivating at the chance to draft Yao.
