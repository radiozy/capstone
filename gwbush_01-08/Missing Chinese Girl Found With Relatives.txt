
A 12-year-old Chinese girl missing for two days was found today with relatives on the East Coast after disappearing from her youth tour group, the authorities said.
Yukun Jia is "safe with relatives, and in good spirits," said Bronwyn Hogan, a spokeswoman for the San Mateo County sheriff's office. 
Ms.
Hogan would not name the city where Jia is staying to protect the girl's privacy.
Ms. Hogan said Jia's relatives met her at San Francisco International Airport on Thursday and they flew east together. 
Adult leaders of the youth group had told investigators Jia had signed a document saying she had no relatives in the United States.
Ms. Hogan said she was not sure why Jia abruptly disappeared from her fellow travelers or whether the girl hoped to defect from China, an angle investigators had pursued.
