
Li Jiajun and Wang Chunlu of China won the men's and women's 500-meter finals yesterday at the world short-track speedskating championships in Chonju, South Korea.
Li won the men's gold in 43.433 seconds, followed by Canada's Jonathan Guilmette in 43.465. 
In the women's competition, Wang won in 45.779, followed by her compatriot Yang Yang in 45.872.
