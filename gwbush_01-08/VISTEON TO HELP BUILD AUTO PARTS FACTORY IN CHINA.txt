
The Visteon Corporation, a maker of auto parts, plans to invest $80 million in a new joint venture factory in China to make vehicle heating and air-conditioning components and add business in Asia. 
The plant, to be built in Dalian, will employ about 600 people by next year, said Bob Pallash, vice president of Visteon's Asia-Pacific operations, in a conference call with reporters. 
The factory will be 60 percent owned by the Halla Climate Control Corporation, a South Korean company controlled by Visteon, he said. 
Visteon, a former unit of Ford, is based in Van Buren Township, Mich.
