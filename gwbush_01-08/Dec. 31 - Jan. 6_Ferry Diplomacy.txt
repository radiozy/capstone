
Taiwan tried mollifying China by letting a couple of boatloads of people, liquor and peanut candy sail across the few miles of choppy water that separate the tiny islands of Quemoy and Matsu from the Chinese mainland. 
The gesture was a far cry from the artillery shells the two sides traded over the islands in the 1950's. 
But China, which wants the main island of Taiwan to open its ports to the mainland, merely sniffed.
Taiwan is worried that fuller direct links could eventually subjugate its economy to the mainland's and undercut Taipei's independence. 
Beijing has already made the next move in this decades-old chess game: it has invited a delegation of Taiwan's opposition lawmakers to discuss broader trade and travel links.
