
The Wm. 
Wrigley Jr. 
Company said yesterday that it was buying several confectionary businesses from the Spanish food conglomerate Agrolimen for $272 million in cash, adding products like Boomer bubble gum, Pim Pom lollipops and Solano candy to its overseas brands.
Wrigley is buying Agrolimen's Joyco confectionery businesses in China, France, India, Italy, Poland and Spain, along with Cafosa, a supplier of bases for chewing gum. 
Wrigley executives said the deal added strong bubble gum brands, provided more diversification into both chewy and hard candies and further expanded Wrigley's offerings in Europe, China and India.
