
The 68,000-square-foot C. V. Starr East Asian Library at the University of California, Berkeley, opened to the public on Monday. 
The library, designed by the New York-based architects Tod Williams and Billie Tsien, holds more than 900,000 volumes, as well as manuscripts, bronze objects and the most comprehensive collection of historic Japanese maps outside of Japan. 
The building also incorporates the Center for Chinese Studies Library, the largest academic repository in the United States of materials on the People's Republic of China.
