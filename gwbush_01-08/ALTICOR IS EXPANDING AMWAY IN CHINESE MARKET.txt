
The Amway Corporation's parent company is expanding its presence in China, its No.1 market. 
The chairman of Alticor, Steve Van Andel, has signed a letter of intent to lease a research building in the Zhangjiang High Technology Park, southeast of Shanghai.
Alticor, based in Ada, Mich., expects to hire at least 20 Chinese scientists and invest at least $1 million in infrastructure and technology for the center. 
The center will develop health, skin care and other products tailored to the company's growing Asian market, a spokeswoman said yesterday. 
Amway is also adding 60 retail shops to the 120 it already has in China. 
It is the only market with Amway retail stores, which became mandatory after the government outlawed direct sale businesses in 1998.
