
Ichiro Suzuki is getting some help from his Japanese teammates.
Hitoshi Tamura hit a three-run homer Saturday and Tsuyoshi Nishioka drove in two runs as Japan advanced to the second round of the inaugural World Baseball Classic with a 14-3 victory over Taiwan.
Suzuki had a hit and scored a run for Japan (2-0).
Tamura, who had 31 home runs last season, connected against Taiwan's starter, Hsu Chu-chien, to give Japan a 3-0 lead.
Japan's victory came before a crowd of 31,047 at the Tokyo Dome. 
South Korea defeated China, 10-1, earlier Saturday to advance, improving to 2-0 in the 16-team tournament.
Taiwan and China are 0-2 and will face each other Sunday. 
Then Japan will play South Korea, closing out their first-round play.
Lee Seung Yeop hit a two-run homer in the third inning and a bases-empty home run in the eighth to lead South Korea.
