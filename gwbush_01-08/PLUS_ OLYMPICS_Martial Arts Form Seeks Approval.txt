
The traditional Chinese martial arts discipline of Wushu has applied to become an Olympic sport.
The president of the sport's governing body wrote to International Olympic Committee president Jacques Rogge, asking that Wushu be added to the 2008 Summer Games in Beijing, China's official Xinhua news agency reported Friday.
The addition of Wushu would contribute to the variety of the world culture and help spread the Olympic movement, International Wushu Federation president Li Zhijian told Xinhua.
If the I.O.C. turns down Li's request, Wushu could be included as part of a cultural program at the 2008 Games.
