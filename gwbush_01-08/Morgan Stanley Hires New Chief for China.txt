
Morgan Stanley, the investment bank, said yesterday that Wei Christianson would become its new chief executive for China.
The firm said Ms. Christianson, would be responsible for oversight and global coordination of all Morgan Stanley's business activities in China, among the world's fastest growing markets and a region of intense focus for Wall Street firms.
She had been chairwoman for China at Citigroup Global Markets (Asia), a division of Citigroup. 
She is based in Beijing.
Ms. Christianson, who is expected to begin work in May, will oversee the firm's investment banking, research, fixed income and foreign exchange, investment management and private-equity activities. 
She will be a managing director and a member of Morgan Stanley's Asia Pacific Executive Committee.
The move has been anticipated since Morgan Stanley's former head in China, Jonathan Zhu, left to join Bain Capital, the private-equity firm.
The change reunites Ms. Christianson with the chief executive of Morgan Stanley, John J. Mack; she was Morgan Stanley's executive director and the firm's chief Beijing representative from 1998 to 2002 when Mr. Mack was president.
