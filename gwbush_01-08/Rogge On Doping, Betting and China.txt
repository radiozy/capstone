
In a year-end message posted on the International Olympic Committee's Web site, Jacques Rogge, the organization's president, said he wanted tougher action in 2008 against doping and illegal sports betting. 
Rogge also said that the focus on political and social issues in China would probably increase leading to the Beijing Olympics, with the Games acting as a ''powerful catalyst.''Rogge said he took issue with the notion that sports was losing the fight against drugs because of the many high-profile doping cases in 2007. 
''The fact that so many instances of unfair play were revealed, and that more athletes admitted responsibility, tells us that we are on the right track,'' he said.Rogge also cited illegal betting as a threat to the credibility of sports. 
''It must be fought in the same aggressive way in which we are fighting against doping,'' he said.
