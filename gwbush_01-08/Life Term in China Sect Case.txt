
A Chinese court has sentenced a man to life in prison for sabotaging railway tracks after a city mayor ignored his demands to free adherents of the outlawed Falun Gong spiritual movement, the New China News Agency reported today.
It said Dou Zhenyang was sentenced on Sunday in the northeastern city of Fushan along with Wang Hongjun, who was sentenced to 13 years, for twice putting blocks on the rail line to Beijing and halting traffic.
The official news agency did not say whether or not the two men pleaded guilty, but said a police investigation showed that the sabotage followed a phone call the pair made to the Fushan mayor's hot line.
The two demanded the release of members of Falun Gong, which was outlawed and declared an evil cult in 1999, but they were ignored, the agency said. 
Those held were detained for attending illegal gatherings.
