
Asiarustic, selling Chinese country furniture, has opened in Harlem. 
The owner, Ana Wong-McNuer, was raised in Peru but makes collecting trips to China, which her parents left in the 1930's. 
The cash-box chair (left) is $1,100; bamboo or wood chests are $1,400 to $1,900; side tables and coffee tables, $1,300 to $2,500. 
From Sept. 
10 to 15, the gallery will give 20 percent of its revenues to help restore the Colonial kitchen in the Morris-Jumel Mansion, a 1765 house and museum in Harlem. 
Asiarustic is at 1028 St. Nicholas Avenue (162nd Street), (212) 795-4403 and www.asiarustic.com.
