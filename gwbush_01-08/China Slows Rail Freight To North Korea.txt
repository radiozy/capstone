
China has reduced freight traffic to North Korea in recent weeks, holding up some shipments of food aid, the World Food Program and rail authorities said. 
The move is apparently in anger over Chinese trains missing in North Korea, where they are sometimes disassembled and sold as scrap metal. 
''A lot of Chinese rail cars have piled up in North Korea and have not come back,'' said an official of China's Railways Bureau in Dandong, through which most of the freight passes. 
''So on this side, we reduced the number of cars going to North Korea.''
