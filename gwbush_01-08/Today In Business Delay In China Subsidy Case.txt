
The Commerce Department said it had delayed a decision in a landmark trade case that could lead to a surge of anti-subsidy complaints against China.
The case involves coated paper imports, but has implications for other industries. 
A preliminary ruling originally expected next week has been delayed until April 2, the Commerce Department spokeswoman said. 
At issue is an agency policy of not imposing countervailing duties on imports from non-market economies like China to offset government subsidies.
