
The eight-team Concacaf Women's Gold Cup tournament, to be played Oct. 
27-Nov. 9, will serve as the qualifying competition for the 2003 Women's World Cup, which will be played in China.
Doubleheaders are scheduled to be played at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena, Calif.; Safeco Field in Seattle; Titan Stadium in Fullerton, Calif.; and Centennial Stadium in Victoria, British Columbia.
The defending champion United States will play its first-round games in California and Seattle.
The eight teams will be divided into two groups for round-robin play. 
The top two finishers in each group will advance to the semifinals. 
The semifinal winners will advance to the tournament in China. 
The winner of the third-place match will have to play a home-and-away series against an Asian team for a spot in the Women's World Cup.
