
A 46-game winning streak for the United States men's basketball team at the University Games ended yesterday when Yao Ming -- one of three 7-footers who make up China's front line -- blocked Melvin Ely's put-back attempt to preserve an 83-82 victory in Beijing.
The United States had won a record six straight gold medals at these sort of mini-Olympics for college students, with players like Tim Duncan and Allen Iverson contributing to the streak.
China jumped to an 11-0 lead yesterday as the Americans missed their first seven shots, and was ahead, 81-70, with about three and a half minutes left before the United States came back, led by Juan Dixon, a member of Maryland's Final Four team last season. 
He hit three of four 3-pointers down the stretch and finished with 26 points.
