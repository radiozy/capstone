
The United States beat Australia, 13-7, in men's water polo to earn a bye into the quarterfinals at the world swimming championships in Barcelona, Spain.
Russia and China added to their medal totals on the sixth day of competition.
Anastasiya Davydova and Anastasiya Ermakova of Russia won the synchronized swimming duet with 99.084 points.
Guo Jingjing of China won the women's 3-meter springboard with 617.94 points to beat Yuliya Pakhalina of Russia (611.58) and Wu Minxia of China (589.80).
Russia leads the standing in gold medals, with five, and overall medals (10). 
China has three gold medals and nine overall medals.
