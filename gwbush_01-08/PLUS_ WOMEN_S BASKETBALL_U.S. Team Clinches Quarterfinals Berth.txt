
Lisa Leslie had 16 points and 10 rebounds as the United States beat South Korea, 91-53, and clinched a quarterfinal berth in the women's world basketball championship yesterday in Changzhou, China.
Tamika Catchings and Sheryl Swoopes each had 17 points.
The United States held a 28-15 lead after one quarter and continued to extend its lead.
The victory was the 15th in a row in world championship play for the United States, the defending champion. 
It is guaranteed a spot in Monday's quarterfinals regardless of what the team does today against France, the European champion.
Also yesterday, Penny Taylor scored 29 points as Australia clinched a spot in the quarterfinals with a 101-72 victory over China. 
Russia advanced by beating France, 74-59. 
Lithuania beat Cuba, 63-60. 
Spain upset Brazil, 78-68, and Yugoslavia beat Argentina, 83-76.
