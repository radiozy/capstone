
Scott Kazmir was pleased with his performance yesterday in the Devil Rays' 11-8 loss to the Philadelphia Phillies in St. Petersburg, Fla.
Kazmir gave up three runs and five hits in four innings with two strikeouts and a walk. 
In two previous starts, he allowed five earned runs in four and a third innings.
''It felt great today, it really did,'' Kazmir said. 
''I threw the fastball well and where I wanted to.''
Kazmir was 10-8 with a 3.24 earned run average last season before an inflamed shoulder sidelined him.CHINESE TEAM IN U.S. 
-- China's national baseball team is in the United States learning the finer points of the game from Major League Baseball representatives as it prepares for the Beijing Olympics, officials said yesterday.
A Chinese delegation of 37 players, coaches and officials is training at Scottsdale (Ariz.) Community College under the guidance of Jim Lefebvre, a former major leaguer. 
China will enter the Olympic baseball competition for the first time next year. 
(REUTERS)TV DEAL UNDER SCRUTINY -- Major League Baseball's $700 million contract with DirecTV will be reviewed by the Senate Commerce Committee on March 27 over concerns that it may limit consumer access to hundreds of games. 
The panel will meet to review a deal announced last week that puts baseball's Extra Innings package of out-of-town games on DirecTV, said a spokesman for Senator John Kerry, Democrat of Massachusetts, the chairman of the hearing.
