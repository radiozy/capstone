
Meb Keflezighi, the American who won the silver medal in the men's marathon in last year's Athens Olympics, and Liu Xiang of China, who won the men's 110-meter hurdles in Athens, were entered yesterday in track and field competitions in New York.
In his first race of the year, Keflezighi, 30, will take part in the Healthy Kidney 10-kilometer run Sunday in Central Park.
His opponents will include Hendrik Ramaala of South Africa, who won the New York City Marathon last November (Keflezighi finished second); Craig Mottram of Australia; and Patrick Ivuti of Kenya.
Liu will run June 11 in the Reebok Grand Prix, the first international competition in the new Icahn Stadium on Randall's Island. 
Liu, 21, is the first man from China to win an Olympic gold medal in track and field. he Forbes list of Chinese celebrities promoted him from 90th last year to third this year.
