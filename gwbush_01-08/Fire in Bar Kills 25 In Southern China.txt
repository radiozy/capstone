
Fire swept through a bar in the southern Chinese city of Zhongshan on Sunday, and the official New China News Agency said 25 people were killed and 11 injured.
Hong Kong's Cable TV quoted witnesses as saying there was an explosion before the fire and that many of the injured suffered severe burns. 
Footage showed police cordoning off the building as workers cleared debris. 
Zhongshan is in Guangdong province, about 40 miles south of the provincial capital of Guangzhou.
