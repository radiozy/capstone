
China beat Paris, Toronto, Osaka and Istanbul to win the right to host the 2008 Summer Olympic Games, in voting by the 119-member International Olympic Committee in Moscow.
It will be China's first chance to host the games, and the country has promised to spend billions to lower pollution and build new roads and stadiums in Beijing. 
Many people hope that the international focus the games bring will pressure China's government to improve its human rights record. 
Others fear that Beijing will use the games to validate its often repressive governing style.
