
Chinese demand for cheap animal feed helped fuel the best week of grain sorghum sales for exporters in the United States in nearly two decades. 
The Agriculture Department said exporters sold 584,324 metric tons of American sorghum in the third week of December, the largest weekly sale since 1995 and the second largest total since 1990. 
Sorghum has long been used as substitute feed by budget-conscious livestock and poultry producers, and, in China, to produce alcohol and other foods.
