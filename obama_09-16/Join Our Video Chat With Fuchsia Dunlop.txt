
Fuchsia Dunlop is a British journalist who enrolled in cooking school in Sichuan almost as a lark -- and since then has become a brilliant translator of traditional Chinese food for Western audiences, especially in her books about the regions of Sichuan and Hunan.
Her most recent cookbook, ''Every Grain of Rice,'' is about everyday home cooking in modern China, the kind of dishes she is convinced the rest of us can pull off without a lot of time or effort. 
The recipes are easy and practical, and even healthy, unlike most Chinese restaurant food in the United States.
