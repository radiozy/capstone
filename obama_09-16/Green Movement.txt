
Asia takes eco-resort to the next level.
SONG SAA PRIVATE ISLAND, CAMBODIA
Opening in February, this 27-villa resort on two remote islands connected by a wooden bridge should help cement Cambodia's reputation as one of Asia's eco hot spots. 
The place was built using only sustainable building materials /- pieces of driftwood serve as columns - and the owners established a marine reserve around the islands. 
You can take a seaplane from Phnom Penh, but the truly earth-conscious will take a boat from Sihanoukville. songsaa.com; $798 per person, all-inclusive.
SIX SENSES CON DAO, VIETNAM.
The Thai-based Six Senses has a reputation for carbon-zero luxury. 
At this new property, on a pristine archipelago in southeast Vietnam, 50 villas incorporate more than a thousand teak carvings and have butterfly-style roofs designed to minimize the use of air-conditioning. 
Infinity pools offer unobstructed views of the sea, itself a protected marine park in which guests can kayak, dive and turtle watch. sixsenses.com/sixsensescondao; doubles from about $663.
NAKED STABLES PRIVATE RESERVE, CHINA.
This resort in the Moganshan Valley, about two hours from Shanghai, has sleek treehouses and earthy thatched huts that use closed-loop water and heating systems. 
Guests go back to nature with fishing, tea picking, horseback riding and hiking through nearby bamboo forests. 
The car-free property - on track to become China's first platinum LEED-certified hotel - will add an organic farm next year. nakedretreats.cn; doubles from about $410.
