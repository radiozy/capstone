
MOSCOW — The Soviet government today [Oct. 
7] ordered all students from Communist China to leave the country before Nov. 
1. It did so in retaliation against the recent ouster of Soviet students from China. 
The Soviet government expressed "regret" that China had unilaterally broken a ten-year cultural exchange agreement. 
It told the Chinese embassy it would consider resuming the student exchanges "as soon as the Chinese side displays a readiness" to do so. 
Communist China last month suspended for a year training of all foreign students in China.
The reason given to embassies in Peking was that Chinese professors and teachers were too busy now with tasks of "the great proletarian cultural revolution." The Chinese embassy here said that 65 Chinese have been studying this school year at Soviet universities and institutions, 20 of them in Moscow. 
— New York Herald Tribune, European Edition, Oct. 
8, 1966
