
John Christopher Balzano and Dr. Anthony Frank Porto were married Friday evening at the Saybrook Point Inn in Old Saybrook, Conn. 
Officiating was Judge Joette Katz, a former associate justice of the Supreme Court of Connecticut, for whom Mr. Balzano was a clerk from 2007 to 2008.
Mr. Balzano (left), 32, is a lecturer at Yale Law School and a senior fellow of the China Law Center of Yale, which focuses on legal reform in China. 
He graduated from Columbia and received a master's degree in East Asian studies. 
He also holds a law degree from Washington University in St. Louis.
Mr. Balzano is the son of Lisa M. Balzano and John J. Balzano of North Caldwell, N.J. 
His mother is the coordinator of the Jeffrey Dworkin Early Intervention program at Montclair State University in Montclair, N.J., where she coordinates services for special-needs children. 
His father retired as a director of finance for Mars, the candy and food company. 
He worked in Hackettstown, N.J.
Dr. Porto, 36, is an assistant professor of pediatrics at the Yale School of Medicine, a pediatric gastroenterologist at the Yale Medical Group and the director of pediatric gastroenterology at Greenwich Hospital in Connecticut. 
He graduated from Columbia and received a master's in public health and a medical degree from Tufts.
Dr. Porto is the son of Louis F. Porto of Brooklyn and the late Diana M. Porto. His father, who is retired, was a telecommunications manager at Verizon in Manhattan.
