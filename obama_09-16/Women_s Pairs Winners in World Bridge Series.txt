
The winners of the Women's Pairs at the 14th Red Bull World Bridge Series in China last weekend were Liu Shu and Zhou Tao of China. 
They finished well ahead of Gan Lin and Huang Yan, also of China, with Suci Amita Dewi and Kristina Wahyu Murniati of Indonesia third.
The leading Americans were Lynn Deas and Disa Eythorsdottir, who finished eighth. 
(Deas won this title in 2010 in Philadelphia with Beth Palmer.)
The winners are not well known outside China. 
Their only international title was claimed by Liu, who won the Third World University Team Cup in 2006. 
However, they bid boldly and played the cards well, as seen in the diagramed deal from the eighth and final session.
After South opened one spade, Liu (West) made a three-heart weak jump overcall. 
Her hand was atypically strong, but the vulnerability was unfavorable. 
Zhou (East), though, immediately wondered if her opponents could make six spades. 
So, over North's raise to three spades, East made a very aggressive jump to five hearts. 
If South had doubled, the penalty would have been 500, more than her side's game, but she went on to five spades.
West led her singleton club. 
When declarer called for dummy's ten, East played a suit-preference nine.
South then led dummy's spade queen, but when East played low, declarer won with her ace and continued with the spade jack.
West took the trick, and East pitched the heart five, using odd-even discards. 
(An odd card is encouraging in that suit. 
An even card is discouraging and carries suit-preference undertones. 
A low even card suggests the lower-ranking of the other two side suits; a high even card, the higher-ranking side suit.)
At Trick 4, West shifted to her heart six. 
East won the trick and gave her partner a club ruff for down one and a cold top en route to the gold medals.
