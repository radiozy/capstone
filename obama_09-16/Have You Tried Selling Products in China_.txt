
We've just published a conversation with Frank Lavin, who introduced an e-commerce platform two years ago, Export Now, that was conceived as an easy, low-cost way for American small businesses to sell their goods in China. 
The basic pitch: "Sell into China without leaving your desk."
In the interview, Mr. Lavin, a former under secretary for international trade at the United States Commerce Department, concedes that things have not gone precisely as planned. 
In fact, he said, he has had a tough time convincing small businesses to try selling in China: "I told our sales team, 'It's like we've got the best mechanism for kidney transplants, but nobody wants a kidney transplant.'"
Mr. Lavin goes on to explain why his company has had greater success with large organizations, including the National Football League.
