
35 billion: The amount in dollars that Chinese officials misused or embezzled in the first 11 months of 2009, according to a national audit released last week.
The announcement was the latest indication of how widespread corruption has become among government agencies and how difficult it will be for Beijing to root it out. 
The audit of nearly 100,000 government departments and state-owned companies found officials engaged in laundering money, issuing fraudulent loans and cheating the government through the sale or purchase of state property. 
More than 1,000 officials were facing prosecution or disciplinary action as a result of the audits, the government's National Audit Office said.
