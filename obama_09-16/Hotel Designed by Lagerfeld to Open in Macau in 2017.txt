
BEIJING -- Karl Lagerfeld and a Macau casino operator announced plans Friday to open a hotel under the Chanel designer's name.
The 270-room Karl Lagerfeld Hotel in Macau is expected to open in 2017 in a 20-story tower, according to Mr. Lagerfeld and to the casino operator, Sociedade de Jogos de Macau, or S.J.M. 
They did not provide financial details.
''An entire hotel designed by me,'' Mr. Lagerfeld said in a statement. 
''I think the idea is great!''
Mr. Lagerfeld is the chief designer for the Chanel and Fendi fashion houses and also has his own label. 
The Macau hotel is being developed under the brand Karl Lagerfeld Greater China.
Mr. Lagerfeld drew attention during the past week by presenting his new Chanel collection in Paris in a full-scale replica of a supermarket with Chanel-branded groceries.
The Lagerfeld hotel will be part of the Lisboa Palace complex, which S.J.M. said last year would also include a Versace-themed hotel designed by the Italian fashion label. 
The complex, in the Cotai district of Macau, will have casinos, restaurants and shops and more than 2,000 hotel rooms, S.J.M has said.
Macau, the only place in China where gambling is legal, had about $45 billion in gambling revenue in 2013, about seven times the revenue of the Las Vegas Strip. 
Competition has intensified as S.J.M. rivals including Wynn Resorts and MGM Resorts International have opened new casinos.
S.J.M. was founded by Stanley Ho, who held a monopoly on gambling in Macau until 2004. 
Mr. Ho transferred most of his 32 percent stake in S.J.M.'s parent company to his relatives in 2011.
