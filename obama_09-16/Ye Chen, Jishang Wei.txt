
Ye Grace Chen and Jishang Wei were married Saturday at the Stanford University Church in Stanford, Calif. 
The Rev. Scotty McLennan, the dean for religious life at Stanford and a Unitarian Universalist minister, performed the ceremony.
The bride, 29, is a postdoctoral researcher in the dermatology department at Stanford's medical school. 
She graduated from the University of California, Berkeley, and received a Ph.D. in chemical biology from Harvard.
She is a daughter if Minghua Zhang and Jianguo Chen of Davis, Calif. 
The bride's father is a botanist at the University of California, Davis.
The groom, 31, is a research scientist at Hewlett-Packard Labs in Palo Alto, Calif. 
He graduated from Xian Jiaotong University in Xian, China, from which he also received a master's degree in electrical engineering. 
He has a Ph.D. in computer science from the University of California, Davis.
He is the son of Guihua Qin and Zhaosheng Wei of Longkou, China. 
The groom's father is a botanist for the town of Longkou.
